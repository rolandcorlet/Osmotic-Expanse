# APPRAISAL OF THE SOCIETY DRAFTS
## *The Osmotic Expanse* — a cross-check of two attached drafts against QUORUM, the Codex and the story

Both drafts are well written and mostly faithful to *What the Water Owed*. Neither was checked against QUORUM or the Director's Codex, so each repeats some factual errors and misses most of the printed society rules. Their best ideas are now in *Society Rules and Social Norms*. The errors below are worth fixing before either draft is shared.

---

## 1 · Society Rules and Social Norms (draft, about 4,500 words)

**Verdict:** strong framing, thin canon. Its rule-versus-norm idea and its table of manners are the best parts, and both have been folded into the compiled *Society Rules and Social Norms*. This draft can be retired, because the compiled document now covers everything in it.

### What works

* The distinction between a written **rule** and an unwritten **norm**, and the point that norms are stronger in a world where everyone can smell you.
* A polite-and-rude table drawn faithfully from the story: speaking to the person the news belongs to, giving hard news plainly, the morning Rite, refusing a plasmid.
* Good observations: slowness is not cowardice; the Yield leaves no room for honour-killing; the law is strict about the poor and silent about the powerful.

### Errors and fixes

| The draft says | The problem | Correct version |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| "The Sacred Code (the big four)… the story names three" | The Code has **seven** Precepts | Codex §8.2 |
| "The Pump — bosses the work crew" | The Pump *is* the work crew, an organ, not an office | QUORUM §2.1, §3.1 |
| "Muster — militia and census" | No book gives the Muster a census duty | QUORUM §3.1: the Muster calls the militia |
| The Rendering happens "twice a year" | Once per Tide, lasting 2 Beats | QUORUM §12.1 |
| Masks are legal "for a fortnight" | The Swell is one Pulse Ten, 10 Beats | QUORUM §12.1 |
| Masks are bought at the Fair "two seasons early" | The Swell comes three seasons after the Rise | Codex §5.3 |
| "Ten generations pay interest" | The story says the debt is two Great Cycles old | the story, Part I |
| Epithalians are "the rich salt-holders" | Dry is a condition any lineage can hold; Corvane happens to be Epithalian | QUORUM Ch. 5 |
| "A guest may not be censored, even in a war" | It is *funerals* that may not be censored (Precept 2) | Codex §8.2; QUORUM §11.1 |
| Skipping the morning gathering makes you "smell wrong for the day"; "never sign anything you cannot read yourself"; stealing water is "capital-level only if witnessed" | Not in the story or the books | remove, or mark as new suggestions |
| Quick-check answer 4 | Does not answer the question asked (why a rich man can win legally and still lose) | rewrite the answer |
| Numbered lists in Part C | Numbering runs on across sections (C3 starts at 4, C5 at 8, C6 at 14), and C4 is missing | renumber |

### Gaps

The draft has nothing on the six organs and the offices' voices, the four supports of a condition, Ledger capacity by condition, the twelve mutualisms, the printed crimes and sentences, the festival rules, Host-City tenancy, or the lineage quarters.

## 2 · What the Water Owed — Kid-Friendly Breakdown (draft, about 8,800 words)

**Verdict:** a very good young-reader companion that needs a correction pass. Keep it.

### What works

* The voice is exactly right for a 9-year-old: "a water balloon with thoughts", "kindness has a receipt".
* **Part 2, "The Individual"** (Rules A–J), is original and strong. *Play your catch, don't fix it* and *witnessing is the real magic* are excellent.
* **Part 6**, the table of what the world says against what a person can do about it, is the best single page across both drafts.
* Part 7 lays out the plot cleanly in Tide order.

## 3 · Recommendation

1. **Use *Society Rules and Social Norms* as the canon reference** for society rules and norms.
2. **Keep the Kid-Friendly Breakdown** as the young-reader companion, after the fixes above.
3. **Retire the Society Rules draft**, since its content now lives in the compiled document.
4. **Decide the story-only rules** listed at the end of *Society Rules and Social Norms*, then add the chosen ones to QUORUM.
