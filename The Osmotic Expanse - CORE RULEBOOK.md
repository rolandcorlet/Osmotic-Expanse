# THE PRIME SERUM

There was no bang. There was a **drop**.

One drop of Prime Serum fell into a warm and mindless soup, and the soup learned to *keep*. Forty million Metabolic Beats later, the keeping has become civilisation: phospholipid continents two thousand Drifts wide, protein ranges carved by the Great Flows, industrial ribosome-foundries the size of weather, and a religion that insists the drop was deliberate.

You are not visitors. You are **organisms** — descendants of that first stubborn chemistry, each of you a walking argument for staying closed. Your world has no sun; the light is other people's metabolism. Your world has no ground; the ground is a membrane that only lets some things through. Your world has no afterlife; when you die, you are immediately, efficiently, *reused*.

That is the whole premise, and it is a mechanical premise:

* **Everything is a gradient.** Health is pressure. Magic is catalysis. Speech is chemistry. Travel is current. Combat is a race to destabilise someone else's containment before they destabilise yours.
* **Nothing is wasted.** Kill a thing and you harvest it. Harvest enough and you *become* it — graft its organ, learn its pathway, drift faster, dissolve harder. Loot is not treasure in the Expanse. Loot is **evolution you haven't spent yet.**
* **Everything leaks.** Dice that come up **1** are inhibitors: your own reaction poisoning itself. The engine punishes you in the currency it also pays you in — energy, structure, time.

Play it as a survival-crawl, a colonial intrigue, a hunt for the edge of the world, or all three. The rules only insist on one thing: **you are an enclosed volume of water that is trying very hard to stay that way.**

### What you do at a table

1. **State intent in the fiction.** "I plug the Breacher's pore-kanal with a peptide plug, then ride the current into the fissure."
2. **The Director sets an Activation Threshold** (or the target's Threshold is already printed on its block).
3. **You build a pool:** Baseline Grade + Pathway Rank + any modifiers. That's how many d6.
4. **Roll. Count 1s off. Compare.** Beat the Threshold and the excess (the **Overflow**) becomes part of the effect.
5. **The world answers back** — in Overflow, in inhibitors, in Stress, in ATP you now don't have.

### The four promises the engine makes

| Promise | Where it lives |
| :-- | :-- |
| A bigger pool both lands more often *and* lands harder — never roll twice for one attempt. | Ch. I |
| Your resources are energy, matter, and time. There are no other axes. | Ch. II |
| Combat is a fluid: you pay per action, act as often as your Diffusion allows, and the environment is a participant. | Ch. IV, VII |
| Death is a process with a countdown, and every death leaves a permanent mark on the organism and the colony. | Ch. V, XI |

# CHAPTER I — RESOLUTION: ACTIVATION THRESHOLDS

## 1.1 The one roll

To do anything uncertain, roll a **Reagent Pool** of d6.

```
POOL = Baseline Grade + Pathway Rank + situational modifiers
```

Read the dice:

* **1s are Inhibitors.** They score nothing and are removed from the total. Each one marks **1 Metabolic Stress** on your Stress track (§5.8).
* **Total** = the sum of the surviving dice.
* **Success** if Total ≥ the **Activation Threshold (AT)**.
* **Breach Overflow** = Total − AT. Overflow is never wasted: it always buys something (§1.4).
* **Runaway Catalysis** if the roll contains **no Inhibitors and at least two 6s**. The reaction feeds itself: you gain the effect's **Rider**, and any Effect Value is counted a second time.

> **Engine note.** These rates are exact, not approximations: a 3-dice pool Runaways 6.0% of the time, 4-dice 8.7%, 5-dice 10.6%, 6-dice 11.5%, 7-dice 11.8%, 8-dice 11.6%. Runaway Catalysis peaks around a 7-dice pool — beyond that, the risk of a self-poisoning 1 keeps the ceiling honest.

## 1.2 Threshold ladder

If there is no creature block to consult, use this ladder. It is calibrated so that a trained Grade-3 organism (pool 4) fails about a third of the time at 12.

| AT | Name | The Expanse says |
| --: | :-- | :-- |
| **8** | Common | Any organism with the pathway can do this under pressure. |
| **12** | Standard | A competent specialist does this more often than not. |
| **16** | Severe | Requires real mastery, good tools, or a plan. |
| **20** | Extreme | Hegemony engineering. Colony-wide effort or genius. |
| **24** | Nucleoid | The Sacred Code itself seems to lean in. |
| **28** | Apotheotic | One organism per generation, if that. |

**Difficulty is a fixed number, not a moving target.** Do not "secretly scale" ATs to the party. Instead, change the *fiction*: a different graft, a different current, a bigger hole.

## 1.3 Situational modifiers

Add or subtract **dice** (never raw totals). Modifiers stack to a maximum of **+5 / −5 dice**.

| Circumstance | Dice |
| :-- | :-- |
| Tool or apparatus made for the task (+1 apparatus bonus is already priced into gear) | +1 to +2 |
| Assisted by one willing trained ally (each ally, max 2) | +1 |
| Good position, prepared reagents, or a genuine advantage stated in fiction | +1 |
| Working in the wrong tonic (Ch. VII) | −1 to −2 |
| Injured: Stress 3 / 5 / 6 | −1 / −2 / −3 |
| Partial cover · heavy cover · total concealment in turbid fluid | −2 / −4 · automatic miss unless searched |
| Squeezed, pinned, or in a vortex | −2 |
| **Catalysed** (a Vial, ally Burst, or graft boosts this roll) | +1, and re-roll one 6 if it comes up 1 |

## 1.4 Overflow: spending the excess

Every successful roll produces Overflow. The rule is simply that the effect gets better by that amount, in units that already exist:

| Attempt | Overflow buys |
| :-- | :-- |
| Attack | Extra damage (§4.9) |
| Movement / pursuit | Extra Drifts, or you put a band of distance between you |
| Manipulation / crafting | Better quality (one grade per 4 Overflow), fewer Vials consumed, or a step less time |
| Social | A concession: a rumour, a discount, a promise, a pass |
| Stealth | Better concealment: −1 to be noticed per 2 Overflow, and you keep your last position |
| Repair | Extra MI restored, or a second hole sealed |

Never re-roll to "confirm" a bigger effect. Overflow *is* the confirmation.

## 1.5 Contested reactions

When two organisms strain against each other — a grapple across a current, a pheromone lie against a detector — **both roll their own pool.** The higher total succeeds; ties go to the defender. The winner's Overflow applies as normal (a wrestler with 6 Overflow slams the loser into the next band, not just down).

Contested reactions are **not** attack rolls. Attacks use the target's printed **Membrane Threshold** (§4.8).

## 1.6 Homeostatic Baseline

If the attempt is unopposed, unhurried, and the Director judges that a bare competence would eventually succeed, **do not roll.** You succeed if:

```
BASELINE = (pool dice) × 3
```

This is the average of your pool with inhibitors removed. A pool-4 specialist has Baseline 12 and therefore cannot fail a Standard task with time, but cannot clear Severe.

## 1.7 Group attempts

The organism with the best pool attempts; every other participating ally with the same pathway adds **+1** (to the +5 cap). If the group fails and the check carries risk, **everyone** marks Stress equal to the number of Inhibitors they personally rolled.

## 1.8 Buying dice: Overdrive and Inhibition

Two universal levers, always available, always paid in the metabolisms of Chapter II.

* **OVERDRIVE — pay energy for structure.** Spend **4 ATP** to add **+2 Reagents** to one pool, or spend **4 MI** to add **+4 Reagents**. You cannot Overdrive twice on the same roll. Overdriving with MI marks **1 Stress** regardless of the outcome — the body knows what it burned.
* **INHIBITION — spend someone else's certainty.** Before a hostile roll is resolved, spend **3 ATP and 1 reserved Pulse Point** to force the roller to **re-roll one die of your choice that came up 6**, or to subtract **2 Reagents**. You must be able to interrupt the fiction (you saw it, smelled it, or are holding a Reactive Osmosis).

## 1.9 Fumbles, complications, and the Director's third option

There is no such thing as "critical fumble." A failed roll means **the thing does not happen, or happens at a price.** Pick one and move on:

1. **Cost** — it works, but lose ATP, gain Stress, break a Vial, make noise.
2. **Turn** — it fails and the situation moves without you (a wave, a door seals, a scent marks you).
3. **Complication** — it works *for the wrong party* (your acid eats the bridge you needed; your signal reaches the wrong colony).

# CHAPTER II — THE THREE METABOLISMS

Three numbers define what an organism can afford: **energy (ATP)**, **matter (Substrate)**, **time-and-attention (Pulse Points, Ch. IV)**. A fourth, **Codon**, only changes what the organism *is*.

## 2.1 ATP — the energy economy

**ATP is spent to do things that are not free.** Bursts, Overdrive, sprinting against a current, forcing a graft, holding a Sustained Reaction.

| | |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Maximum ATP** | 8 + (RTC × 2), plus grafts and lineage gifts |
| **Regeneration** | +2 ATP at the end of each Pulse Cycle; **+1 more** if you spent no ATP in that Cycle |
| **Full recovery** | a **Reconstitution** (Ch. VIII) restores all ATP |
| **Zero ATP** | you enter **Depletion**: you cannot Begin a Burst, and every Drift beyond your free one costs 1 MI |

ATP is also the **living currency** of the Hegemony: pay a toll and you *metabolise* it. It cannot be stored in a purse beyond your own capacity — you can carry at most **your maximum ATP** in free-floating energy. Everything else must be paid in Substrate.

**Debt of fire.** At 0 ATP you may still act, but you are burning yourself. See §5.9 Autophagy.

## 2.2 Substrate — the matter economy

**Substrate (S)** is raw, generic biological material: glucose, amino chains, lipid beads, mineral dust. It is carried, traded, spent on crafting, burned for repair, and left inside a corpse when you kill it.

* **Repair:** 1 Substrate restores **1 MI**, outside combat only, and requires a Pathway (Somatic 1) or the *Substrate Patch* action.
* **Crafting:** recipes cost Substrate plus **Specimens** (§9.4).
* **Bulk:** every 20 S of loose goods is **1 Bulk**. Your carrying Bulk = **VSC × 5**; exceeding it costs −2 DFF and marks Stress whenever you must move quickly. A **Vacuole** graft adds +5 Bulk per rank.
* **Currency:** prices by rarity are in §9.2. A common organism carries its **Level × 5 S** and a pouch of Vials.

## 2.3 Codon — the evolution economy

**Codon is experience in the only honest sense: information about how to be alive.** You earn Codon for defeating threats, completing Vectors, and — uniquely — for *surviving consequences*: a Total Lysis you pulled someone out of, a colony you saved, a hazard you mapped.

| | |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Cost to Grow** | Level → Level + 1 costs **(20 × Level) + 20** Codon |
| **Foe yields** | Tier 0 = 1, T1 = 3, T2 = 7, T3 = 15, T4 = 30, T5 = 60; a Catastrophe is ×3 |
| **Objectives** | a cleared hazard, exposed conspiracy, or negotiated peace pays 25–200 Codon by tier |
| **Spending** | Codon is **never spent**. It accumulates, and your **Level** is where the total has carried you. Growth then lets you *spend* Substrate and Specimens on your body (§2.4). |

Cumulative Codon: Level 2 = 40 · 3 = 100 · 4 = 180 · **5 = 280** · **6 = 400** · 7 = 540 · 8 = 700 · 9 = 880 · **10 = 1080** · **11 = 1300** · 12 = 1540 · 13 = 1800 · 14 = 2080 · **15 = 2380** · **16 = 2700** · 17 = 3040 · 18 = 3400 · 19 = 3780 · **20 = 4180**.

A Cohort of four clears roughly two to four significant threats per Level, and a Chapter of the world is measured in tens of thousands of Codon. Codon is tracked on the sheet as a running total; when the total crosses a threshold, the organism **Grows** at the next Reconstitution.

## 2.4 Specimens — the loot that is also progression

Every entity in the Bestiary lists a **Harvest**: Specimens of four grades.

| Grade | Name | Used for |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| C | **Common Tissue** | Vials, basic grafts, Substrate conversion (1 Specimen = 5 S) |
| G | **Greater Organ** | Rare grafts, Apparatus tuning, Pathway unlock |
| P | **Pristine Sequence** | Trait purchase, Stage-transition features |
| T | **Terminal Codon** | Relic work, Apotheosis, resynthesis of the dead |

Harvesting takes a **Synthesise action** (or 10 minutes outside combat), costs 1 Bulk, and requires an **OSM or RTC check** at the entity's tier × 4 + 8. Failure ruins the sample: you get Substrate instead.

**A body is a bank.** Killing the *Membrane Shredder* does not just feed your colony; its Greater Organ is the raw material for the exact graft that will let your Kinetic Striker survive the next one. Every Bestiary entry is written backwards from this.

## 2.5 Cohesion — the group metabolism

Your Cohort keeps a shared track, **Cohesion (0–10)**, built by play: rescues, kept oaths, shared meals at a chemotactic hearth, successful rituals.

Spend 1 Cohesion to:
* pass one Reagent to another organism (they gain +1 die);
* refuse a Stress mark yourself;
* add +2 to a Frequency Check;
* activate a bonded Symbiote or oath-lock without ATP.

Cohesion falls when oaths are broken, when an organism goes dormant and is left, or when a chemical lie is exposed. At **Cohesion 0**, the Cohort's Baselines are all −1 until something is repaired. The Director's Codex tracks Colony-level Cohesion for settlements.

# CHAPTER III — BASELINES AND PATHWAYS

## 3.1 The five Metabolic Baselines

A Baseline is a **Grade from 1 to 6**: how many dice you bring when the world tests you. Grades are not "scores"; they are *reagent quantities*. A Grade-1 organism simply has less of the right chemistry in the room.

| Baseline | Grade 1 — starved | Grade 3 — typical | Grade 6 — Nucleoid-grade |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **VISCOSITY (VSC)** — mass, density, structural containment. | Thin-walled, leaks at a scratch. | Holds shape under ordinary pressure. | A walking pressure-vessel. Nothing moves you. |
| **DIFFUSION (DFF)** — speed, reactive agility, flow through medium. | Sinks, staggers, is late to everything. | Reads a current and rides it. | Appears to be in two places in the same Cycle. |
| **REACTIVITY (RTC)** — chemical intelligence, catalysis, synthesis. | Cannot bend a reaction it did not memorise. | Folds proteins by hand. | Turns ambient fluid into a weapon mid-thought. |
| **OSMOSIS (OSM)** — perception, chemoreception, intuition, resistance to intrusion. | Blind to scent, easy to lie to. | Reads a mood from a molecule. | Knows what is in the next chamber and what it ate. |
| **AFFINITY (AFF)** — signalling, command, symbiotic binding. | Its pheromones are noise. | Commands a colony's attention. | Speaks and the tissue of the world obeys. |

**What each Baseline is used for** (the only table you need to memorise):

| Baseline | Rolls for | Also sets |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| VSC | Brute force, shoving, resisting poison/pressure/crush, heavy Apparatus, enduring Lysis | Maximum **MI**, Bulk capacity, Lysis Countdown boxes |
| DFF | Balance, evasion, precise body control, light Apparatus, pursuit, stealth-in-motion | **Pulse Points**, **Membrane Threshold**, Frequency |
| RTC | Bursts, crafting, appraisal, chemistry, medicine, decoding sequences | Maximum **ATP**, Burst Effect Value |
| OSM | Perception, tracking by scent, insight, resisting deception and Genetic effects | Detection range, Harvest checks, Hazard notice |
| AFF | Command, negotiation, recruitment, symbiote handling, ritual | Bond strength, Cohesion gains, minion control |

A **Pathway** is a learned catalytic habit: the routes your body has worn smooth. Rank runs 0 to 4.

| Rank | Name | Meaning | Dice |
| --: | :-- | :-- | --: |
| 0 | **Latent** | You have never needed this. Untrained attempts use Baseline only. | — |
| 1 | **Expressed** | You do this as an adult does breathing. | +1 |
| 2 | **Anchored** | It is written into your chromatin; you do it better than most colonies. | +2 |
| 3 | **Dominant** | You are locally famous for it. | +3 |
| 4 | **Sovereign** | There is a ritual named after you. | +4 |

The **Core Pathway List** — the Atlas adds class-exclusive ones:

| Pathway | Usual Baseline | Governs |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Membrane Penetration** | VSC or DFF | Landing blows, breaching grafts, forcing seals |
| **Pressure Tolerance** | VSC | Hazards, crushing, hypertonic drift, Lysis Countdown |
| **Fluid Navigation** | DFF | Swimming, currents, balance, chase, evasion |
| **Concealment** | DFF + OSM | Masking, ambush, infiltration |

| **Signal Discourse** | AFF | Pheromological argument, command, ritual speech |
| **Symbiosis** | AFF | Bonds, minions, mounts, colony relations |
| **Vigour** | VSC | Endurance, hunger, sleep debt, sustained effort |
| **Pulse Discipline** | DFF | Initiative, timing, Interrupts, held reactions |

# CHAPTER IV — THE PULSE ECONOMY

Combat in the Expanse is not a duel of blows; it is a contest of **reaction rates** in a medium that is itself reacting. Nobody "waits their turn". Everyone is catalysing at once, and the Cycle exists only so the table can keep track.

## 4.1 Begin the Pulse War

1. **Detection Phase.** Both sides roll: `OSM + Pathway (Chemoreception)` vs the other side's `DFF + Pathway (Concealment)`, contested. Whoever wins the higher total by **4 or more** gains the **First Volley**: everyone on that side acts once before the other side is in the Cycle, spending PP as normal but with no Reactive Osmosis available. A tie means both sides are startled; skip a Cycle while everyone orients.
2. **Frequency Check.** Each organism rolls `d6 + DFF + Pathway (Pulse Discipline)` for the whole encounter. Highest acts first. Ties: more unspent PP, then higher OSM, then players.
3. **Set the theatre.** Place each side in **bands** (§4.4), and note every zone of fluid, current, and hazard on the field.

## 4.2 The Cycle

A **Pulse Cycle** is about six seconds of frantic chemistry. At the start of your turn in the Cycle you receive **Pulse Points**:

```
PP = 2 + DFF Grade + modifiers (lineage, grafts, conditions)
```

Typical: 3 at Stage I, 4–5 at Stage II, 5–6 at Stage III, 6–8 at Stage IV. Unspent PP do **not** carry between Cycles.

### The Action Matrix

| Cost | Action | What happens |
| --: | :-- | :-- |
| **1** | **Drift** | Move up to your **Flow** (3 + DFF Drifts; see §4.4). Also: stand, draw a graft, pass a Vial, break a hold with a contested VSC. |
| **1** | **Vent** | Reduce your Metabolic Stress by 2. Costs 2 ATP. |
| **1** | **Sense** | OSM + Chemoreception against the field's hidden things; you learn zones, ambushes, harvested remains. |
| **2** | **Strike** | One attack with an Apparatus or natural weapon (§4.8). You may make a second Strike in the same Cycle if you can pay 2 more PP. |
| **2** | **Synthesise** | In-combat crafting: apply a Vial, seal a pore, repair an ally's graft, set a trap (one Cycle to arm). |
| **3+** | **Burst** | Activate an Enzymatic Burst (§Ch. VI). Higher-tier Bursts cost 3, 4, or 5 PP and that much ATP. |
| **1 (held)** | **Reserve** | Hold one PP until another organism acts. Spent on **Reactive Osmosis** (§4.10) or **Inhibition** (§1.8). |
| — | **Free** | One short signal per Cycle (a phrase of chemical semiotics), one 5-foot-feeling step, dropping an item, speaking a Bonded word. |

**Split Frequency.** You may stop acting, keep your unspent PP, and re-enter the Cycle immediately after any later organism acts. Say when. This is how a fast organism lands three 2-PP Strikes across one Cycle instead of two in a row — and how a slow organism loses that argument.

## 4.3 Momentum (the velocity economy)

If you Drift at least **3 Drifts in a straight line** and then Strike in the same Cycle, you gain **+2 Reagents** and the Strike's type becomes **Kinetic**. If you began the Drift inside a Nutrient Current, the bonus is **+3**. Momentum is the reason a Turbulence Navigator carries a knife and a Current Rider carries nothing heavy.

## 4.4 Bands, not grids

Measure in **Drifts** (one Drift ≈ two metres of fluid). Organisms sit in bands relative to each other:

| Band | Drifts | Reach | Notes |
| :-- | --: | :-- | :-- |
| **Contact** | 0 | touch, lash, engulf | Grapples, Breachers, Guards live here. Leaving Contact while an enemy is present provokes a **Flank Breach** (§4.11). |
| **Near** | 1–3 | short lash, thrown spore | Most light Apparatus. |
| **Mid** | 4–9 | projector, spray, lunge | Common ranged band. |
| **Far** | 10–20 | arc, torrent, cascade | Requires line-of-sight through clear fluid; turbidity cuts it to Near. |
| **Loose** | 21+ | out of reach | Only area, current-borne, or scent-borne effects. |

Movement through a **zone** costs what the zone says (Ch. VII). A Current may carry you for free or throw you; a Gel Field costs 2 PP per Drift.

## 4.5 Size and reach

| Size class | Examples | Drift reach | MI modifier | MT modifier |
| :-- | :-- | --: | --: | --: |
| **Microscopic** | spores, cilia agents | 0 (Contact only) | −4 | +2 |
| **Minute** | most Stage I organisms | 1 | — | — |
| **Mesoscopic** | riders, guards, shredders | 2 | +6 | −1 |
| **Macroscopic** | titans, behemoths, maws | 3–5 | +20 | −3 |
| **Vast** | catastrophes, world-cores | 6+ | +60 | −5 |

## 4.6 The Detection Phase, in play

Detection is not a die rolled once by the Director behind a screen. Both sides roll, and **both sides learn something**: winners learn the enemy's bands and count, losers learn only that something is there. In the Expanse, everything smells. Ambush requires chemistry, not darkness.

## 4.7 Conditions

Conditions are written on the organism. Most end with a save, a Vent, or a Reactive Osmosis.

| Condition | Effect | Ends when |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Leaking** | Lose 1 MI at the start of your turn; your wake is trackable. | a Synthesise action or Somatic Burst seals the hole |
| **Turgid** | Swollen: −2 DFF, MT −2, but +5 temporary MI. | tonic normalises, or a 1-PP Vent |
| **Flaccid** | Shrunken: −2 VSC, reach −1 band. | end of next Cycle |
| **Jostled** | −1 PP; MT −2. | end of next Cycle |
| **Denatured** | −2 RTC; Burst Effect Values halve. | an RTC save at end of your turn |
| **Catabolic** | ATP costs +1 for everything; ATP regen halved. | a Reconstitution |
| **Locked** | Cannot Begin a Burst or use a class Burst feature. | a save each Cycle, or a Reactive Osmosis (1 PP) |
| **Sequestered** | Enclosed in gel, shell, or a vortex: cannot act. | an ally breaks the cage, or VSC save at −2 each Cycle |
| **Hijacked** | One enemy holds your Reserve: they may force your held PP to be spent on *their* action. | OSM save, or the hijacker is killed/revealed |
| **Masked** | Cannot be targeted by scent, OSM, or tracking until you Strike or are in a host's turbulence wake. | you Strike, or an enemy's successful Sense |
| **Marked** | All attackers gain +2 Reagents against you; you cannot gain Masked. | the marker is killed, or a 2-ATP chemical scrub |
| **Brittle** | Graft soak halves against Kinetic; Lytic damage against you ignores 2 soak. | a Synthesise action with a Lipid patch |
| **Overclocked** | +1 PP, but mark 2 Stress at the end of each Cycle. | you Vent, or 3 Cycles pass |
| **Encysted** | Take half damage, cannot act, cannot be moved. | you or an ally spends 3 ATP to crack the cyst |
| **Bloom** | Regenerate 2 MI per Cycle; every organism in Mid band or closer can sense you. | a Reconstitution, or a hostile scrub |

## 4.8 Attacks

**Build the pool:**

| Apparatus class | Baseline | Examples |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Weight** (heavy, mass-driven) | VSC | osmotic maul, chitin ram, bony flail |
| **Lash** (fast, body-driven) | DFF | flagellar whip, peptide blade, cilia saw |
| **Projector** (aimed chemistry) | DFF or RTC | spore pistol, acid duct, needle launcher |
| **Resonator** (catalytic, hands-free) | RTC | enzyme horn, phase bell, AE crucible |

Add **Pathway (Membrane Penetration)** for Weight/Lash/Projector, or **Pathway (Catalysis)** for Resonators, plus the Apparatus bonus (§Ch. IX).

**Against the target's Membrane Threshold:**

```
MT = 10 + DFF Grade + graft/size/cover/condition modifiers
```

A stationary object has MT 5 (or 8 if braced). A braced Guard in Testate armour has MT around 12 and enormous soak — being *hard to hit* and *hard to hurt* are different purchases, and organisms buy differently.

**Resolve:** Total ≥ MT → hit.

```
DAMAGE = Overflow + Effect Value + Apparatus BD − target's Soak
```

* **Soak** comes from grafts, terrain, and some Bursts. Soak is applied *once* to the whole hit, and a hit always deals at least **1** damage.
* **Type** the damage (Lytic, Kinetic, Osmotic, Thermal, Voltaic, Genetic) — see §4.9 and the Graft Matrix in Ch. IX.
* **Runaway Catalysis** doubles the Effect Value *before* soak and adds the apparatus's **Rider**.

## 4.9 Signature Consequences

Damage does damage. Each type *also* leans the world in one direction — this is where the flavour of the Expanse actually lives, and it is why armour choice is a strategy and not a number.

| Type | What it is | Signature Consequence on a hit (choose one; the attacker may choose the same one every time — consistency is a tactic) |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **KINETIC** | Mass, momentum, a thing hitting another thing | Push 1 Drift per 4 Overflow · break a graft slot · inflict **Jostled** |
| **LYTIC** | Enzymatic digestion of membranes and protein | Inflict **Brittle** · −1 soak permanently until repaired (max −3) · dissolve one Vial or consumable |
| **OSMOTIC** | Pressure and water flight | Inflict **Turgid** or **Flaccid** · knock into the next band · force a Fluid Navigation save or be swept by the nearest current |
| **THERMIC** | Exothermic and endothermic violence | Ignite the fluid: the target's zone becomes a Hazard for 1 Cycle · inflict **Catabolic** · cauterise: the target cannot be healed next Cycle |
| **VOLTAIC** | Signal and charge: nerve, gradient, pheromone | Inflict **Locked** or **Overclocked** · interrupt a Sustained Reaction · mark a target (**Marked**) |
| **GENETIC** | Sequence assault: viruses, hijack, rewrite | Inflict **Hijacked** · steal one Vial or one held Specimen · force a Sequence save or lose a class feature for 2 Cycles |

## 4.10 Reactive Osmosis

Holding **1 PP** buys you one of these, at any time in the Cycle, once per Cycle:

* **Deflect:** your MT against one incoming attack becomes **+4**.
* **Inhibit:** force an attacker within Contact or Near to re-roll one Reagent, or spend 3 ATP to subtract 2 dice (§1.8).
* **Brace:** halve Osmotic and Kinetic damage from one effect; ignore push.
* **Shed:** end one condition that says "ends when: a Reactive Osmosis" (Locked, Turgid, Marked).

A Reserve you never spend is lost at the end of the Cycle. Sentinels and Guards are built around never needing it.

## 4.11 Flank Breach

When you Drift out of an enemy's Contact band, they may spend a Reserve to make one free Strike at +1 Overflow. Turning your back in a fluid is a chemical statement, and fluids remember.

## 4.12 Sustained Reactions (concentration)

Bursts and graft effects marked **Sustained** persist while you hold them. Each Cycle at the start of your turn you must spend the listed ATP (or 1 PP if it is free) and mark **1 Stress**. Taking damage forces a `VSC + Pressure Tolerance` save at the effect's own AT; failure ends it and marks 2 more Stress. Two Sustained effects at once: −1 PP each Cycle.

## 4.13 Swarms as one body

Twenty cilia-creatures are not twenty turns. A **Swarmling pack** of up to 6 is one token with **Segments**. Rules in the Bestiary's Swarm chapter, in one line each:

* One MT, shared, but a hit removes **Segments** equal to ⌈Overflow ÷ 3⌉ + 1 instead of subtracting MI.
* It gets **1 Strike per 2 Segments remaining** (so a 6-segment pack can Strike 3 times, spending PP as it likes).
* Area effects are its doom: an area Burst removes Segments from *every* pack in the zone.

## 4.14 Ending a Pulse War

Combat ends when one side can no longer catalyse — which in the Expanse usually means one side is **harvesting** the other. Fleeing is a first-class action:

* Break to **Loose** band and spend a Cycle unobserved → you are gone; the pursuers must win a **Chase** (below) or give up.
* Yield: an organism may spend its whole Cycle signalling surrender (a chemical white flag it cannot fake). Killing a yielding organism marks 2 Stress on every witness and drops Cohesion 1.

### Chase (a 3-line subsystem)

Parallel Fluid Navigation contests, one per Cycle, at the terrain's AT. Loser shifts a band. Ahead of the party: escape at Loose + 2 bands; the party escapes at Contact (grapple) or after 6 Cycles in terrain the pursued knows better than the hunter (Director's call).

# CHAPTER V — BREACH, STRESS, AND THE LYSIS PROTOCOL

## 5.1 Membrane Integrity

**Membrane Integrity (MI)** is how much of you is still inside you.

```
MAX MI = 12 + (VSC × 4) + Hull + 2 × (Level − 1)
```

**Hull** is your Phenotype's structural tradition, bought at creation:

| Hull | Phenotypes | Why |
| --: | :-- | :-- |
| **3** | Corrosors, Scouts, Mimics, Messengers, Engineers | Something else is paying for your survival |
| **4** | Most Phenotypes | A body like anyone else's |
| **6** | Guards, Breachers, Brutes, Tanks, Weavers | You are the wall |

At Stage transitions (§Player's Atlas) MI jumps again, because a Stage transition *is* a body change.

## 5.2 Soak

**Soak** subtracts from damage after Overflow is counted. It is bought, never granted by a Baseline.

| Source | Soak |
| :-- | --: |
| Light graft (Lipid Weave, Fibrous wrap) | 1–2 |
| Medium graft (Testate plates, Mucoid mantle) | 3–4 |
| Heavy graft (Somatic bulwark, layered Testate) | 5–6 |
| Terrain (behind a Protein Bridge, in a Gel pocket) | 2–3 |
| Burst (Lattice, Shell, Refraction) | 2–5, temporary |
| Viscous fluid (the medium itself soaks Kinetic) | 1 |

A hit always deals at least **1 damage**, no matter how much soak. When a hit exceeds your remaining MI by **8 or more**, it becomes a **Breach**: you go straight to Dormancy and mark a Lysis Countdown box immediately (§5.5).

## 5.3 The Graft Matrix (summary — full version in Ch. IX)

| Graft family | Strong against | Weak against |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Lipid Weave** | Osmotic +2, Thermal +1 | Lytic −2 |
| **Fibrous Lattice** | Kinetic +2, Lytic +1 | Thermal −2 |
| **Testate Shell** | Lytic +2, Kinetic +1 | Osmotic −2, and −1 DFF |
| **Mucoid Gel** | Thermal +2, Voltaic +2, Osmotic +1 | Genetic −2 |
| **Somatic Graft** | all +1 (it lives, it adapts) | Genetic −2; feeds on 1 ATP/Cycle or the soak halves |

Choose armour the way a colony chooses a border policy: **what are you prepared to be wrong about?**

## 5.4 Dormancy

At **0 MI** you do not die. You go **Dormant**:

* You cannot act, cannot sense beyond Contact, and your Baselines count as 1 for saves.
* You are **Leaking**: at the start of each of your turns you lose **1 MI** and mark **1 Lysis Countdown box**.
* Any organism that reaches Contact can stabilise you with a **Synthesise action**: `RTC + Synthesis` at AT 12 + your Level, restoring MI equal to Overflow + 4 and closing one Countdown box.
* You cannot be healed above 0 while Dormant except by stabilisation.

## 5.5 The Lysis Countdown

```
COUNTDOWN BOXES = VSC + (Hull ÷ 2, rounded up) + 2
```

Typical: 7 at Stage I, 9–10 at Stage IV.

When the boxes fill, the organism suffers **Total Lysis** (§5.6). A Stabilised organism (back above 0 MI) stops counting but keeps the boxes marked until a **Dormant Cycle** (§5.10) clears them.

Countdown boxes also accumulate from other disasters: **Autophagy** (§5.9), certain hazards, Genetic hijacks, and Overdrive at 0 ATP. The Countdown is the game's most honest clock — it is why nobody in the Expanse picks a fight they cannot afford.

## 5.6 Total Lysis

Your containment fails. Your contents join the world.

1. **Your body is available for Harvest** at full value. Allies who harvest you are, mechanically, carrying a piece of you (see Legacy, Ch. XI).
2. **Roll on the Legacy Scarring table** (§5.7) even for the dead — the table is also read by whoever reconstructs you, and by the colony that inherits your plot.
3. **Return is possible but expensive.** A **Re-Synthesis** requires a Terminal Codon, 400 × your Level Substrate, a Sequence Reading contest at AT 16+, and 1d6 Metabolic Beats of downtime. The restored organism returns with one Pathway rank permanently reduced by 1 and 2 Countdown boxes pre-marked. The Director and player should discuss this *before* it is needed, not after.

## 5.7 Legacy Scarring (d10)

Roll whenever an organism survives Total Lysis, survives a Breach, or is Re-Synthesised. The scar is mechanical and permanent until removed by a Nucleoid-grade ritual.

| d10 | Scar | Effect |
| --: | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | **Weakened wall** | −1 VSC; MI −4 |
| 2 | **Leaky channel** | Always Leaking until a Somatic Burst closes it (1 ATP/Cycle upkeep) |
| 3 | **Signal static** | −2 AFF for command; your pheromones are legible to everyone |
| 4 | **Fused pathway** | One Pathway rank −1, permanently |
| 5 | **False memory** | You believe you are someone else's tissue: disadvantage on Sequence Reading about yourself |
| 6 | **Metabolic debt** | Max ATP −4; you must Vent at the start of every Pulse War or gain 1 Stress |
| 7 | **Reactive scar** | When hit for 8+, deal 3 Lytic damage to the nearest attacker |
| 8 | **Grafted alien** | Gain the Harvest trait of one creature that killed you (Director picks from its block) |
| 9 | **Unstable genome** | Once per Cycle you may re-roll one Inhibitor; but roll on the Cascade table when you do and fail |
| 10 | **Chosen by something** | An Order in the Bestiary now regards you as kin. It will help once, and it will come to collect |

## 5.8 Metabolic Stress

Inhibitors are not a damage system; they are an **attention** system. Every 1 you roll marks **1 Stress** on a track of 8.

| Stress | Effect |
| --: | :-- |
| 0–2 | Nothing. You are still in control of your chemistry. |
| 3 | −1 Reagent to all pools |
| 5 | −2 Reagents; you cannot gain Overclocked benefits |
| 6 | −3 Reagents; ATP costs +1 |
| 7 | On your next failed roll, roll on the Cascade table |
| 8 | **Autophagic Collapse**: immediately mark 2 Lysis Countdown boxes and drop to Stress 5 |

**Clearing Stress:** −1 at the end of a Cycle in which you spent no ATP and took no damage; −2 for a **Vent** action (2 ATP, 1 PP); all cleared by a **Reconstitution**; −3 by a **Chemotactic Rite** (a colony meal, 1 Cohesion).

## 5.9 Autophagy

When you spend your last ATP, or mark Stress 6+ with 2 ATP or less, you enter **Autophagy**: your body starts eating itself to keep the lights on.

* Each Cycle at your turn's start, lose **2 MI** and gain **3 ATP**.
* You may keep paying this way indefinitely. It is the single most heroic and most stupid rule in the game.
* While in Autophagy, Lysis Countdown boxes mark 1 extra per Cycle, and every Bursts' Effect Value halves.
* Autophagy ends when your ATP exceeds 4 again, or at 0 MI (Dormancy as normal).

## 5.10 Recovery

| Rest | Time | What it does |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Vent** | 1 PP in combat | −2 Stress |
| **Reconstitution** | 1 **Fugue Interval** (~an hour of drift-and-settle) | Full ATP; clear all Stress; heal MI equal to `VSC + Hull`; clear hazards |
| **Dormant Cycle** | 6 hours in a secured nest or cyst | Full MI; clear Lysis Countdown boxes; refresh once-per-encounter features; re-spend Colony Cohesion |
| **Mend** | downtime, per session | A Synthesis contest (AT 12+) removes one permanent scar of rarity Common–Uncommon; Severe scars need a Nucleoid ritual |

**Never award two full rests in a row.** The Expanse runs on scarcity of safety; a Reconstitution in a Current is not a Dormant Cycle, and the difference is the campaign's tension.

## 5.11 Death-spiral guardrails (for the table)

1. A Dormant organism may always be stabilised by an ally — the party is never helpless while one member can act.
2. The Countdown is **visible**. Write the boxes on the sheet in ink. Players should be able to say "I have four boxes left" — that sentence is play.
3. Total Lysis is a **choice the players can make** (Ch. XI: Legacy). It should be reachable but never arbitrary.
4. If the whole Cohort is going down, the Director asks: *who arrives?* In this world, something always smells a rupture.

# CHAPTER VI — ENZYMATIC BURSTS AND THE SIX DOMAINS

There is no magic in the Expanse. There is only the **Law of Catalysis**: with the right enzyme, at the right concentration, in the right gradient, anything a body can be *made* to do quickly.

The gold-tinted enzyme that makes the impossible cheap is **Aetheric Enzyme (AE)**. Organisms that carry AE in their vesicles, and know the sequence to spend it on, are called **Catalysts** by everyone else and **specialists** by themselves.

## 6.1 Anatomy of a Burst

A Burst is a printed procedure. Every Burst has:

| Field | Meaning |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Domain** | One of the six (§6.3). Your Phenotype has a **Bonded Domain**; Bursts from other domains cost **+2 ATP** and +1 Stress. |
| **Tier** | I–IV. Unlocks with your Level: Tier I at 1, II at 6, III at 11, IV at 16. |
| **Cost** | ATP and PP. Paying is the *trigger*: you declare the spend, then roll. |
| **Band** | Contact / Near / Mid / Far / Self / Zone. |
| **Pool** | Usually `RTC + Pathway (Catalysis)`, or the domain's own Pathway. Against a creature: the target's MT. Against terrain: the AT ladder. |
| **Effect Value (EV)** | The burst's own number, added to Overflow for damage/heal/pressure. Tier I +4, II +6, III +8, IV +10. |
| **Rider** | What Runaway Catalysis adds. |
| **Sustained?** | Whether it persists (§4.12). |

**Escalation.** A Burst learned at Tier I may be pushed to a higher Tier's EV by paying that Tier's cost — you do not need to know the higher version. This is the cheapest way for the engine to let a veteran do old tricks harder.

## 6.2 Learning and carrying Bursts

* Your Phenotype grants a fixed set. **Free Bursts** (all organisms) come from your lineage's Gift.
* Additional Bursts are learned from **Specimens** (a Pristine Sequence of a creature that uses it), 20–200 S, and a **Sequence Reading** contest at AT `10 + (2 × Burst Tier)`. On a failure, mark 1 Stress and try again after a Dormant Cycle.
* **AE capacity:** you can have at most **RTC + Pathway (Catalysis)** Burst "sequences hot" at once. The rest must be re-hot with a Synthesise action (2 PP, 1 Substrate).
* **Vials of AE** (Ch. IX) let anyone spend 1 Vial to make one Burst's pool **+2** or to fuel a Burst they do not know once. Vials are how a non-Catalyst stays useful in a Burst-heavy fight, and how a Catalyst survives an ambush.

## 6.3 The six Domains

| Domain | Damaging type | Pathway used | Signature feel |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **PROTEAN** | Kinetic | Protean Folding (or Synthesis) | Fold matter, build structures, bind, hold |
| **LYTIC** | Lytic | Catalysis | Dissolve, deny area, eat armour |
| **OSMOTIC** | Osmotic | Pressure Folding (or Fluid Navigation) | Push, pull, crush, move people |
| **THERMIC** | Thermal | Catalysis | Heat, cold, steam, pressure cook |
| **GALVANIC** | Voltaic | Signal Discourse | Interrupt, mark, command, hijack timing |
| **SOMATIC** | Genetic | Somatic Shaping | Grow, heal, mutate, rewrite the self and allies |

* **Sequence Override** (Signal Discourse, contested RTC vs RTC): steal the Burst's target or band after it hits the Threshold but before resolution. Only Wraiths, Messengers, and Commanders are expected to try this.

# CHAPTER VII — THE FLUID MEDIUM

The terrain is not a board. It is a **substance with opinions.** Every region has a **Tonic State**, a **Viscosity**, and usually a **Flow**, and those three numbers change every roll at the table. Learn to read them and you can beat an Elite with a current and a bad day.

## 7.1 The three terrain numbers

| Number | What it changes | Typical |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Tonicity** | Whether water wants to be inside you or outside you. Sets Baseline bonuses/penalties and Lysis risk. | Isotonic (neutral) |
| **Viscosity** | How much a Drift costs. | Clear (1 PP per Drift) / Viscous (2) / Gel (3) |
| **Flow** | Direction and force of movement. A Current of strength *n* pushes 1 band per Cycle against a failed `DFF + Fluid Navigation` at AT `8 + 2n`. | 0–4 |

Write them on the scene card. If you have to ask "can I swim through that?", the answer was already in the numbers.

## 7.2 Tonic states

| State | Effect on all organisms inside |
| :-- | :-- |
| **ISOTONIC** | Nothing. Home. Baseline everywhere. |
| **HYPERTONIC** (salt, sugar, heavy mineral) | **DFF pools −2**, **PP −1**, **VSC pools +2**, Drift costs +1 PP. Mark 1 Stress per Fugue Interval. Water is leaving you: every heal is halved. |
| **HYPOTONIC** (fresh, dilute) | **DFF pools +2**, **PP +1**, but **MT −2** and any hit for 6+ also inflicts **Turgid**. Lysis Countdown boxes mark +1 per Cycle while Dormant. Water is trying to burst you. |
| **ACIDIC SURGE** | Lytic damage `4 + tier × 2` per Cycle at turn start, unless soak ≥ 3 against Lytic. Grafts degrade: every 3 Cycles, −1 permanent soak until repaired. |
| **ALKALINE POCKET** | Reverse acid: no damage, but all Lytic Bursts cost double and Somatic healing is halved. |
| **THERMAL GRADIENT** | Choose: hot (Thermal pools +1, Volatiles thrive, Osmotic effects double) or cold (Cold: PP −1, all Sustained Reactions cost +1 ATP). |
| **ANOXIC / CATABOLIC** | ATP regen becomes 0. Bursts cost +2 ATP. Vent costs 1 MI. |
| **RADIANT (AE-rich)** | Runaway Catalysis threshold softens: a roll with **no Inhibitors** Runaways (two 6s not required). Tempting. Terrifying. See the Anomalies. |
| **NECROTIC (dead zone)** | Nothing lives, nothing regenerates, no Harvest. MI cannot be restored. Corpses here are *safe* — which is why colonies bury their dead in them. |

## 7.3 Zone traits (stack freely; keep 3–4 per scene)

| Trait | Rule |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Current (strength n)** | As above. Riding with the Flow gives +1 PP and +2 Drifts; against it costs double. |
| **Gel pocket** | Movement costs 3 PP per Drift; attacks into it −2; **Sequester** effects here have +4 to their break AT. |
| **Turbid** | Ranged attacks beyond Near are −2; beyond Mid auto-fail. Scent still works. |
| **Clear** | Far band sight works; Scent is −2. |
| **Debris field** | Half cover (+2 MT, +1 soak); Momentum impossible; Flank Breach risk doubled. |
| **Membrane wall** | Blocks all but Lytic/Osmotic soft effects. Can be breached: MI 20 + 10 × tier. |
| **Protein bridge** | Solid highway, no Flow. Can be collapsed (VSC or Lytic contest at AT 14) — the drop is real. |
| **Reef growth** | Provides cover, and 1 Substrate per 2 Cycles if harvested (1 Bulk cap). |
| **Spore bed** | Any Kinetic damage in the band releases spores: OSM save or **Masked**/**Catabolic** (Director's choice). |
| **Scent trail** | A track. Anyone with Chemoreception 2+ reads where you were and roughly when. |
| **Light bloom** | Bioluminescent: everyone gains +1 OSM, and **Masked** is impossible in the band. |
| **Nursery** | Filled with juvenile entities; harming them marks Stress and draws the Order's attention. |

## 7.4 Hazards

Hazards are creatures without intent. They have a **tier**, a **save AT**, and a **trigger**.

```
HAZARD DAMAGE = 4 + (2 × tier) per exposure
HAZARD SAVE  = 8 + (2 × tier) for the obvious attribute
```

| Hazard | Trigger | Damage/Effect | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Pore Charge** | A pressure plate of membrane | 8 + 2×tier Osmotic, push 1 band | A mine. Detectable by OSM at AT 12. |

# CHAPTER VIII — DRIFT, HARVEST, AND COLONY LIFE

## 8.1 Metabolic Time

*The Expanse does not count hours; it counts the Rhythm of bodies — the Pulse, the Beat, the Tide.*

Every unit of time in the Expanse is named for something a body does: it pulses, it beats, it drifts into fugue, it goes dormant. The rules call this **Metabolic Time**; organisms simply call it **the Rhythm** (*“by the Rhythm, it is the third Beat of the Rise”*).

| Unit | Length | Made of | Felt as | What it governs |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Turn** | your share of a Cycle | — | your moment to act | You receive Pulse Points (2 + DFF) and spend them; Split Frequency holds some to act later in the same Cycle (§4.2) |
| **Pulse Cycle** | 6 seconds | one turn for every organism, in Frequency order | a combat round | Combat; durations of “N Cycles”; one Reactive Osmosis per Cycle; −1 Stress after a quiet Cycle (§4.2, §5.8) |
| **Pulse War** | usually 3–6 Cycles (~18–36 s) | a Detection Phase, then Cycles | a fight | “Once per Pulse War” abilities; a fight is one scene (§4.1, §4.14) |
| **Scene** | up to ~1 Fugue Interval | as many Cycles or exchanges as it needs | one situation, one purpose, one place | “Once per scene” abilities (see What a scene is, below) |
| **Fugue Interval** | ~1 hour | 600 Cycles | an hour | A Reconstitution; one travel segment; foraging; a Chemotactic Rite; hazards that tick every hour (§5.10, §8.2) |
| **Dormant Cycle** | 6 hours | 6 Fugue Intervals | a night's sleep | Long rest: full MI, Lysis Countdown cleared, once-per-encounter features refreshed (§5.10) |
| **Metabolic Beat** | ~1 day | 24 Fugue Intervals: 20 lit, 4 dim | a day | Downtime, rituals, Re-Synthesis (1d6 Beats); meals are chemical, not culinary |
| **Drift Season** | ~30 Beats | 30 Beats | a season | Current routing changes; trade contracts rewrite; one phase of the Tide (Codex §5.3) |
| **Osmotic Tide** | ~4 Drift Seasons (~120 Beats) | Isotonic Rise → Hypertonic Zenith → Isotonic Fall → Hypotonic Zenith | a year | The world's grand tonicity swing: every region's numbers move; “once per Tide” abilities (Codex Ch. V) |
| **Great Cycle** | 16 Tides (~1,900 Beats) | 64 Drift Seasons | a generation | Registers are purged; old debts are re-read (Codex §5.1) |

**Conversions.** 10 Cycles = 1 minute · 600 Cycles = 1 Fugue Interval · 6 Fugue Intervals = 1 Dormant Cycle · 24 Fugue Intervals = 1 Beat · 30 Beats = 1 Drift Season · 4 Drift Seasons (120 Beats) = 1 Osmotic Tide · 16 Tides = 1 Great Cycle.

**Two “Tides”.** “Once per Tide” always means the Osmotic Tide (a year). The Codex's mass-battle rules (Codex §3.6) also count a *battle* Tide of one Fugue Interval; that use stays inside mass conflict.

**What a scene is.** Many Gifts, grafts, Vials and features work *once per scene*. A **scene** is one continuous situation with one purpose in one place — a Pulse War from the Detection Phase to its end (§4.14), one negotiation (the three exchanges of §11.1), one leg of travel (§8.2), one harvest or one job. A scene ends, and every *once per scene* ability resets, when the place, the purpose or the people involved change, or when about **one Fugue Interval** (~1 hour) has passed — whichever comes first. A scene is not a **Pulse Cycle** (6 seconds, one combat turn) and not a **Beat** (a day holds many scenes). When in doubt the Director calls the cut out loud — *“new scene”* — and the table resets its once-per-scene powers together.

## 8.2 Travel as play

Travel between named places is measured in **Drifts** and **segments** (one segment ≈ one Fugue Interval at the party's Flow). Do not roll for every hour. Instead:

1. Name the route's **three features** (a Current fork, a toll membrane, something dead and large).
2. Roll **one journey test**: `DFF + Fluid Navigation` for a Flow crossing, `OSM + Chemoreception` for reading a region, `VSC + Pressure Tolerance` for a tonic shock. Everyone participates via group roll (§1.7).
3. **On a failure, spend an encounter or a hazard** — never "nothing happens", never "you lose 3 days for no reason".
4. Every 2 Fugue Intervals in the open, mark **1 travel Stress** unless the Cohort spends a Fugue Interval chemically grooming (1 Substrate each; also +1 Cohesion).

**Flow chart example — Anchor's Reach to Sump Nine:** 4 segments. Fork of the Second Flow (Flow 3, DFF AT 14); a toll membrane with a Warden gate (social, AFF AT 12); the Carcass Shelf, where something enormous died centuries ago (OSM AT 16 or the Cohort wanders into a Harvest dispute with three Detritus Crawlers). Three beats, three rolls, one fight.

## 8.3 Foraging, harvest, and the field pantry

* **Harvest a kill:** 10 minutes, OSM or RTC contest (Bestiary block lists the AT). Yields Specimens + Substrate.
* **Forage a region:** 1 Fugue Interval, `OSM + Chemoreception` at the region's AT; yields `2 × tier` Substrate and possibly one Common Specimen.
* **Purify fluid:** Synthesis contest; failure makes you mark Stress and gain nothing.
* **Carrying a corpse:** Bulk = size class × 2. Dead things are worth more than most live ones, and everyone can smell that you know it.

## 8.4 Colonies, plots, and projects

An organism that survives long enough acquires **a place**. Colony rules live in the Codex (building them is the Director's job), but players care about these four:

| Asset | Cost | What it gives |
| :-- | --: | :-- |
| **Niche** (a secured alcove) | 100 S | Safe Dormant Cycle; +1 Cohesion recovery per Beat |
| **Workbench / Synthesis lab** | 300–1500 S | Craft at −20% Substrate; unlocks Rare recipes |
| **Store-cyst** | 250 S | +20 Bulk storage; immune to tonic drift |
| **Clientele** (3 colonists) | 200 S + upkeep 5 S/Beat | One free assist per scene; income `1d6 × 5 S`/Beat |
| **Vessel (drift skiff, symbiotic)** | 600–4000 S | Cross regions without Flow contests; carries 10–40 Bulk |
| **Warden contract** (reputation purchase) | 500 S or a service | One Order or faction will not shoot first, once |

**Upkeep is the point.** A colony with five assets costs 30 S a Beat. That number is why you take the next Vector.

## 8.5 Retainers, minions, and symbiotes

* A **retainer** (colonist, guard, scholar) uses the Bestiary's *Colonist* block. They follow simple orders, will not enter an obvious death, and cost 5–20 S a Beat.
* A **Symbiote** (Ch. IX) is a bonded living creature: it acts on your **Symbiosis** Pathway, shares your Reserve pool, and dies with a Stress mark on you. Max controlled creatures = AFF Grade (Sovereign Symbiosis: +2).
* **Commanding minions in a Pulse War:** spend 1 PP to give one minion a free Strike or Drift. This is how a Complement Cascade Commander becomes an army, and how a Replicator Agent becomes unbearable.

## 8.6 Rumour, information, and the price of a scent

Chemotaxis is the Expanse's internet. To buy information: name what you want to know, pay 5–50 S in *fresh chemical samples* (the actual currency of news), and roll `AFF + Signal Discourse` at AT 10–20. Success gives a true answer; **Overflow buys a second, related secret.** Failure gives a *confident* wrong answer — the Director must always know one true thing and one plausible lie about any question.

## 8.7 Reputation: six anchor factions

Tracked 0–10 with three states: **Hostile (0–1)**, **Wary (2–5)**, **Client (6–8)**, **Kin (9–10)**. Full details in the Codex; players need only know the ladder and that **every act of harvest, mercy, or contract is witnessed** by someone's chemistry.

# CHAPTER XI — CHEMICAL DIALOGUE

Nobody in the Expanse speaks with a tongue. Organisms **emit**: chains of signalling molecules, modulated by pressure, temperature, and the shape of the emitting organ. A sentence is a smell with grammar. A lie is a smell with the wrong shape, and someone can tell.

## 11.1 Three registers of conversation

| Register | Used for | Pool (attacker) | Defends with |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **SEMANTIC** — facts, trade, technical speech | contracts, bargaining, information | AFF + Signal Discourse | target's OSM + Chemoreception |
| **AFFECTIVE** — status, threat, reassurance, grief | intimidation, comfort, recruitment, command | AFF + Symbiosis or Vigour | target's VSC + Pressure Tolerance |
| **DECEPTIVE** — masking, forgery, false scent | lying, infiltrating, forgery | DFF + Concealment (or OSM + forgery tools) | target's OSM + Chemoreception, and **any** antigen register |

A social scene runs as **three exchanges** (like a duel). Each exchange is one contested roll; the winner's Overflow decides the shift on the Stakes Ladder. If nobody wins two of three, the conversation ends in stalemate — which is itself information.

## 11.2 Stakes Ladder

Never "roll to persuade". State what a success *buys*, then read the ladder:

| Rung | Result | Cost to the target |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Refusal** | "No." | nothing |
| **Concession** | information, a discount, a pass through a zone | small, deniable |
| **Compliance** | they do the thing, once, reluctantly | real but recoverable |
| **Binding** | they do the thing **and encode it as an Oath** | they lose an Oath box (11.4) |
| **Conversion** | they change allegiance, faction, or identity | they lose a Pathway rank to loyalty |

Overflow buys rungs: **4 Overflow = one rung**. A Runaway Catalysis buys **two**. Never skip a rung the target would not cross.

## 11.3 The Chemotactic Table: twelve moods

Read a creature's mood from how it smells. Moods shift by one rung per successful exchange (two with high Overflow).

| Mood | Smells like | Behaves | Shifts from |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Hostile** | acid, heat | attacks on sight | Kin (betrayal), Wary (threat) |
| **Wary** | salt, thin | questions, blocks, tests | Client (service), Hostile (pressure) |
| **Curious** | sweetness, sharp | asks, offers trades | Wary (boredom), Client (useful) |
| **Client** | balanced, ritual | helps for price | Kin (repeated service), Wary (non-payment) |
| **Kin** | yours | risks itself for you | Client (betrayal), Devoted (sacrifice) |
| **Devoted** | yours, amplified | dies for you once | Kin (betrayal), Fanatic (martyrdom) |
| **Fanatic** | burning | will burn the world for the idea | Devoted (doubt), Hostile (a better idea) |
| **Grieving** | cold, flat | withdraws, memorialises, holds grudges | Hostile (if you caused it), Kin (if you help) |
| **Hungry** | enzymatic, hot | takes, harvests, follows | Wary (fed), Hostile (denied) |
| **Terrified** | ammonia | flees, screams chemically, bites | Hostile (cornered), Kin (rescued) |
| **Ritualised** | neutral, layered | formal, bound by rite | Client (rite completed), Hostile (rite broken) |
| **Masked** | nothing | says nothing, decides everything | any (it chose to be unreadable) |

## 11.4 Oaths: promises written in your own membrane

An **Oath** is literal: you fold a sequence into your own chromatin so that breaking the promise damages you. This is the Expanse's whole social contract, and it is mechanical.

* **Encoding** takes a Chemotactic Rite (1 Fugue Interval, 10 Substrate, one witness with OSM 3+). Mark **Oath boxes = AFF Grade** on your sheet.
* **Keeping** an Oath: +1 Cohesion per scene where keeping it costs you something real.
* **Breaking** an Oath (voluntary or through failure): mark **2 Lysis Countdown boxes**, drop the witness faction one rung, and roll once on the Legacy Scarring table (§5.7).
* **Oath-breaking is not a punishment; it is a plot.** An Oath box spent is the Director's licence to make the consequence *interesting* rather than merely fatal.
* Some things cannot be sworn: a Warden Circles antigen oath excludes Viral Wraiths entirely; a Deep Choir oath is incompatible with the Hegemony's licence registry.

## 11.5 Bonds between organisms

A **Bond** is a two-way chemical agreement (PC–PC, PC–NPC, PC–symbiote). Each Bond costs both parties one Oath box and gives:

| Benefit | Rule |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Shared sense** | once per scene, perceive through the bonded partner's Contact band |
| **Metabolic gift** | transfer up to 5 ATP or 5 MI to the partner as a 1-PP action, 5 S cost |
| **Reserve relay** | you may spend your Reserve to defend your partner (Reactive Osmosis at range) |
| **Frequency echo** | if one is surprised, the other is not |
| **Grief** | if the partner Total-Lyses, you mark 3 Stress and gain +2 to all pools for 3 Cycles |

Maximum Bonds = AFF Grade. Breaking a Bond requires a Reconstitution and marks 2 Stress on both.

| Rite | Cost | What it does |
| :-- | --: | :-- |
| **Chemotactic Rite** (shared meal of rendered Substrate) | 3 S each, 1 Fugue Interval | +2 Cohesion, clear all Stress to 0, shift one NPC mood one rung per participant |

## 11.7 Interrogation and the ethics of memory

Truth in the Expanse is *chemical and cheap*. A **Memory Vial** (§9.5) can be extracted from a dead or Dormant organism with a Sequence Reading contest (AT 16) — and the organism's Oath boxes limit what it can be made to release: an oath-bound subject cannot yield the sworn secret, and attempting it destroys the sample.

Live extraction requires the subject's **Denatured**, **Locked**, or **Sequestered** state and marks **2 Stress on every participant**. The Director should treat this as a faction act, not a free action: the Hegemony licenses extraction, the Warden Circles criminalise it, and the Sequence Underground *sells it to you* to see who buys.

# CHAPTER XII — DEATH, SCARRING, AND LEGACY

## 12.1 What a death is for

A Total Lysis should tell the table something true about the world: that it is indifferent, that harvest is respect, and that the dead keep working. Play the moment. In the Expanse, the dead are not buried; they are **returned to the argument.**

## 12.2 Legacy: the successor organism

When an organism Total-Lyses, the player builds a **Legacy** — not a new character, a *continuation of the same argument* by different means.

**Inheritance table (roll or choose two):**

| d6 | Inheritance |
| --: | :-- |
| 1 | One graft from the dead organism's slots, installed free, no contest |
| 2 | One Pathway rank from the dead organism (max 3 for the successor) |
| 3 | One Specimen cache: `tier × 2` of the dead's Harvest grade |
| 4 | The dead's faction standing (one rung lower) and one named contact |
| 5 | One of the dead's scars (choose which) — the body remembers |
| 6 | One of the dead's Bonds, transferred to the successor at half strength |

The successor begins at the Cohort's current Level with the standard starting kit for that Level, **minus** everything the dead is still carrying (the dead's gear stays with the Cohort if someone physically carries it — a Bulk cost and a moral one).

**Colony inheritance:** the dead's plot, clients, and debts pass to the successor if the successor performs a Mourning Rite. Otherwise the Hegemony reclaims them in 3 Beats. Debts to the Hegemony are chemical and cannot be escaped by dying.

## 12.3 Retirement and the long tail

At any point in Stage IV, an organism may **Retire** into one of four endings, each of which becomes a location or a faction problem in later campaigns:

| Ending | What the world gains |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Wall** | Your body is grafted into a colony's border. It still works. It still remembers you. |
| **Foundry** | Your sequences are licensed into production. Your children are industrial. |
| **Choir** | You walk into a Deep region and stop being a person. Something out there now has your voice. |
| **Seed** | You are sealed in a cyst with one wish. Somewhere, a warden keeps the cyst and knows what you wished. |

A retired organism's asset list (Colony sheet) stays in play. Retired characters are the best NPCs you already own.

## 12.4 Apotheosis: past Level 20 (optional)

Beyond 20, Growth costs **1,000 Codon per Level** and each Growth grants a **Synthesis Choice** plus one **Apotheotic Choice**:

* +1 Baseline Grade (above 6, to a hard 8)
* +1 Pathway rank (to Sovereign+)
* a Domain Master: your Bonded Domain's Bursts cost 0 PP once per Cycle
* an Order Authority: command one Bestiary Order's creatures as minions (AFF limit applies)
* a Region: one region's tonic state is permanently yours to set once per Tide

Every Apotheotic Choice adds one **Nucleoid Attention** mark. At three marks, the Curator comes (Bestiary, Anomalies). Apotheosis is not a reward; it is a negotiation with the world's centre, and the centre bills.

# APPENDIX B — GLOSSARY OF THE EXPANSE

**Activation Threshold (AT)** — the number a pool must equal or beat. · **Affectives** — status-signal speech. · **AE (Aetheric Enzyme)** — the golden catalyst that makes Bursts cheap; also *gold-tinted residue* in loot. · **Anchored** — Pathway rank 2. · **Antigen register** — a faction's list of recognised "self" chemistries. · **Apparatus** — a weapon or tool-organ. · **ATP** — energy and living currency. · **Autophagy** — eating yourself for fuel. · **Baseline (Homeostatic)** — 3 × dice, the un-rolled success. · **Bands** — contact/near/mid/far/loose distance zones. · **Bond** — a two-way chemical agreement between organisms. · **Breach** — damage exceeding MI by 8+; instant Dormancy. · **Breach Overflow** — the amount you beat a Threshold by. · **Bulk** — carried matter. · **Burst** — a catalytic procedure (magic). · **Catabolic** — energy-starved condition. · **Catalysis** — the Pathway of Bursts. · **Catalyst** — an organism that uses Bursts. · **Cohesion** — group trust resource. · **Cycle (Pulse Cycle)** — six seconds of combat. · **Codon** — earned information; progression. · **Concentration → Sustained Reaction** — a Burst you hold. · **Curator** — the Nucleoid's answer to overreach. · **Denatured** — catalytic functions impaired. · **Detection Phase** — the contested scent roll before a Pulse War. · **Dormancy** — 0 MI state. · **Dormant Cycle** — long rest. · **Drift** — ~2 m of fluid, and the unit of movement. · **Enzymatic Pathway** — a learned skill, rank 0–4. · **Escalation** — paying a higher Tier's cost for a known Burst. · **Expressed** — Pathway rank 1. · **Flow** — your Drift speed; also current strength. · **Frequency Check** — initiative. · **Fugue Interval** — about an hour; the rest/travel block. · **Gene-Debt** — the price a Relic takes. · **Gift** — a lineage's once-per-scene ability. · **Graft** — installed alien biology; armour and organs; uses Slots. · **Grow** — gain a Level. · **Harvest** — extracting Specimens and Substrate from the dead. · **Hull** — structural tradition, 3/4/6, part of MI. · **Hypercatalysis → Runaway Catalysis** — no Inhibitors, two 6s. · **Inhibitor** — a rolled 1: costs attention, marks Stress. · **Latent** — Pathway rank 0. · **Legacy** — a successor organism built from the dead. · **Lysis Countdown** — boxes to death. · **Lysis (Total)** — death; your contents join the world. · **Membrane Integrity (MI)** — hit points. · **Membrane Threshold (MT)** — how hard you are to connect with. · **Metabolic Beat** — a day. · **Metabolic Stress** — attention/damage track, 0–8. · **Metabolic Time** — the Expanse's way of counting time, by what bodies do: Turn, Pulse Cycle, Fugue Interval, Beat, Drift Season, Osmotic Tide, Great Cycle (§8.1). Organisms call it *the Rhythm*. · **Nucleoid** — the Core's centre; the AT 24 rung. · **Oath box** — your capacity to promise. · **Order** — one of the seven enemy traditions. · **Overflow** — see Breach Overflow. · **Overdrive** — buying dice with energy or body. · **Osmotic Tide** — the world-scale tonicity season. · **Parasitic graft** — a graft that chose you. · **Pathway** — see Enzymatic Pathway. · **Phenotype** — your class (24 exist). · **Pulse Points (PP)** — action economy per Cycle. · **Pulse War** — a fight. · **Reagent Pool** — the dice you roll. · **Reconstitution** — short rest. · **Rejection** — too many Slots. · **Relic** — a rule-breaking artefact with a Gene-Debt. · **Rider** — a Burst/weapon's extra effect on Runaway Catalysis. · **Runaway Catalysis** — the critical success. · **Scene** — one continuous situation with one purpose in one place: a Pulse War from Detection to its end, one negotiation, one leg of travel, one harvest. It ends when the place, the purpose or the people change, or after about one Fugue Interval (~1 hour). *Once per scene* resets then (§8.1). · **Segment** — a swarm body unit. · **Sovereign** — Pathway rank 4. · **Soak** — damage reduction. · **Specimen** — harvested genetic loot (C/G/P/T). · **Split Frequency** — holding PP to act later in the Cycle. · **Stages I–IV** — Level bands 1/6/11/16. · **Symbiote** — a bonded living creature. · **Symbiosis** — the Pathway of bonds and command. · **Synthesis Choice** — the paid upgrade at each Growth. · **Terminal Lysis → Total Lysis.** · **Tier (1–5)** — threat band, tied to Level bands. · **Tonicity** — whether water wants to be inside you. · **Total Lysis** — death. · **Vector** — a quest: a task that transmits you somewhere. · **Vent** — spend energy to shed Stress. · **Yield** — a Vector's reward.
