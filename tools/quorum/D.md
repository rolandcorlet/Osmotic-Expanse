Some are ancient, some are sick, all of them have moods.

## 14.2 Learning: Lodges and Induction

Pathways are taught in **Lodges**. A student is **Induced**; a master is the Lodge's **Source**. Each of the 24 Phenotypes (Atlas §3) keeps a Lodge in every Colony-sized settlement: a bunk, a meal, and gossip for members of the trade. A Lodge counts as a Hearth for its Phenotype.

* **Training** follows the Core's prices — Pathway ranks at 60, 150 and 400 S, with rank 3 and above needing a teacher or a P-grade Specimen (Core §9.9) — and the Codex's time and AT (Codex §5.7). What QUORUM adds is that **the teacher is a person**: a Lodge's Source teaches for a Ledger line (Ch. 6), usually one service a Tide.
* **Leaving a Lodge** badly is a line you owe it.

## 16.3 Rooting a character

The Atlas makes an organism (Atlas §1). These six choices put it **inside** the world. Add them after Step 6 (*Write three things down*). Each takes a minute.

| # | Choice | Options | Gives |
| --: | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | **Budline** | roll or choose (Ch. 7) | a root name, two relatives (one Wary, one Kin), and the budline's d6 effect |
| 2 | **Condition of birth** | Dry, Settled or Swollen (Ch. 5) | starting Substrate +60 / +0 / −20 (Swollen: +1 Cohesion at the first session) |
| 3 | **Organ of origin** | one of the six (Ch. 2) | +1 die on social rolls with that organ; its Hearth will feed you; one enemy in another organ |
| 4 | **One plasmid** | roll (Ch. 7) or invent one | +1 die to a narrow task, permanent — a gift at your first division |
| 5 | **One Ledger line each way** | one thing owed to you, one thing you owe (Ch. 6) | you begin already owed and owing, like everyone |
| 6 | **One Clock running on you** | a Warden register, a landlord, a creditor, a Choir circle that sang for your budding | 4 segments; it lives on the PC block (Ch. 18) |

Then choose the **Cohort's sponsor** together (Ch. 15).

**Worked example — Siss-of-the-Nine-Smells** (Atlas §1). *Budline:* a famous unbroken Oath (+1 Cohesion the first time Siss keeps any Oath in a session). *Condition:* Settled. *Organ of origin:* the Pump — Siss grew up running messages for haulers, and the Vacuole's quartermasters still remember the strike she carried word of. *Plasmid:* read a Speaker's honesty. *Owed:* the haulers of Hab Six owe her a hiding place. *Owes:* 40 S to a Flow Guild courier-mother, witnessed at the Contract Fair. *Clock:* the Brine Office has a sample of her — she carried the strike's word through their gate. *Sponsor (with the table):* the Flow Guilds.

## 17.2 SUMP NINE — a Settlement on the Fork of the Second Flow

*Printed as a destination: four segments from Anchor's Reach, past a toll membrane with a Warden gate (Core §8.2). QUORUM fills it in: a poor salvage settlement at a channel mouth, where the Second Flow's wrecks wash up.*

```
SUMP NINE              Settlement · Q 6 · founders: Flagellan salvage crews
POPULATION             1,800  ≈ 120 hearths
FEED                   9,000 S/Beat        TALLY  1,200 S/Beat  (13%)
MOBILISED              150 = BP 15
STRUCTURE              nine membrane walls 20/12 · one somatic gate 60/16 · a gel moat at the nursery
COLONY COHESION        4        INTERIOR 4 (a strike is forming)
CHARTER                (1) no Total Dissolution inside the membrane; (2) the water tally is not negotiable;
                       (3) every hab feeds one nursery shift
OFFICES                Porter (Immunan, Warden-linked) · Tally-Keeper (Epithalian, in arrears) ·
                       Registrar (Lysosan, Unlisted since the last purge, serving anyway) ·
                       Reader (Ash) · Muster (Barophil) · Printwright vacant · Steward vacant and disputed
CLOCKS                 COLONY 4/6 · the Tally-Keeper's Hegemony loan, 3 Beats behind
```

**The books don't foot.** Spending on pumps, waste, the nursery and the gate runs slightly over the tally. The Tally-Keeper is three Beats behind on a Hegemony loan at 5 S/Beat (Codex §7.1) and frightened. **Tonight:** an Oath plague in Hab Six (Codex §11.3); a nursery collapse because its protein supports were cut for salvage; and the Registrar offering to erase one line — wearing Archivist Dol's face (Codex §7.2). Ch. 4's worked Convocation happens here.

## 17.3 The Tallow Market

*A floating bazaar on a Lipid Plateau raft: 400 organisms, no weapons, no registers (Codex §6.2).* The Expanse's **Oath market**: you can buy someone else's Oath obligation, and it binds.

* **Transfers are lawful here** — the only place a Ledger line can be **transferred** without a Rite (Ch. 6).
* **No registers** means the Unlisted trade openly, and the Wardens hate it.
* **The hook printed in the Codex:** someone wants the Cohort to hold a specific debt. Run the purchase as a Convocation; run what the debt *is* as a Vector.

## 17.5 Knot Nine

*A junction where seven ribbons cross: a city, a market, a courtroom (Codex §6.5).* A Metropolis by size (Q 10) with **Bridge law** — on a bridge, an Oath is enforced by the bridge. The Warden register here has recorded a Cohort member as **two different organisms**, and the law says one of them must be lysed. Run that as a trial (Ch. 10) where both defendants are the same PC. **Archivist Dol** keeps the register (Codex §7.2) and erases entries for the Underground. Counterfeiting a signature in trail-script (Ch. 11) is Knot Nine's commonest crime.
