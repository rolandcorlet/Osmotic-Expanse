# QUORUM — Colonies, Kin, and Law · v3 table edition

*The society book of The Osmotic Expanse, in the v3 format. Every rule and number here is taken from QUORUM (rev 2) and is unchanged; the section is given on each card so you can find the full text. What is new is the delivery: each part says what it is in plain words, when you use it, the rule as short steps, and how it sounds at the table. The AI Director looks these same cards up during play.*

*This edition covers what a table needs to run society in play. QUORUM (rev 2) remains the full reference for the Colony Sheet and Colony Builder, lineages in society, culture, the three sample settlements, and the tables of ideas (chapters 2.6–2.8, 8, 14, 16.2–17, 18).*

## How to use this book

You never need to announce which rule you are using. A player says what their character does, in their own words. The Director decides which card it falls under, says what is at stake, and calls a roll only if the outcome is in doubt. Most society scenes need no roll at all: they need someone who wants something.

## 1 · The four questions

*QUORUM Ch. 1*

**In plain words.** Every social scene comes down to four things: how they feel tonight, what they remember of you, what you can afford to be here, and what trouble is already coming.

**You use this when:** The Cohort walks into any room with people in it.

**The rule**

- Mood: how they feel right now. It can change in one exchange.
- Standing: your history with their faction, 0 to 10.
- Condition: your class here (Dry, Settled, Swollen or Unlisted).
- A Clock: what is already coming for you.
- Mood is not Standing. Say which you mean: "Its mood is Hostile, that is tonight. Its Standing with you is Wary, that is history."

**At the table.** Player: "Will the gate-clerk help us?" Director: "She is in a foul mood tonight, but the Wardens still think well of you. She will help, and she will be rude about it."

## 2 · Standing

*QUORUM §1.1*

**In plain words.** How much a faction trusts you, as a number from 0 to 10. It is your reputation with that group, and it is remembered.

**You use this when:** Someone asks "what do they think of us?", or the Cohort does something a faction saw.

**The rule**

- 0–1 Hostile: they shoot first, or will not trade.
- 2–3 Wary: they question, block, test.
- 4–5 Neutral: they deal with you as a party, not a person.
- 6–7 Obliging: help, for a price.
- 8 Confided: they hide something for you and tell you one true thing you did not ask for.
- 9–10 Bound: you are inside their Charter, with a duty, a voice and protection.
- It moves only if the faction witnessed what you did.
- One rung up with a faction is one rung down with its rival.
- Standing is not friendship.

**At the table.** Director: "The Ash saw you harvest that body properly. Their Standing with you goes up one. The Wardens, their rivals, hear of it: down one."

## 3 · A colony is a body

*QUORUM Ch. 2*

**In plain words.** A town here is organised like a living body, with six organs. Everyone belongs to one. Your organ is your place, not your job: who feeds you, who you answer to, who looks for you if you vanish.

**You use this when:** The Cohort arrives in a settlement, or needs something from "the authorities".

**The rule**

- The Membrane: gates, borders, the register. Sells passage and registration.
- The Pump: labour and haulage. Sells hands, or a strike.
- The Vacuole: stores, water rights, the treasury. Sells loans, rations, storage.
- The Nucleolus: the council. Sells rulings, offices, charters.
- The Hearth: the commons. Shared meals, healing, Rites, news.
- The Lysosome: waste, the dead, and justice. Sells Assays, burial, salvage.
- Organ health runs Sound, Strained, Failing, Necrotic, Excised. A failing organ is an adventure.

**At the table.** Player: "Who do we see about water?" Director: "That is the Vacuole. The Tally-Keeper holds the water rights, and the Vacuole here is Strained, so she will want collateral."

## 4 · The seven offices and the Assembly

*QUORUM Ch. 3*

**In plain words.** Seven named jobs run a colony. Together their votes make the Assembly: fifteen voices, eight to pass, ties go to the Tally-Keeper.

**You use this when:** The Cohort needs a decision from someone with authority, or wants to hold office.

**The rule**

- Porter (the Membrane, 2 voices): who is read at which gate.
- Registrar (the Membrane, 3): enters and erases register lines; witnesses every Oath.
- Tally-Keeper (the Vacuole, 3): sets and audits the tally; owns the water debt.
- Steward (the Vacuole and the Hearth, 2): feeds the colony.
- Reader (the Lysosome, 2): funerals, last-memory readings, harvest brokerage.
- Printwright (a Hegemony lease, 1): holds the leased print shift.
- Muster (the militia, 2): calls the militia.
- Each office has a boon and a burden. Offices are re-chosen at the Contract Fair.

**At the table.** Director: "Three people can make that happen: the Registrar, the Tally-Keeper or the Porter. The Registrar owes you nothing. The Porter does."

## 5 · Quorum: the whole colony decides

*QUORUM §4.1–4.8*

**In plain words.** A public vote you can smell. Everyone signals, and when enough of the colony leans one way, the question is settled and everybody knows it.

**You use this when:** A whole colony must know the answer: war, exile, the water, a charter. Not for a private deal.

**The rule**

- Name the Issue and its two answers. Draw a track from Against to For; it starts in the middle.
- The length each way is the colony's Quorum number: Hamlet 4, Settlement 6, Colony 8, Metropolis 10. High Cohesion makes it 2 shorter; low Cohesion 2 longer.
- Each round, every PC and named person takes one signal action. Play each as a scene, not just a roll.
- Speak at the Hearth: AFF + Signal Discourse against 12. Moves 1, +1 per 4 Overflow.
- Move the crowd: AFF + Symbiosis against 12. Moves 1, +1 per 4 Overflow; a failure moves 1 the other way.
- Pay for signal: 10 S × the Quorum number, no roll. Moves 1, once per side per round.
- Swear it: a public Oath tying yourself to the outcome. Moves 2.
- Court an organ, or call a faction that is Obliging or better: 1 per round while they back you.
- False signal (a lie): DFF + Concealment against 16. Moves 2, and if exposed swings 3 the other way.
- Quench (silencing the vote, a crime): RTC + Synthesis against 16.
- Then the Speaker reads the air and the marker moves. At either end the colony knows. Ask: who lost, and what will they do tonight?
- Run it like a fight: three to five rounds, fast, with someone always about to cheat.

**At the table.** Director: "The Issue is whether the Pump gets a seat on the council. The track is six each way. Bresk, you are first: what do you do to sway them?" Player: "I stand up at the shared meal and tell them who carried the water last Tide." Director: "That is speaking at the Hearth. Affinity, against twelve."

## 6 · Convocation: a room decides

*QUORUM §4.9–4.11*

**In plain words.** A negotiation with three to six parties around one proposal, called the Motion. Each wants something different.

**You use this when:** A meeting, a hearing, a deal with several sides. For one person, just talk.

**The rule**

- Each round, each party makes one move. Three to five rounds.
- Offer: AFF + Signal Discourse against their OSM + Chemoreception. Every 4 Overflow moves them a rung, against a real price you name.
- Read: learn a party's price, leverage or a secret. If they are not hiding it, no roll.
- Reveal: produce evidence (a witnessed Ledger line, a register entry, a Memory Vial). One rung, no roll. Each piece works once.
- Alliance: AFF + Symbiosis against their VSC + Pressure Tolerance. Two parties move as one. Costs 1 Cohesion or a Ledger line.
- Bind: propose the Motion as an Oath, so the outcome is enforceable.
- Pressure: one rung; on a failure the room turns a step toward Hostile and the colony's Clock advances.
- Walk: leave. You drop a rung with the host, and every party must answer why you left. Strongest when the room already leans your way.
- Never skip a rung a party would not cross.
- A party bound to you can be played by proxy at −1 die.

**At the table.** Player: "I put the ledger page on the table: their foreman promised us this, and the Registrar saw it." Director: "That is a Reveal. No roll: the room moves a rung your way. The Guild's factor has evidence of her own. Does she use it?"

## 7 · Condition: Dry, Settled, Swollen, Unlisted

*QUORUM Ch. 5*

**In plain words.** Class. The Dry are dense and rich, the Settled are the middle, the Swollen are watery and poor, the Unlisted are off the books entirely. It shows on the body.

**You use this when:** Deciding who gets served, believed or let in; when the Cohort's fortunes change.

**The rule**

- Upkeep each Tide: Swollen 0, Settled 10 S, Dry 40 S.
- Condition is held by a Name, a licence, a hearth or a seat.
- It sets how many Ledger lines you may carry: Swollen 3, Settled 5, Dry 7.
- The Tide moves the ladder every season (QUORUM §5.3 has the table).
- The Unlisted have no rights and pay no tax.

**At the table.** Director: "You are Settled here, so the steward seats you, but after the Dry family. If you want the front table, that is a different class of problem."

## 8 · The Ledger: who owes whom

*QUORUM Ch. 6*

**In plain words.** A page on the Cohort's sheet listing favours owed to you, favours you owe, and which of them you can prove.

**You use this when:** Someone promises something, or the Cohort wants to call in or trade a favour.

**The rule**

- A line is one sentence: who, what, witnessed by whom.
- An unwitnessed line is only a rumour. It may be true; it cannot be called in.
- Call it in: one scene. It buys one concession, one helper's assist, or one pass through a gate. Once per line.
- Refusing a called line costs you: the witness's faction drops a rung, and worse if it was sworn as an Oath.
- Pay it off: the line clears, and you clear 1 Stress or gain 1 Cohesion.
- A line can be transferred, forged, or inherited by the dead's family.
- A witnessed line can be spent as evidence in a Convocation.

**At the table.** Player: "Ock owes us one hard harvest. We call it in." Director: "The Registrar witnessed that, so he cannot refuse without the Guild losing face. He sends his best cutter, once."

## 9 · Kin, names and favours

*QUORUM Ch. 7*

**In plain words.** Nobody has parents. Bodies divide, and both halves remember being one. Family (a budline) is the record of those divisions, with the debts and grudges that divided with them. Between families, people live by small priced favours.

**You use this when:** A character's family, name or inheritance matters; someone offers a plasmid.

**The rule**

- A plasmid is the deepest courtesy: a gift of a skill. +1 die to a narrow task; lasts a Tide unless integrated.
- Debts and Ledger lines pass down a budline when someone dies.
- Names are earned and recorded; a Naming Rite makes one official.

**At the table.** Director: "She offers you a plasmid for reading old script. Taking it is a gift, and gifts here are written down."

## 10 · The uphill economy

*QUORUM Ch. 9*

**In plain words.** Goods drift for free from where they are plentiful to where they are scarce. Carrying anything the other way, uphill, costs energy. The whole economy is the argument over who pays for uphill.

**You use this when:** Prices, haulage, strikes, smuggling.

**The rule**

- Hauling uphill: +1 Stress per 2 segments, or 2 ATP per segment.
- The Pump (the haulers) can strike, and a strike is a weapon.
- Channel-makers open illegal routes; it is a crime against the Membrane.

**At the table.** Director: "It is cheap here because it drifts here. Carrying it back up to the Rim is where the money is, and the work."

## 11 · Carcass law: trials

*QUORUM Ch. 10*

**In plain words.** Justice is a reading. The accused is not only questioned; their body is tested, because here a body cannot easily lie about what it is.

**You use this when:** The Cohort, or someone they care about, is accused.

**The rule**

- A trial is a Convocation whose Motion is "what is owed".
- The room: accuser, accused, the Reader, a Quorum Speaker, up to two interested parties.
- Each party states lineage, tonic and Oaths. Refusing starts you a rung down.
- In round two the Reader assays the accused: RTC + Sequence Reading against 12 (16 for an Elite or a Lysosan). A success reveals the truth.
- The leading side's Motion then goes to the colony as an Issue, and the verdict is confirmed by Quorum.
- A hung verdict means "clear but watched": −1 die at the Membrane until the next Rereading.
- Three ways out, always: pay, swear, or run.

**At the table.** Director: "The Reader will test you in the second round. If you have nothing to hide, that helps you. If you do, now is the time to say how you intend to fool it."

## 12 · The scent-web: news and proof

*QUORUM Ch. 11*

**In plain words.** News is a smell that travels. A rumour gets harder to kill the longer it lives, until it is treated as law.

**You use this when:** The Cohort spreads, traces or tries to kill a story.

**The rule**

- To disprove: a Fresh rumour 8, a Repeated one 12, a Settled one 16. Law can only be erased.
- A smell is not proof. A recorded line is. A Reveal in front of a crowd also counts.

**At the table.** Director: "It is still a fresh rumour. One good counter-fact, said where people can hear, kills it. Wait a season and it will be much harder."

## 13 · The year: four festivals

*QUORUM Ch. 12*

**In plain words.** Each season has a festival that bends a rule for a few days and puts everyone important in one place.

**You use this when:** Setting a scene in time, or when the Cohort needs a rule to bend.

**The rule**

- The Contract Fair (3 Beats): debts signed, charters granted, offices re-chosen. An Oath sworn here costs no Substrate.
- The Salt Vigil (1 Beat and a dim): fasting, the arrears audit, duels in the open.
- The Rendering (2 Beats): the year's dead honoured and harvested. Harvest +1 grade.
- The Swell (10 Beats): masks and carnival; the poor rule the streets. Lying about who you are is lawful.
- The Rereading (every 16 Tides): registers purged, old debts re-read, the Unlisted may apply again.

**At the table.** Director: "It is the Swell. Everyone is masked and nobody may ask who you are. That will not be true in nine days."

## 14 · The duel that replaces a war

*QUORUM §12.4*

**In plain words.** Two champions fight in public to settle a dispute that would otherwise cost a hundred lives.

**You use this when:** Two sides are heading for bloodshed over a debt, a right or an insult.

**The rule**

- Name what the duel decides. Both sides swear to it as an Oath.
- Each side names a champion, who may fight for someone else.
- Each side may name a second, who may produce evidence once, before blood.
- The bout is a Pulse War between the two. It ends at the first yield, knockout or ring-out.
- Killing someone who has yielded marks 2 Stress on every witness and costs 1 Cohesion.

**At the table.** Director: "The Muster would rather not bury anyone. She offers a duel: your champion, theirs, and the water right goes to the winner."

## 15 · Host-Cities

*QUORUM Ch. 13*

**In plain words.** The largest creatures are not fought. They are lived in, like a city inside a giant, and the giant has moods.

**You use this when:** The Cohort is inside a living city.

**The rule**

- Rent: 1 ATP per tenant per Beat.
- The Host has a Temper Clock of 6 (QUORUM §13.3 says what moves it and what happens when it fills).

**At the table.** Director: "You feel the walls flush. The Host did not like that."

## 16 · Cohort charters

*QUORUM Ch. 15*

**In plain words.** A licence that makes your group count as one legal person, backed by a sponsor. It is why four strangers are allowed to roam, fight and harvest.

**You use this when:** Starting a campaign; passing gates; when the sponsor asks for something.

**The rule**

- The seal lets each member through a registered gate without a Rite, once per Beat.
- Every sponsor grants something, asks something, and has a rival.
- Hegemony: cheap licensed gear; one enforcement job per Tide.
- Warden Circles: the right to detain; report every unregistered person.
- Flow Guilds: free passage on Guild currents; carry one cargo a season.
- Reclaimers' Ash: harvest rights on unclaimed bodies; harvest every body properly.
- Sequence Underground: safe houses; carry a sequence and do not read it.
- Deep Choir: shelter in the deep; sing at the Salt Vigil.
- Ranks: Provisional, Sealed, Named, Ancient.

**At the table.** Director: "The gate reads your seal and opens. Somewhere a clerk notes that the Ash's Cohort came through at second shift."

## 17 · A day in a colony

*QUORUM §16.1*

**In plain words.** What everyone is doing right now when the Cohort walks in.

**You use this when:** Describing a settlement at any hour.

**The rule**

- A Beat (a day) is 24 intervals: 20 lit, 4 dim.
- 1–2: the Hearths wake; the gates open their registers.
- 3–10: first shift. The Pump hauls, markets run, the Lysosome holds its assays.
- 11–12: the shared meal, everywhere at once.
- 13–20: second shift; at its end the Speaker reads the air.
- 21–24, dim: curfew at the gates. Smugglers and lovers move.

**At the table.** Director: "It is the shared meal. Every Hearth is eating at once, so the streets are empty and anyone you want is sitting down."
