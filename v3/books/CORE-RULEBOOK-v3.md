# The Catalytic Engine — Core Rulebook · v3 table edition

*The rules of The Osmotic Expanse in the v3 format. Every rule and number is from the Core Rulebook and is unchanged; each card cites its section so you can find the full text. What is new is the delivery: what it is in plain words, when you use it, the rule as short steps, how it sounds at the table, and who does the work. The AI Director looks these same cards up during play.*

*This edition covers what a table needs to play. The Core Rulebook remains the full reference for Bursts and Vials, terrain and tonic in detail, crafting, the full modifier list, social chemistry (registers, the Stakes Ladder, Rites, Bonds), Cohesion, levelling, and death and legacy.*

## 1 · How play works

*New in v3*

**In plain words.** You say what your character does, in your own words. The Director says what happens. Dice come out only when the outcome is in doubt and something is at stake.

**You use this when:** Always. This is the whole game.

**Who does the work:** Player says; Director rules; Engine counts.

**The rule**

- Nothing at stake: it just happens.
- Impossible here: the Director says why, in the fiction, and what is in reach.
- A printed rule covers it (a Strike, a harvest, a toll): use that rule.
- Otherwise it is a roll. The Director picks the score by how you do it and the difficulty by how hard it is, and says what failing will cost before you roll.
- After the roll the situation has changed, pass or fail. A failed roll never means "nothing happens".

**At the table.** Player: "I wedge my flail in the gate so it cannot close." Director: "Nothing stops you. It holds. The drone looks at the flail, then at you."

## 2 · The one roll

*Core 1.1*

**In plain words.** Roll a handful of six-sided dice and add them up. Ones are bad: they count for nothing and cost you Stress.

**You use this when:** The outcome is in doubt and matters.

**Who does the work:** Director calls it; player rolls; Engine adds.

**The rule**

- Dice = your Baseline score + a Pathway (skill) that fits + modifiers. Never fewer than 1 die.
- Each 1 is an Inhibitor: it scores nothing and marks 1 Stress on you.
- Flagellans (v3): each 1 marks Stress only three times in four. The Engine rolls for it; on paper, roll a d4 per 1 and ignore the Stress on a 1.
- Add up the rest. If the total reaches the Threshold, you succeed.
- Overflow is how far you beat it by. It always buys something.
- Runaway Catalysis: no 1s and at least two 6s. Your effect counts twice, with a bonus.
- Unopposed and unhurried, you succeed without rolling if your dice × 3 would reach the Threshold.
- Contests: both roll, higher wins, ties to the defender. Attacks are never contests.

**At the table.** Director: "That is Diffusion, with Concealment: six dice against twelve. If you miss, the drone asks for your claim in front of the whole queue." Player rolls 4, 1, 5, 6, 2, 3: "Twenty." Director: "The 1 costs you a point of Stress. Twenty beats twelve by eight: you are through, and nobody saw which way you went."

## 3 · The five Baselines

*Core 1*

**In plain words.** Your five core scores. The Director picks one by how you are doing the thing, not what the thing is.

**You use this when:** Any roll.

**Who does the work:** Director picks.

**The rule**

- VSC, Viscosity: force and toughness. Push, lift, hold, endure, resist poison and pressure.
- DFF, Diffusion: speed and agility. Dodge, sneak, balance, chase.
- RTC, Reactivity: chemistry and craft. Make, mend, heal, appraise, decode.
- OSM, Osmosis: senses and instinct. Notice, read a mood, resist a trick.
- AFF, Affinity: presence. Persuade, calm, command, bond.

**At the table.** Player: "I get past the guard." Director: "How?" Player: "I talk to him about his shift until he stops looking at my bag." Director: "That is Affinity."

## 4 · How hard is it? The Threshold ladder

*Core 1*

**In plain words.** The number your dice must reach. The Director picks a rung by name.

**You use this when:** Calling any roll that has no printed number.

**Who does the work:** Director picks the rung.

**The rule**

- 8 Common: anyone trained can do this under pressure.
- 12 Standard: a competent specialist manages more often than not.
- 16 Severe: needs real mastery, good tools or a plan.
- 20 Extreme: colony-wide effort.
- 24 Nucleoid and 28 Apotheotic: legend.
- A good plan, the right tool or a friend's help adds dice; a bad position removes them. Modifiers never go beyond +5 or −5 dice.

**At the table.** Director: "Climbing it is ordinary, twelve. Climbing it quietly with a body on your back is hard: sixteen."

## 5 · Overflow: what beating the number buys

*Core 1.4*

**In plain words.** Extra success is never wasted.

**You use this when:** After any successful roll.

**Who does the work:** Engine, except in talk, where the Director decides.

**The rule**

- An attack: extra damage, one for one.
- Movement or a chase: extra distance.
- Making or mending: one grade better per 4 Overflow.
- Talking: one concession.
- Sneaking: harder to notice, 1 per 2 Overflow.
- Repair: extra health restored, one for one.

**At the table.** Director: "You beat it by nine. He does not just let you in, he tells you which door the foreman uses."

## 6 · When a roll fails

*Core 1.9*

**In plain words.** Failing changes the situation. It never just stops the story.

**You use this when:** Any failed roll.

**Who does the work:** Director chooses, and should have said the cost before the roll.

**The rule**

- A cost: it works, at a price.
- A turn: it fails and the situation moves.
- A complication: it works, for the wrong party.
- Costs to pick from: time, noise, harm, position, gear, a Clock, a debt, attention.

**At the table.** Director: "You get the panel open, but it took too long. You can hear the escort coming back."

## 7 · Your body: MI, ATP and Stress

*Core 5*

**In plain words.** MI is your health. ATP is your energy for special abilities. Stress is strain, and too much makes everything harder.

**You use this when:** Taking harm, using abilities, pushing your luck.

**Who does the work:** Engine tracks all three.

**The rule**

- MI (Membrane Integrity): at 0 you go Dormant.
- ATP: spent on Bursts and on Overdrive. Overdrive adds dice to one roll: 4 ATP for +2, or 4 MI for +4 and a Stress.
- Stress 3, 5 and 6: −1, −2 and −3 dice on everything.
- Stress 7: your next failed roll triggers a Cascade (a random mishap).
- Stress 8: Autophagic Collapse. Your body starts eating itself: 2 Countdown boxes, and Stress drops to 5.
- Clearing Stress: a quiet Cycle clears 1; a Vent clears 2; a proper rest clears all.

**At the table.** Director: "That is your fifth point of Stress. You are down two dice on everything until you Vent or rest. You can feel your hands shaking."

## 8 · Going down, and dying

*Core 5.4–5.6*

**In plain words.** At 0 health you are knocked out (Dormant), and a countdown starts. Friends can save you. When the countdown fills, you are gone.

**You use this when:** Anyone hits 0 MI.

**Who does the work:** Engine tracks the countdown; players act to save.

**The rule**

- Dormant: you cannot act.
- Each of your turns while Dormant marks 1 Countdown box.
- A single hit that passes your remaining MI by 8 or more is a Breach: Dormant at once, and 1 box.
- Stabilise: a friend next to you spends a Synthesise action: RTC + Synthesis against 12 + your Level. Success brings you back up and closes 1 box.
- When the boxes are full: Total Lysis. Death.
- A full rest (a Dormant Cycle, six safe hours) clears the boxes.

**At the table.** Director: "Vash is down. That is one box of five. Ilm, you are next to him: do you want to try to bring him back?"

## 9 · The Pulse War: how a fight runs

*Core 4.1–4.2*

**In plain words.** A fight goes in rounds called Cycles. On your turn you spend Pulse Points (PP) on whatever you want to do.

**You use this when:** Violence starts.

**Who does the work:** Engine sets the order and counts points; players choose.

**The rule**

- Everyone rolls for order (Frequency). Highest goes first.
- On your turn you get your Pulse Points and spend them. Unspent points are lost.
- A Cycle is about six seconds. When everyone has acted, a new Cycle starts.
- You can still try anything: shove someone into the current, cut a rope, shout an offer. Say it and the Director rules it.
- You can Yield: a surrender that cannot be faked. Killing someone who has yielded marks 2 Stress on every witness and costs the group 1 Cohesion.
- A fight ends when one side is down, has fled, has yielded, or nobody wants it any more.

**At the table.** Director: "Brost, you are first, with six points. The nearest swarm is three Drifts away and the gate drone still has not moved. What do you do?"

## 10 · What you can do on your turn

*Core 4.3–4.11*

**In plain words.** A menu of common actions and what they cost. It is not the limit of what you may try.

**You use this when:** Your turn in a fight.

**Who does the work:** Player chooses; Engine charges.

**The rule**

- Drift, 1 PP: move, or stand, draw, pass something, break a hold.
- Sense, 1 PP: look for hidden things.
- Vent, 1 PP and 2 ATP: clear 2 Stress.
- Strike, 2 PP (3 with a heavy weapon): one attack.
- Synthesise, 2 PP: use a Vial, patch a wound, pull out a lodged weapon, stabilise a friend, harvest, set a trap.
- Burst, 3–5 PP and the same in ATP: a special ability.
- Reserve, 1 PP: hold a point back for one reaction this Cycle.
- Reactions (one per Cycle): Deflect (+4 to be hit), Brace (halve a push or crush), Inhibit (spoil an attacker's roll), Shed (drop a condition), Flank Breach (a free Strike on someone leaving your reach).

**At the table.** Player: "I move up, hit it once, and keep one point back in case it comes for Ilm." Director: "That is a Drift, a Strike and a Reserve: four points. Roll the Strike."

## 11 · Attacks and damage

*Core 4.8, 5.2*

**In plain words.** Roll against how hard the target is to hit. The better you roll, the more it hurts.

**You use this when:** Any Strike.

**Who does the work:** Engine does all of it.

**The rule**

- You hit if your total reaches the target's Membrane Threshold (MT).
- Damage = Overflow + your weapon's damage − the target's Soak (armour). Minimum 1.
- Each damage type adds a side effect. Kinetic pushes or knocks off balance. Lytic eats armour. Osmotic swells or shrinks and can sweep you away. Thermic makes the ground dangerous. Voltaic locks abilities. Genetic hijacks.
- Swarms have Segments instead of health; a good hit removes several.

**At the table.** Director: "Twenty-two against its thirteen. The flail folds it in half and the blow throws it back a Drift."

## 12 · Distance: bands and Drifts

*Core 4.4*

**In plain words.** How far apart things are, in five rough ranges. A Drift is one step.

**You use this when:** Positioning in a fight or a chase.

**Who does the work:** Engine tracks.

**The rule**

- Contact: touching.
- Near: 1–3 Drifts. A short lash or a throw.
- Mid: 4–9 Drifts. A spray or a lunge.
- Far: 10–20 Drifts. Murky water cuts this to Near.
- Loose: beyond that. Only currents and scents reach.

**At the table.** Director: "It is at Mid: too far to hit this turn unless you spend two Drifts closing."

## 13 · Conditions

*Core 4.7*

**In plain words.** Lasting effects on a body. The Director describes them; the Engine applies the numbers.

**You use this when:** An attack, hazard or ability says so.

**Who does the work:** Engine applies; Director describes.

**The rule**

- Jostled: knocked off your line.
- Leaking: losing yourself into the water each turn.
- Brittle: armour crazed like old glass.
- Turgid or Flaccid: swollen tight, or gone slack.
- Marked: lit up for every attacker; you cannot hide.
- Masked: hidden from scent until you strike.
- Locked: one of your abilities held shut.
- Hijacked: something else has a hand on you.
- Sequestered or Encysted: caged, or sealed in a shell.
- Each says how it ends: an action, a save, a rest.

**At the table.** Director: "Where it struck, your side has gone chalky and stiff. You are Brittle until someone patches it."

## 14 · Hazards

*Core 7.4*

**In plain words.** Dangerous places and things. Each one can be noticed first, has something that sets it off, and has a way round.

**You use this when:** The site prints one.

**Who does the work:** Director shows the tell; Engine pays the harm.

**The rule**

- Discovery: how it can be noticed, and the roll.
- Trigger: what sets it off.
- Effect: what it does.
- Counter: how to avoid, disarm or use it.
- Show the tell before it ever goes off.

**At the table.** Director: "The floor here hums, low enough to feel in your wall. A hard blow anywhere near it might wake whatever is underneath."

## 15 · Harvest

*Core 2.4, 8.3*

**In plain words.** Cutting something valuable out of a body or a place. It is how you get paid.

**You use this when:** A creature is down, or the site has something to cut.

**Who does the work:** Player rolls; Engine grades and shares.

**The rule**

- A Synthesise action and a roll, usually RTC + Synthesis, against the Tier's Threshold.
- Success yields Specimens, graded C (common), G (good), P (prime), T (treasure).
- A failed cut ruins the sample.
- The Cohort shares the cut in a picking order.
- An unstamped cut is worth nothing outside until a weighing house has graded and stamped it.
- Leaving the dead uncut is an offence, and it draws scavengers.

**At the table.** Player: "Before we go, I cut what we killed." Director: "Reactivity with Synthesis, against twelve. Take your time: nothing is rushing you."

## 16 · Oaths

*Core 11.4*

**In plain words.** A promise written into your body before a witness. It can pay for things money cannot.

**You use this when:** A player promises something that matters, or someone demands a promise.

**Who does the work:** Player swears in their own words; Engine records.

**The rule**

- Swearing takes about an hour, 10 Substrate, and a witness.
- It uses one of your Oath boxes.
- Kept: trust, and often a reward.
- Broken: 2 Countdown boxes, the witnessing faction thinks less of you, and a lasting scar.

**At the table.** Director: "The drone will take a promise instead of the toll. Say it in your own words: what do you owe, and to whom?"

## 17 · Rest and recovery

*Core 5.10*

**In plain words.** Three sizes of rest.

**You use this when:** Between dangers.

**Who does the work:** Player asks; Engine restores.

**The rule**

- A Vent (in a fight): 1 PP and 2 ATP clears 2 Stress.
- A Reconstitution (about an hour): full ATP, Stress to 0, some health back.
- A Dormant Cycle (six safe hours): full health, Countdown boxes cleared.
- Some places charge for rest: a Vector may move a Clock.

**At the table.** Director: "You can rest here for an hour. The whale will be an hour further gone when you get up."

## 18 · Chases

*Core 4.14*

**In plain words.** Running away, or running something down.

**You use this when:** Someone flees.

**Who does the work:** Engine runs it.

**The rule**

- Both sides roll Fluid Navigation against each other each round.
- The loser of each round shifts a band.
- The quarry escapes when it is two bands beyond Loose.

**At the table.** Director: "The shaft is the only way out and the count is running. Vash, you lead: roll to keep the others on your line."

## v3 rulings (November 2026)

*Made while the AI Director was built and played. The original book carries each one as a note at the end of the section named; the AI Director's Engine follows them.*

- **Growing, and moving on** (2.3 Codon; 17 Nov 2026). A Growth happens at the next Reconstitution after your Codon total crosses the threshold (Atlas §4.1). An organism carried into a Vector printed at a higher Level is owed the Codon and the Growths to reach it, and chooses them at once, without waiting for a rest (Codex §5.7).
- **Sharing out a harvest** (2.4 Specimens; 12 Nov 2026). Every Specimen a harvest yields is offered round the Cohort in a picking order kept for the whole game. The organism at the front takes it or passes, and goes to the back either way. Nobody chooses for another player.
- **Protean Folding** (3.2 Enzymatic Pathways; 17 Nov 2026). Protean Folding, granted by the Protean tradition (Protein Weaver, Turbulence Navigator), is rolled with **RTC**. This Core list printed no Baseline for it; both Phenotypes that hold it key on RTC.
- **Someone with no stat block** (4.1 Begin the Pulse War; 16 Nov 2026). A person or thing with no printed block (a clerk, a pilgrim, a bystander) never starts a Pulse War. Say the cost first, then make one roll; the consequences come from the world (a witness, a Clock, a faction), and nothing is invented for harvest.
- **An Oath has words and a witness** (11.4 Oaths; 13 and 17 Nov 2026). An Oath is sworn in the player's own words and is owed to somebody the table can see (a fold, a clerk, a crew): name the witness when it is sworn. Breaking an Oath also advances the campaign's **COLONY** Clock by one (Codex §5.2: a broken Oath disturbs a settlement).
