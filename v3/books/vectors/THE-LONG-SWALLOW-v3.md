# The Long Swallow · v3 table edition

*Levels 7 to 11. Book and Director's kit ready; not yet in the host's Vector list.*

This edition is written to be run as a conversation. Players say what they do in their own words; nothing below is a list of permitted moves. All prices, statistics and procedures are in the original Vector and are unchanged.

## The pitch (read this to the players)

There is a living person so vast that people have built a shanty town in its wound. In six days the authorities will legally dissolve it for parts, because something unregistered is inside it. The person, the Unread, has asked for you: it swallowed something long ago that it refuses to digest, holding it has kept it alive far too long, and it wants you to go in and cut it out so it can finally stop. You will be walking around inside someone who can feel everything you do. Everything valuable you take hurts it, and it will remember.

## The one hard rule

Nothing is ever done to a player's organism by force: no swallowing, dissolving or implanting unless the player chose it.

## The Clocks (always on the table)

- **LICENCE** (6). Six days until the crews open the Host and dissolve it. The thing that cannot be avoided. *Who moves it:* The Engine, one per Beat. A deal with Foreman Ock can slow it.
- **IMMUNE** (5). How much the Host's body has noticed you. Every cut, blow or harvest inside it adds one. At three someone is Marked; at five the Host can no longer open a door for you. *Who moves it:* Printed triggers only: things the body can feel.
- **CHURN** (4). The digestion timetable. Each time it fills, everything is pushed one room deeper. *Who moves it:* The Engine, on schedule.

## The truth (Director only)

- The thing in the Nodule is a person, the Undersigned: a living witness to who agreed to an ancient decision. It can be carried out as a witness or harvested as a document, not both.
- The Unread filed for its own dissolution long ago. It consents. It wants to stop.
- Five groups want the Receipt for five different reasons, and a Hegemony crew is already inside harvesting.
- Twelve lost workers are alive on the Shelf, and their own foreman's paperwork calls them salvage.
- A way out always exists. The Director says the three exits aloud whenever the Cohort feels trapped.

## Secrets, and three ways to each

Give a secret away whenever a player looks, asks or pokes in a way that could find it. The leads are suggestions, not the only doors.

**The Host will be legally dissolved in six days.**
- The Weep: The licence is read aloud on the road every day.
- The Chapel of the Unwasted: The Congregation explain it, angrily, for free.
- The Weep: The Host itself says "six", with a very long pause, the first time anyone speaks to it.

**There are always three ways out: the Host opens a door (if it likes you), ride the digestion out, or cut your way out and pay for it.**
- The Weep: The Hermit sells the outflow timetable.
- The Chapel of the Unwasted: The ferry pilots know all three.
- The Nine-Cycle Churn: Anything loose is visibly carried toward the drain.

**The digestion has been trying to finish one single object for generations and cannot. That is where the Nodule is.**
- The Chapel of the Unwasted: The pilgrims' log of the Churn.
- The Nine-Cycle Churn: Taste the water: it points a direction.
- The Nodule: The Host's immune chief, Ninth Response, will tell you: it wants the foreign thing out.

**The Nodule opens to a name.**
- The Weep: A scar left by an earlier crew, with a short record: "the Nodule answers to a name."
- The Chapel of the Unwasted: The Congregation's name-rite.
- The Shelf of Not-Finished Things: The Undersigned has unspent Oath boxes, visible to anyone who asks politely.

**The Host is a person, and it agreed to be opened. It filed for its own dissolution and the filing is still pending.**
- The Weep: The licence says "the body known as".
- The Chapel of the Unwasted: Ask it what it is. It answers, for nine seconds, and everyone goes quiet.
- The Shelf of Not-Finished Things: A ledger on the Shelf holds the Host's own filing.

**A Hegemony harvest crew under Foreman Sulk is already inside, cutting without the right signature.**
- The Chapel of the Unwasted: A fresh cut in the chapel wall made from the inside, with official tool marks.
- The Nine-Cycle Churn: Spent charge-slugs in the channel.
- The Shelf of Not-Finished Things: They are there, working.

**Twelve lost workers are alive on the Shelf. Their foreman's licence counts them as salvage.** *(optional)*
- The Chapel of the Unwasted: Numbered sacs in the inventory: one batch is a crew.
- The Shelf of Not-Finished Things: They speak.

**Every valuable thing taken from inside the Host hurts it and raises IMMUNE. Taking things closes the door behind you.**
- The Weep: The Hermit says it before selling anything.
- The Chapel of the Unwasted: The pilgrims say it as a prayer.
- The Nine-Cycle Churn: The first Sentinel says it as a warning.

## The sites

### 1 · The Weep — the wound, and the town built in it

**Open with:** A crier is reading a death warrant for somebody the size of a hill, and when you step off the road the hill says one word, slowly enough that the whole street stops: "Six."

**The question this scene answers:** How does the Cohort get inside, and what do they owe for it?

**What is here:** A tear in the Host's side as wide as a street, its edges folded into a shanty town. Water sellers, an abandoned toll booth still fining nobody, and a Hermit who knows the Host's timetable.

**Ways on:** Inward to the Chapel; Back to the Vat Road.

**Senses:** a wall that breathes; water sold by the cupful; a toll booth muttering fines to nobody; a deep slow pulse underfoot; an old scar with tool marks in it.

**People**

- **the Hermit of the Weep.** Wants something you have, never coin. Fears the licence. *Voice:* Cheerful, blunt, counts on fingers. *Offers:* The outflow timetable and four ways in. *Never:* Will not go inside.
- **the Unread (the Host).** Wants to be allowed to stop. Fears breaking its promise. *Voice:* Enormous, slow, few words with great weight. Long pauses. *Offers:* Opens doors for those it trusts. Offers its memory of a lost age. *Never:* Will not dissolve what it swore to keep.

**Things players often try** (anything else: rule it the same way)

- *Ask the Host for a way in.* No roll if polite: it asked for them. Each player chooses their own way in.
- *Buy the timetable.* A trade, no roll. The Hermit names something each of them has.
- *Read the old scar.* OSM, ordinary.
- *Deal with the thirsty scavengers.* Sealed water ends it in a sentence. Otherwise a fight.

**When a roll fails, pick a cost:** a debt to whoever let them in; a Beat; Goodwill; water.

**Dangers: show the tell before the strike**

- **thirsty scavengers.** Tell: shapes following the water sellers. Strike: they mob whoever carries water openly.

**If the table stalls:** The crier starts the warrant again. / The Hermit holds up four fingers: "Four doors. Pick." / The wall beside you opens a hand's width, and waits.

**A moment for each character:** *the one who stands in front*: The Host speaks to you first: you are nearest its size. *the one who makes, mends and reads the small print*: You can read the tool marks in the scar. *the one who moves through anything*: You could ride the outflow in backwards. *the one who notices*: You notice the toll booth is still reading people.

### 2 · The Chapel of the Unwasted — a swallowed shrine, now a ferry stop

**Open with:** A ferry of living pilgrims bumps the pew you are standing on, and its pilot says, without looking up from her counting, "Names first. True ones."

**The question this scene answers:** Will the people who live here vouch for the Cohort, and what will that cost?

**What is here:** A shrine swallowed generations ago: pews of folded tissue, a bell nobody rings, a notice board, and forty pilgrims who live here on purpose.

**Ways on:** Deeper, by ferry, to the Churn; Back to the Weep.

**Senses:** a hush like a held breath; pilgrims counting under their breath; a bell with a wrapped clapper; a fresh cut in an old wall; numbered sacs stacked like luggage.

**People**

- **the ferry pilot.** Wants a true name from each passenger. Fears the bell being rung. *Voice:* Calm, counts while she talks, never lies. *Offers:* Sponsorship so the body tolerates you; hides you once. *Never:* Will not lie about what is deeper.

**Things players often try** (anything else: rule it the same way)

- *Give a true name.* No roll. It buys sponsorship.
- *Read the log or the inventory.* No roll if welcome; otherwise OSM, ordinary.
- *Examine the fresh cut.* RTC or OSM, ordinary.
- *Ring the bell.* It is their choice. Say first that it tells the body where everyone is.

**When a roll fails, pick a cost:** a true name given away; the pilgrims' trust; the body notices; a Beat.

**Dangers: show the tell before the strike**

- **a Sentinel.** Tell: the walls flush and the pilgrims go still. Strike: it challenges whoever was loud; with the right token or an honest answer it escorts instead.

**If the table stalls:** The pilot reaches nine and starts again. / A pilgrim points at the cut: "Not ours." / One of the numbered sacs knocks from the inside.

**A moment for each character:** *the one who stands in front*: The ferry needs someone to hold it against the current. *the one who makes, mends and reads the small print*: The inventory is a ledger: you can read it. *the one who moves through anything*: Catch the sac before it floats off. *the one who notices*: Spot that the cut was made from the inside.

### 3 · The Nine-Cycle Churn — the digestion, still trying

**Open with:** The walls close like a fist and open again, and in the gap you see it: one pale lump the size of a house going round and round in a room that has been trying to finish it for longer than your family has existed.

**The question this scene answers:** How does the Cohort get through a room that is trying to digest them?

**What is here:** A channel of muscle squeezing on a timetable, sheets of enzyme, a pump the size of a flywheel, and one lump that never dissolves, turned over and over.

**Ways on:** Onward, with the flow, to the Shelf; Back, against it, to the Chapel.

**Senses:** pressure on every side, then none; a sour sting; the pump's slow thud; everything loose drifting one way; thick water that drags.

**People**

- **a Gastric Sentinel.** Wants to know whether you belong. Fears letting a foreign thing through. *Voice:* Asks one question, repeats it. *Offers:* Passes anyone who answers honestly or is sponsored. *Never:* Will not be bribed.

**Things players often try** (anything else: rule it the same way)

- *Jam the pump.* RTC with Synthesis, hard, and it costs supplies. The room stops: safe, but stuck.
- *Turn the flow.* DFF with Fluid Navigation, hard. It carries them the wrong way once.
- *Ride it out.* No roll: they arrive at the Shelf and take the printed harm at the squeeze.
- *Answer the Sentinel.* The printed challenge; an honest answer or sponsorship passes.

**When a roll fails, pick a cost:** pushed a room deeper; the body notices; gear swept away; separated from the others.

**Dangers: show the tell before the strike**

- **the squeeze.** Tell: the thud speeds up and the walls flush dark. Strike: everyone is thrown toward the Shelf.
- **enzyme sheets.** Tell: a shimmer drifting like a curtain. Strike: it burns what it touches.

**If the table stalls:** The thud quickens. / A Sentinel drifts closer and asks its question. / A spent charge-slug floats past: someone else came this way.

**A moment for each character:** *the one who stands in front*: Brace and hold the others through a squeeze. *the one who makes, mends and reads the small print*: The pump is a machine. You know machines. *the one who moves through anything*: This is a current, and currents are yours. *the one who notices*: Taste the water: it tells you where the lump leads.

### 4 · The Shelf of Not-Finished Things — everything it could not bring itself to end

**Open with:** Hundreds of faces turn toward you from inside the walls, a dozen of them wearing work harness, and somewhere down the row a saw stops and a tired voice says, "Whoever that is, I have a licence."

**The question this scene answers:** What does the Cohort take, who do they speak for, and what do they tell the foreman?

**What is here:** Rows of slow-tissue pods, each holding something the Host refused to dissolve: wrecks, calves, twelve lost workers, all alive and aware. A Hegemony crew is cutting pods open.

**Ways on:** Inward to the Nodule; The drain, with the Churn; Back, against the flow.

**Senses:** a crowd's quiet breathing; old promises murmuring from a wreck; the whine of a saw; stacked riches within arm's reach; being watched kindly.

**People**

- **Foreman Sulk Nine-Yards.** Wants to finish this contract and retire. Fears a charge he cannot talk his way out of. *Voice:* Tired, reasonable, counts yards. *Offers:* Stops and bargains if the Cohort can name the signature he lacks. *Never:* Not a villain. Will not fight if given a way to sit down.
- **Ock's lost crew.** Wants to be counted as people. Fears the licence. *Voice:* Twelve voices finishing each other's sentences. *Offers:* Know every pod on the Shelf.
- **the Undersigned.** Wants to be asked. Fears being used again without consent. *Voice:* Careful, formal, still under oath. *Offers:* Its own spoken consent opens the Nodule. *Never:* Will not lie.

**Things players often try** (anything else: rule it the same way)

- *Talk to the crowd.* OSM or AFF, ordinary; hard if the Cohort has been taking things.
- *Harvest the Shelf.* It is the richest haul there is. Say the price first: each cut hurts the Host and raises IMMUNE.
- *Challenge Sulk's licence.* AFF or RTC, ordinary. Success and he sits down to talk.
- *Ask the Undersigned.* No roll. It answers honestly.

**When a roll fails, pick a cost:** Goodwill; the body notices; the crowd turns away; Sulk goes back to cutting.

**Dangers: show the tell before the strike**

- **Sulk's crew.** Tell: the saw, and "I have a licence". Strike: they defend the contract.
- **the licence.** Tell: the crowd counts the days aloud. Strike: this room is dissolved first.

**If the table stalls:** One of the twelve says a name the Cohort knows. / Sulk walks over with a clipboard. / A pod nearby is cut open, and the Host flinches.

**A moment for each character:** *the one who stands in front*: Put yourself between a saw and a pod. *the one who makes, mends and reads the small print*: You know exactly which signature Sulk is missing. *the one who moves through anything*: Reach the pod before the saw does. *the one who notices*: You see the Oath boxes on the one at the centre.

### 5 · The Nodule — the promise, and its keeper

**Open with:** The guardian in front of the sealed door does not raise a weapon. It bows, and says: "You want it out. So do I. Shall we discuss how?"

**The question this scene answers:** Does the Receipt leave as a witness, a document, or not at all, and does the Host get to stop?

**What is here:** A sealed knot of tissue the size of a chapel, untouched by digestion. The Host's own immune authority stands before it.

**Ways on:** Whichever of the three exits the Cohort has earned.

**Senses:** stillness after all that motion; clean tissue, never burned; a door with no handle; the Host's pulse, slower here; somebody inside, listening.

**People**

- **Ninth Response.** Wants the foreign thing out of the body. Fears harm to the Host. *Voice:* Courteous, exact, speaks for the body. *Offers:* A route and a safe exit if the thing leaves. *Never:* Will not let the Host be hurt for profit.

**Things players often try** (anything else: rule it the same way)

- *Say the name.* No roll if they have it. The door opens.
- *Ask the Host to unseal it.* Needs the Host's trust (Client or Kin). No roll.
- *Cut it open.* RTC with Synthesis, extreme, or acid at a price. Say first: loud, and the body will know.
- *Bargain with Ninth Response.* It opens by talking. AFF, ordinary.

**When a roll fails, pick a cost:** the body's recognition; the Host's trust; a Beat; a promise to carry the Undersigned somewhere.

**Dangers: show the tell before the strike**

- **Ninth Response and the Sentinels.** Tell: it stops bowing. Strike: the whole body turns on whoever cut.

**If the table stalls:** Ninth Response repeats its offer. / A voice from inside the door asks who is there. / The Host says a name, slowly.

**A moment for each character:** *the one who stands in front*: Carry the Undersigned out. *the one who makes, mends and reads the small print*: Read the seal: it is a contract. *the one who moves through anything*: Lead the way out before the squeeze. *the one who notices*: Hear the voice inside before anyone else.

## How it can end, and what comes next

- If the Undersigned is carried out as a witness: the oldest decision in the world goes on trial, and the Cohort is why.
- If it is printed: the Cohort is rich and owned.
- If the Host is left to the licence: the richest harvest in the world, and everyone knows who stood by.
- The best ending is one the players invent, such as registering the Undersigned as a citizen.
