# The First Carve · v3 table edition

*For two to six new organisms, Level 1. Ready to run in the v3 host.*

This edition is written to be run as a conversation. Players say what they do in their own words; nothing below is a list of permitted moves. All prices, statistics and procedures are in the original Vector and are unchanged.

## The pitch (read this to the players)

A whale the size of a town has died across a river channel, and everyone wants a piece before it rots. You are a small crew with one day to get in, cut something valuable, get it weighed and stamped so it is legally yours, and get out. Three things are against you: the whale is going bad (CARCASS), the authorities are going to blow it up on a schedule (TOLL), and every hungry thing for miles can smell it (HUNGER). And something inside this whale is not whale.

## The Clocks (always on the table)

- **CARCASS** (6). The whale is digesting itself. The value clock.
- **TOLL** (5). The Render Charge is on a schedule. The law clock.
- **HUNGER** (4). Everything in the Reach can smell a wound. The danger clock.

## The truth (Director only)

- The whale did not die by accident. It was herding something into the Reach, and its gut locked around it.
- The "something" is a canister of ancient Lattice machinery. It is still running a print job: a brood of not-whales.
- Nobody here is a villain. The drones want the brood made legal. The Hegemony team wants its canister back. The brood wants to live.

## Secrets, and three ways to each

Give a secret away whenever a player looks, asks or pokes in a way that could find it. The leads are suggestions, not the only doors.

**A cut is worth nothing unless a Tally Post has weighed and stamped it.**
- The Toll-Vent: The toll board prints it under the prices.
- The Soft Field: The clerk says it before anything else.
- The Toll-Vent: A skiff crew in the queue is turned away with an unstamped load.

**A crew came before you. Their claim was voided and they did not come out. The ledger says CLAIM VOID - SEE NETH.**
- The Soft Field: The last entry in the post's ledger.
- The Soft Field: A tally-sled on the cable, loaded with tools and nothing else.
- The Render Hall: Four bodies in the still, wearing harvest gear.

**The drones are not guarding the whale from thieves. They are guarding something inside it.**
- The Toll-Vent: The customs gate faces inward.
- The Soft Field: A drone escort is moving into the whale, not out.
- The Brood of the Not-Whale: Nine-Folds has set up a second customs post at the nursery gate.

**A warm, sweet current runs up from deep in the gut. Dead whales are not warm or sweet.**
- The Soft Field: Anyone who tastes the water notices it.
- The Render Hall: One plug in the stomach wall is warm; the current starts there.
- The Brood of the Not-Whale: The nursery is where it comes from.

**The Gut Stone, the richest thing in the whale, sits in the far curve of the gut past the stomach.**
- The Render Hall: It can be seen from the still.
- The Render Hall: Pump-Hand Osk will say where the hose is headed, if helped.
- The Brood of the Not-Whale: Nine-Folds' forms name it as the "source parcel".

**The brood are not whale young. Their chemistry matches the warm slag, not the whale. Something printed them.**
- The Brood of the Not-Whale: A careful smell or taste (the Eyes is best at this).
- The Brood of the Not-Whale: Nine-Folds will say it in exchange for a registration signature.
- The Gut Stone: The canister in the stone is still warm and still working.

**The Hegemony will fire a Render Charge to destroy the carcass when the TOLL clock fills. It cannot be survived, only outrun.**
- The Toll-Vent: The toll board lists a "clearance schedule".
- The Render Hall: The pump crew are counting down out loud.
- The Gut Stone: The Warden says so, as a courtesy, before anything else.

**The canister carries a serial number in a format older than any living lineage. The Hegemony will pay for it to be reported.** *(optional)*
- The Gut Stone: Reading the stone face up close.
- The Gut Stone: The Warden asks whether anyone has read it.

**Neth is a Guild fixer who voids honest claims and resells them. Tallow Ring sold this whale twice.** *(optional)*
- The Soft Field: The clerk, if pressed or paid.
- The Toll-Vent: Gossip in the skiff queue.

## The sites

### 1 · The Toll-Vent — the wound mouth

**Open with:** You are third in a queue of skiffs at a wound the size of a town square, and the queue has just stopped: a small folded official has turned to look straight at you.

**The question this scene answers:** How does the Cohort get in: pay, promise, talk, sneak or fight?

**What is here:** A torn flank panel the size of a town square, with the channel's current running through it. Folded out of the whale's own gristle is a Chaperone Drone customs post: three Drifts of folded membrane, a chitin gate, a queue of drones, and a toll board with prices in Specimens.

**Ways on:** Inward: the cut-lane down to the Soft Field; Back out to the Toll Reach and its queue of skiffs.

**Senses:** the current pulling in through the torn flank; a taste of old blood and salt; the toll board ticking as prices change; bare patches on the wound lip where something has been grazing; a low hum under the lip, felt more than heard.

**People**

- **the gate drone (a Fold Sentinel).** Wants to see a valid claim before anyone passes. Fears being blamed for an unregistered entry. *Voice:* Clipped, formal, reads from a form. Never raises its voice. *Offers:* Accepts a toll, a licence, or a witnessed promise. *Never:* Will not start a fight. Will not be bribed, but will be persuaded.
- **a skiff crew ahead of you.** Wants to get their load stamped and go home. Fears the schedule. *Voice:* Tired, chatty, bitter about the Guild. *Offers:* Gossip for a kind word or a drink.

**Things players often try** (anything else: rule it the same way)

- *Pay the toll.* No roll. It is printed: Specimens or Substrate per body. TOLL eases.
- *Promise instead of paying.* An Oath, witnessed by the drone. No roll to swear it; the cost comes later.
- *Talk their way through.* A roll on AFF, ordinary difficulty. Failing costs time or a worse price, never a locked door.
- *Slip past.* A roll on DFF with Concealment, hard. Failing means being seen, and the drone asks for the claim in front of everyone.
- *Fight through.* It is their choice. Say first that it is loud and the law will notice, then run the Pulse War.

**When a roll fails, pick a cost:** time (the queue grows restless); a worse price; being noticed by the drones; the swarms come for whoever is wet and bleeding.

**Dangers: show the tell before the strike**

- **the gas pocket under the lip.** Tell: the floor hums; a careful organism can feel it. Strike: any hard blow sets it off.
- **the Gnaw Swarms.** Tell: bare grazed patches; a shimmer at the edge of the wound. Strike: they come for anything thrashing.

**If the table stalls:** The drone repeats its question, more slowly, and holds out a form. / The skiff behind bumps yours: "Pay it or move." / The toll board ticks to a higher price.

**A moment for each character:** *The Wall*: Your name is on the manifest: the drone knows it and says so. *The Ledger*: You can read the toll board's small print faster than anyone. *The Rider*: You know the current at this wound: you rode this whale. *The Eyes*: You are the first to feel the hum under the lip.

**Printed in the original (unchanged):** Vector §8.1. Danger: 2 Gnaw Swarms + 1 Fold Sentinel = 6 BSP. The Fold Sentinel does not start the fight: it demands the Cohort show its claim. If the Cohort has paid or sworn, it stands aside even after a strike. Pays: The toll chit (TOLL -1), a legal route in, the Specimens printed in the Bestiary for each harvested swarm (C3 / G1 with the harvest tool; 1.5 Codon each), and 15 Codon for getting inside without making an enemy of Order II.

### 2 · The Soft Field — the licensed cut-ground, and the Tally Post

**Open with:** The cut-lane opens into a hanging forest of pale fat, and somebody has been here: the lanes are marked, a sled waits on its cable, and the scale at the Tally Post is still swinging.

**The question this scene answers:** Does the Cohort get a cut, and can they make it legal?

**What is here:** The good part of the whale: hanging sheets of lipid and connective tissue, cut lanes marked with Guild claim-line pheromone, and tally-sleds on a cable line. At the centre stands the Tally Post, where a cut is weighed, graded and stamped. Tools are stacked, unblooded. The sleds are empty.

**Ways on:** Onward: the cut-lane to the stomach (the Render Hall); Out: the sled-cable back to the surface; Back: up the cut-lane to the Toll-Vent.

**Senses:** sheets of fat swaying like curtains; a sweet taste in the water that does not belong; the creak of the sled cable; a glassy shimmer in one panel (the Medusa nest); soft ground that sucks at you.

**People**

- **the Tally Post clerk.** Wants a quiet shift and a clean ledger. Fears Neth, and being caught between two claims. *Voice:* Dry, exact, quotes prices. Warms up if treated as a person. *Offers:* A stamp at the price the post rolls. Gossip costs extra. *Never:* Will not stamp something that has not been on the scale.

**Things players often try** (anything else: rule it the same way)

- *Cut the far panels and avoid the nest.* The harvest rule, as printed. No fight.
- *Drive the Medusa off first.* A Pulse War, or a clever trick: clouding the water blinds them.
- *Read the ledger.* No roll if the clerk allows it; otherwise OSM or AFF, ordinary.
- *Haggle with the clerk.* AFF, ordinary. Failure fixes the price where it is.

**When a roll fails, pick a cost:** the whale goes further off (CARCASS); a spoiled cut, one grade lower; the nest wakes; a debt to the clerk.

**Dangers: show the tell before the strike**

- **the Glaze Medusa nest.** Tell: a glassy shimmer, and a panel nobody has cut. Strike: they glaze whoever comes close.
- **slough patches.** Tell: ground that looks wet and slack. Strike: it slows and, later, burns.

**If the table stalls:** The clerk clears its throat: "Are you cutting or looking?" / A drone escort drifts past, heading deeper in. / The sweet current gets stronger from the far lane.

**A moment for each character:** *The Wall*: Stand between the nest and whoever is cutting. *The Ledger*: This is your room: the cut and the price are yours to get right. *The Rider*: Cross on the hanging panels where nobody else can. *The Eyes*: Spot the nest, and the sled loaded with nothing but tools.

**Printed in the original (unchanged):** Vector §8.2, §7.2. Danger: 2 Glaze Medusa + 2 Hijack Mites = 6 BSP. Add 2 more Mites (8 BSP) once the Cohort has got Lesson 4. The Medusa are nesting in a hanging panel: a Cohort that backs off and takes the far panels gets the same harvest with less blood. Pays: The harvest proper: loose tissue is Tier 1, so the check is AT 12 and the grade depends on CARCASS (§7.2). A stamped cut is 40 to 120 S, only if the claim stands. The first crew's salt cache (60 S of salt = a CARCASS rewind). 20 Codon for getting a cut stamped rather than merely taken.

### 3 · The Render Hall — the stomach, still running

**Open with:** The stomach is still working. A pump barge is roaring at the near side, nine crew are shouting over it, and as you arrive one of them loses their footing at the edge of the churn.

**The question this scene answers:** Can the Cohort cross the stomach, and what do they do about the stranger in the water?

**What is here:** The stomach is still warm, and it is churning. The fluid here is hypertonic: it pulls at you. A Hegemony pump barge and its crew of nine are running a hose, draining fluid. Dry ribs run along the stomach wall. At the bottom is the still, hot and quiet.

**Ways on:** Onward: the pyloric channels (warm, sweet, going up); Out: the pump shaft; Back: the cut-lane to the Soft Field.

**Senses:** a pull on your skin from the too-salty water; the pump's roar, felt in the body; a sheen on the haze over the middle of the floor; dry ribs along the wall like a walkway; one warm spot in a cold room.

**People**

- **Pump-Hand Osk.** Wants to get out alive and finish the shift. Fears the churn. *Voice:* Young, loud, swears cheerfully, grateful. *Offers:* Owes a life: tells everything, lends the pump.
- **the barge foreman.** Wants the chamber drained on schedule. Fears losing a hand on her watch. *Voice:* Flat, practical, counts things. *Offers:* Lets the Cohort use the dry ribs or the pump in return for help. *Never:* Will not stop the pump for strangers.

**Things players often try** (anything else: rule it the same way)

- *Walk the dry ribs.* No roll to walk. Safe from the haze. Slower.
- *Cross through the middle.* The haze is a printed hazard. Say the sheen first.
- *Save Osk.* The stabilising roll as printed (RTC with Synthesis).
- *Take over or open the pump.* Talk the foreman round (AFF) or seize it. Printed prices: loud, but it drains the room.

**When a roll fails, pick a cost:** dragged a band by the churn; noise (the hungry things hear it); armour eaten by the haze; Osk gets worse.

**Dangers: show the tell before the strike**

- **the Enzyme Field.** Tell: a sheen on the haze over the mid floor. Strike: it burns whoever enters or fights inside it.
- **the Bleeders.** Tell: ribbons of red moving against the current. Strike: they latch on and drink.
- **the churn.** Tell: loose things spiral toward the middle. Strike: it drags you a band each Cycle.

**If the table stalls:** Osk shouts for help. / The foreman points at the ribs: "That way if you want to keep your skin." / From the still, something large and pale shows in the far curve: the stone.

**A moment for each character:** *The Wall*: Anchor the line that pulls Osk out. *The Ledger*: You are the one who can stabilise a dying stranger. *The Rider*: The churn is a current, and currents are yours. *The Eyes*: See the sheen before anyone steps into it.

**Printed in the original (unchanged):** Vector §8.3. Danger: 3 Cytoplasmic Bleeders + 2 Detritus Feeders = 8 BSP, plus the Enzyme Field and the churn. The Bleeders attach (break with a contested VSC, 1 PP) and drink. The Feeders Render any corpse in the water. Pays: The first hard fact (the Gut Stone's location, visible from the still). The first crew's four bodies, worth a read, not a haul. 20 Codon for stabilising a stranger and getting the fact in the same room.

### 4 · The Brood of the Not-Whale — the cost room

**Open with:** The water turns warm and bright. Hundreds of small blind shapes turn toward you all at once, and a very old drone at a desk made of folded membrane says, without looking up, "Purpose of visit?"

**The question this scene answers:** What does the Cohort do about the brood: register it, harvest it, or walk past?

**What is here:** A chamber that should not exist in a dead animal: warm, lit and sweet. Three hundred or more blind juveniles swim here, whale-shaped but wrong, with six fins where a Drifter has four. They cluster round a warm shard of slag. The room feels like being asked questions. At the chamber's gate a Chaperone Drone elder has set up a customs post, with forms and toll receipts.

**Ways on:** Onward: the pyloric duct to the Gut Stone; Back: out the way you came.

**Senses:** warmth, like a hand held near; sweetness thick enough to taste; the feeling of being asked questions; six fins where there should be four; the scratch of a stylus on a form.

**People**

- **Nine-Folds, the drone elder.** Wants the brood registered as persons before the schedule runs out. Fears that they will be rendered as salvage. *Voice:* Ancient, patient, bureaucratic, quietly desperate. Speaks in form numbers, then stops and speaks plainly. *Offers:* Tells the truth for a signature. Offers a Name and the drones' goodwill. *Never:* Will not threaten. Will not lie on a form.
- **the brood (through a Receptor Lurker).** Wants to be let live. Fears everything. *Voice:* Repeats your own words back as questions. *Offers:* Nothing to trade but trust. *Never:* Will not fight unless startled.

**Things players often try** (anything else: rule it the same way)

- *Talk to Nine-Folds.* Three exchanges. Say what success buys before the first roll.
- *Sign as witness.* No roll. It is a choice with a printed reward, and it binds.
- *Harvest the brood.* It is allowed and it pays well. Say the price plainly first: the Clocks move and the drones remember.
- *Work out what they are.* OSM with Chemoreception, ordinary. On a failure they still learn it, from Nine-Folds, at a price.

**When a roll fails, pick a cost:** the brood startles; Nine-Folds closes the desk; time on the schedule; a promise asked for in return.

**Dangers: show the tell before the strike**

- **a startled brood.** Tell: the shapes bunch and the sweetness sours. Strike: the Lurkers and the shoal defend the nursery.

**If the table stalls:** Nine-Folds slides a form across the desk and waits. / One juvenile bumps gently against the nearest organism. / A distant thud from above: the pump crew have started the count.

**A moment for each character:** *The Wall*: An escort's signature carries weight: yours is the name Nine-Folds wants. *The Ledger*: You know exactly what this brood is worth, both ways. *The Rider*: You saw the whale herd something. Now you know what. *The Eyes*: You can smell that they are not whale.

**Printed in the original (unchanged):** Vector §8.4. Danger: Only if the Cohort startles the brood: 2 Receptor Lurkers + 3 Drifter Shoal = 7 BSP. A Lurker reads you for free, then announces what it intends to do. Its Camouflage reads as neutral to Chemoreception at AT 14. Pays: 25 Codon for learning what the not-whale actually is; a G Specimen; the Choir's goodwill; and, if the brood is registered, a Name. If harvested instead: 400 to 900 S, HUNGER +2, TOLL +1. Both are legitimate play: charge for both.

### 5 · The Gut Stone — the yield, the owner, and the schedule

**Open with:** The duct opens on a boulder of fused slag as big as a house, warm, humming, ringed with broken glass. On the far side a Hegemony officer in heavy plate raises one hand, politely, and says: "That is ours. You have until the charge."

**The question this scene answers:** Who leaves with what, and does everyone get out before the charge?

**What is here:** A plug of fused slag, gut-crud and salt, 30 Drifts across, wedged in the curve of the gut. It is warm. Around it lies a field of shards, everything cutting loose, and an acid halo where something inside leaks. One shaft leads up.

**Ways on:** Out: the exit shaft up to the pump; Back: the pyloric duct to the Brood.

**Senses:** heat off the stone face; a sting in the water near the stone; shards chiming against each other in the current; a hum with a rhythm, like something working; one shaft of cold water from above: the way out.

**People**

- **the Protease Warden.** Wants the canister recovered intact and reported. Fears losing it to scavengers, or to the charge. *Voice:* Courteous, tired, absolutely certain. Gives one warning. *Offers:* Pays for the serial to be reported. Lets the Cohort leave with ordinary cuts. *Never:* Will not give up the canister.
- **the Lattice Guardian (in the stone).** Wants the print job finished. Fears interruption. *Voice:* Does not speak. Answers yes or no by warming or cooling the water. *Offers:* Goes quiet if convinced the job is complete. *Never:* Cannot leave the stone.

**Things players often try** (anything else: rule it the same way)

- *Deal with the Warden.* AFF, hard, easier if the Cohort has the brood's registration or the serial.
- *Read the stone.* OSM or RTC, ordinary, but it means standing in the sting.
- *Take the canister.* Say it out loud first: this is a deadly fight. Then it is their choice.
- *Run for the shaft.* The chase rule as printed.

**When a roll fails, pick a cost:** cut by the shards; the Warden's patience; a segment of the schedule; the stone stings whoever is touching it.

**Dangers: show the tell before the strike**

- **the Warden's team.** Tell: the Warden marks whoever comes closest, and says so. Strike: everything it has goes at the marked one.
- **the Render Charge.** Tell: a count, heard through the walls, getting shorter. Strike: the carcass ends; it is outrun, never survived.

**If the table stalls:** The Warden says the count out loud. / The stone warms, and something inside it turns over. / Cold water from the shaft: the way out is still open.

**A moment for each character:** *The Wall*: Hold the shaft mouth while the others go up. *The Ledger*: Name the price of the serial to the Warden's face. *The Rider*: The race up the shaft is yours to lead. *The Eyes*: Read the serial. This is what you were paid to bring back.

**Printed in the original (unchanged):** Vector §8.5. Danger: E5, Deadly: 1 Protease Warden + 2 Cytoplasmic Bleeders + 4 Antigen Gnats = 12 BSP, against a Deadly budget of 13 for a Cohort of four. Pays: The recovery crew will pay 200 to 400 S for a Cohort willing to carry the serial into a Hegemony office and say it out loud.

## v3 rulings (November 2026)

*Made while the AI Director was built and played. The original book carries each one as a note at the end of the section named; the AI Director's Engine follows them.*

- **TOLL and a loud fight** (3.2 TOLL; 13 and 16 Nov 2026). A loud fight reaches the law only half the time: roll a d6, and TOLL advances on 4–6 (HUNGER is paid every time). Once TOLL stands at 3 or more, the Director no longer spends their own discretionary advance on it; only the printed triggers move it from there.
- **Opening a panel** (3.1 CARCASS; 13 Nov 2026). Opening a panel is one event the site pays: the first panel opened in a scene advances CARCASS, and further panels in the same scene do not (the host may set every panel to pay its own segment).
- **A body left uncut** (3.3 HUNGER; 17 Nov 2026). A body left uncut in the water when the Cohort leaves the site advances HUNGER and, as this section prints, the campaign's COLONY Clock.
- **A Beat in the carcass** (3.1 CARCASS; 18 Nov 2026). A cut stamped at a Tally Post is a cut signed. TOLL advances every Beat; CARCASS advances only on a Beat when no cut was stamped and no haul shipped. If about ten exchanges at the table pass with no Beat, the day has run out: close it (Codex §5.1).
- **Salt, priced** (3.1 CARCASS; 18 Nov 2026). Salt costs 10 S per 2 Drifts. A salted panel rewinds CARCASS and a salted wound rewinds HUNGER, each **once a scene**, so a sack of salt is a choice, not a way to empty the Clocks. Salt the Cohort finds (the first crew's cache) costs nothing.
- **A stamped cut is safe** (3.1 CARCASS; 18 Nov 2026). When the whale goes over (CARCASS 6) and in the Render Cascade (HUNGER 4), a cut stamped at a Tally Post counts as shipped: only unstamped cuts are lost or drop a grade. When the whale goes over, HUNGER jumps to 3 and the ORDER Clock opens at 2, as printed.
- **Paper pays once** (3.2 TOLL; 18 Nov 2026). Each kind of paper (a valid claim, a licence, a toll chit, an Ash witness, a Guild tally receipt) rewinds TOLL once in a Vector, and the Gut Stone's serial once more. Shipping a stamped haul earns its own tally receipt: TOLL −1, once a scene.

## How it can end, and what comes next

- If the brood was registered: three hundred new citizens owe the Cohort a Name, and somebody will want that undone.
- If the serial was reported: a Hegemony office now knows the Cohort by sight.
- If Neth was crossed: the Guild has a file.
- Whoever paid the Eyes in advance is waiting in Tallow Ring.

## Words

| Word | In plain words |
|---|---|
| Director | The one who runs the game and plays the world (the "dungeon master"). |
| Cohort | Your group. The party. |
| Organism | Your character. Everyone here is a single cell the size of a person. |
| Hand | A ready-made character you can pick up and play: the Wall, the Ledger, the Rider, the Eyes. |
| Vector | One adventure. |
| Site | One place in the adventure. A scene. |
| MI | Membrane Integrity. Your health. At zero your wall is breached. |
| ATP | Your energy. Spend it on special abilities (Bursts). |
| Stress | Strain you build up, mostly from rolling 1s. Too much makes every roll harder. |
| Breach | Your wall has been broken open (MI at zero). You are in real trouble. |
| Dormancy | Knocked out. You curl up and stop. |
| Lysis | Death: the cell bursts. A Lysis Countdown is how many bad turns you have left when dying. |
| Soak | Armour. Takes this much off every hit. |
| Baseline | One of your five core scores: VSC, DFF, RTC, OSM, AFF. |
| VSC | Viscosity. Strength and toughness. |
| DFF | Diffusion. Speed and agility. |
| RTC | Reactivity. Cleverness with chemistry: making, mending, working things out. |
| OSM | Osmosis. Senses and instinct. Noticing, and not being fooled. |
| AFF | Affinity. Presence. Talking, leading, bonding. |
| Pathway | A skill. It adds dice to a roll when it fits what you are doing. |
| AT | Action Threshold. The number your dice must reach. Higher is harder. |
| MT | Membrane Threshold. How hard you are to hit. |
| Overflow | How far you beat the number by. More Overflow buys a better result. |
| Inhibitor | A die that rolled 1. It counts for nothing and gives you 1 Stress. (Flagellans take that Stress only three times in four.) |
| Flagellan | One of the six lineages: the fast, restless people of the great currents. They ride out strain: a rolled 1 costs them Stress only three times in four. |
| Pulse War | A fight, played in turns. |
| Cycle | One round of a fight: everyone acts once. |
| PP | Pulse Points. What you spend to act on your turn. Moving, striking and defending all cost some. |
| Strike | An attack. |
| Band | How far apart two things are: Contact, Near, Mid or Far. |
| Drift | One step of distance. |
| Brace | Dig in. Harder to move and to hurt until your next turn. |
| Condition | A lasting effect such as Jostled (knocked off balance) or Masked (hidden). |
| Substrate | Money. Written S. |
| Codon | Experience. What your character learns and grows from. |
| Specimen | A cut sample of something valuable. Graded C (common), G (good), P (prime), T (treasure). |
| Harvest | Cutting something valuable out of a body or a place. |
| Tally Post | A weighing house. A cut is worth nothing until a post has weighed and stamped it. |
| Oath | A promise written into your body. Keeping it earns trust; breaking it scars you. |
| Clock | A countdown everyone can see. When it fills, something big happens. |
| Tonic | How salty the water is compared with you. The wrong kind pulls at you and makes things harder. |
| Hegemony | The big government. Engineers, pumps, soldiers and paperwork. |
| Guild | The licensed traders. They decide whose claim is legal. |
| Ash | The keepers of the dead. Nothing that dies should be wasted. |
| Chaperone Drone | A small folded-membrane official. Drones run tolls, gates and forms. |
| Order II | The drones' own service. Their stamp makes a thing legal. |
| Lattice | Ancient machinery that can print living things. Nobody fully understands it. |
