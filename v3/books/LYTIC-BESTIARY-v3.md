# The Lytic Bestiary · v3 table edition

*68 creatures in the v3 format. Every statistic, trait, special, weakness and harvest line is from the Lytic Bestiary and is unchanged. What is new: each Order opens with what its creatures want and how to avoid a fight, and each creature is laid out the same way, with what it does first and its numbers last. The Lytic Bestiary remains the reference for abiotic entities and hazards, Colonists, Constructs and Relic-Bearers, and the building rules.*

## How to read a creature

Each creature has two halves.

**For running it** (what the Director needs first):

- **In a fight:** how it behaves.
- **Weak point:** what beats it without a slugging match. Players who find this out should be rewarded for it.
- **Traits and specials:** what makes it different.
- **Leaves behind:** what can be cut from the body.

**Its stat block** (the numbers the Engine uses). A stat block is a creature's character sheet in one line:

| Part | Means |
|---|---|
| Standard, Elite, Swarmling… | How big a threat it is. |
| T1, T2… | Its tier: roughly what level of Cohort it is meant for. |
| MT | Membrane Threshold: the number an attack must reach to hit it. |
| MI | Its health. Swarms have Segments instead. |
| soak | Armour: taken off every hit. |
| BD and a type | The damage its attack adds, and what kind (Kinetic, Lytic, Osmotic…). |
| pool | How many dice it rolls. |
| PP | Pulse Points: how much it can do each turn. |
| Flow | How far it moves. |

The Director never reads these numbers out. Players learn how hurt a creature is from how it looks: unhurt, scratched, hurt, badly hurt.

## People are not in this book

A clerk, a foreman or a bystander has no stat block. If a player attacks one, there is no Pulse War: the Director says what it will cost, the player makes one roll, and the world answers (see the Core Rulebook v3 and the README).

## Order I: Lytic Swarms

Hunger with a body count. They dissolve things before eating them, so they damage armour, structures and the ground. They are not evil: they are digestion that learned to move. **What they want:** to feed. **Before a fight:** look for grazed, bare patches. **Way out:** give them something else to eat or drink, or spread something across the whole area.

### Cytoplasmic Bleeder

**In a fight:** finds the slowest organism, attaches, and lets its allies do the killing.

**Weak point:** Detach is one OSM contest (AT 14) or a Thermal/Lytic burn; Phagocyte Engulfment is fatal.

**Traits:** Attach (a hit gives it 1 Barb: it rides on the target, and while Barbed it Strikes for free at −1 die each Cycle, taking Reactive Osmosis damage from the host's own efforts)

**Special: Tap (2 PP):** drains 3 MI from the host and heals itself for that amount. Costs 1 ATP the first time (it has no ATP pool; track this as a 3-use limit per fight).

**Leaves behind:** C4 / G1 / P1 · Codon 3 · BSP 2 (its Greater Organ is the Tap Valve: Lytic family, +2 soak, and a Minor Cost of hunger)

**Stat block:** Standard · T1 · Microscopic-Mesoscopic · MT 13 · MI 22 · soak 1 · BD 2 Lytic · pool 4d · PP 3 · Flow 4

### Detritus Feeder

**In a fight:** ignores the fight, eats the battlefield. In a long fight, a Feeder becomes four Feeders.

**Weak point:** if you deny it corpses (burn them, salt them, take them) it becomes a nuisance instead of a threat.

**Traits:** Acid-Immune (Lytic damage 0) · Harvest Instinct (it will spend its action on a corpse rather than on you — this is exploitable)

**Special: Render (2 PP):** on a corpse within Contact, it Harvests: the corpse's remaining Harvest value drops by one grade, and the Feeder grows: +2 Segments permanently.

**Leaves behind:** C3 / G1 · Codon 1.5 · BSP 1 (run it as Tier 2 in the Wastes and it yields C4 / G1, Codon 3.5 — which is why Reclaimer crews count the ground before they count the swarm)

**Stat block:** Swarmling · T1 (T2 in the Wastes) · MT 12 · MI 10 · soak 0 · BD 1 Lytic · pool 3d · PP 2 · Segments 6

### Gnaw Swarm

**In a fight:** flows over everything, bites once per Cycle per two segments, and cannot be frightened.

**Weak point:** area damage, Dry Zones, and any Thermal effect end Segments 1-for-1 with no Overflow needed.

**Traits:** Scent-Led (never Blind for more than one Cycle if it has smelled you) · Corrodes (any hit: target graft −1 soak until repaired)

**Special: Swarm Over (2 PP):** all live segments Strike one creature in Contact. Each Strike at −2 dice. Against a braced target this is a meat grinder; against a moving target it usually misses.

**Leaves behind:** C3 / G1 · Codon 1.5 · BSP 1 (the C is worth 15 S; a Cohort that ignores these bodies is throwing money away)

**Stat block:** Swarmling · T1 · Mesoscopic cluster · MT 12 · MI 10 · soak 0 · BD 1 Lytic · pool 3d · PP 2 · Segments 6

### Bloom Matriarch

**In a fight:** arrives, roots, and grows the fight. Every Cycle it is unaddressed, the encounter's BSP increases by 2.

**Weak point:** its brood is its clock: kill the sac, not the swarm, and it loses a phase. It cannot leave — a Cohort that breaks the ground under it (pump, flood, collapse) can drown the whole fight.

**Traits:** Brood Sac (at the end of each Cycle, spawns 1 Swarmling T2 with half Segments; the sac must be cut — a Strike at AT 16, 20 S of acid, or a Breacher's charge to stop the spawning) · Rooted (it cannot be pushed; its Folds anchor it to the ground)

**Special: Bloom Surge (5 PP, once per phase):** all its Swarmlings act immediately, and the ground in a 6-Drift zone becomes a spore bed (4 Lytic/Cycle).

**Special: Chemical Veil (3 PP):** for 2 Cycles, Lytic damage to the Matriarch is halved; the veil can be stripped by any Dry effect, Thermal burn, or a Smith's Tune (AT 16).

**Leaves behind:** C12 / G7 / P3 / T1 / T1 · Codon 21 · BSP 10 — and the Serum Signature: a Pristine Sequence that predates the Closing. This is Act I's smoking gun; do not let it be sold casually.

**Stat block:** Catastrophe · T2 · Vast · PHASE 1 MT 12 · MI 60 · soak 5 · BD 6 Lytic · pool 6d · PP 5 · Flow 2 (rooted)

### Enzymatic Horror

**In a fight:** walks at the armour. It will pick the most armoured opponent, every time.

**Weak point:** Mucoid-family grafts are immune to its Corrodes; Diver Gradient or a Desiccation zone halves its Surge.

**Traits:** Acidic Surge (Core VII: once per Cycle, everything in Contact takes 5 Lytic) · Corrodes · Testate-Soft (Lytic weapons gain +2 against armour-wearers nearby)

**Special: Dissolve Armour (3 PP):** one target in Contact: its grafts lose 2 soak permanently (reparable by a Smith or a Nucleoid rite) and it gains Brittle.

**Leaves behind:** C5 / G2 / P1 · Codon 7 · BSP 2 (the Pristine Sequence is Acid Gland: the raw material of Acidic Corrosor kit)

**Stat block:** Standard · T2 · Macroscopic · MT 14 · MI 30 · soak 2 · BD 3 Lytic · pool 5d · PP 3 · Flow 4

### Protease Locusts

**In a fight:** arrives as a sound, strips a site's organic structure in four Cycles, and leaves before the Wardens do.

**Weak point:** Turbid or Dry terrain grounds them; Hypertonic air costs them 1 PP per Cycle.

**Traits:** Cloud Flight (Flow 5; ignores ground terrain) · Enzyme Rain (when a Strike hits, splashes 1 Lytic to everything in the band)

**Special: Bloom Chorus (3 PP, once per Pulse War):** all Locust swarms in the region (Director's count, usually 2–5 tokens) Strike the same target at once, adding +1 Overflow each.

**Leaves behind:** C4 / G1 · Codon 3.5 · BSP 1

**Stat block:** Swarmling · T2 · MT 13 · MI 14 · soak 0 · BD 2 Lytic · pool 4d · PP 2 · Segments 8

### Swarm Of Nine Mouths

**In a fight:** Cycle 1 reads your armour, Cycle 2 picks the type you cannot soak, Cycle 3 eats someone.

**Weak point:** its Segment count is its action economy: area effects do not just damage it, they silence its specials.

**Traits:** Nine Digestions (each Cycle it chooses one damage type — Lytic, Thermal, Osmotic — and its attacks become that type; it reads what the Cohort is armoured against first) · Cannot be Flank-Breached

**Special: Devour (5 PP, once per Pulse War):** a Dormant or grappled creature is consumed: it goes to Total Lysis, and the Swarm gains +12 MI and one of the victim's Harvest traits for the rest of the fight.

**Leaves behind:** C8 / G3 / P2 / T1 · Codon 14 · BSP 4

**Stat block:** Elite · T2 · Macroscopic colony · MT 15 · MI 60 · soak 4 · BD 4 Lytic · pool 6d · PP 4 · Flow 5 · Segments 12 (as a single organism)

### Autolytic Hulk

**In a fight:** it wants to be hit while you are close. Deny it that and it is just slow.

**Weak point:** killing it far from anyone; Desiccation (no fluid to rupture with); freezing.

**Traits:** Autophagic (when it takes 8+ damage, its next Strike gains +2 BD — it converts damage to appetite) · Segment Split (at 0 MI it does not die: it splits into 2 Swarmlings at full Segments, and the fight continues)

**Special: Rupture Burst (4 PP, once):** it detonates: `8 + Overflow` Lytic to everything in its band, then becomes a Swarmling with 6 Segments.

**Leaves behind:** C6 / G3 / P2 · Codon 15 · BSP 2

**Stat block:** Standard · T3 · Macroscopic · MT 15 · MI 38 · soak 3 · BD 4 Lytic · pool 6d · PP 3 · Flow 3

### Lysophage

**In a fight:** disarms, then eats. A Lysophage fight that lasts six Cycles has left the Cohort unarmed.

**Weak point:** fight it without Apparatus (Lash-and-soak, Bursts, Desiccation); Smith Overcharge on its own dissolved gear.

**Traits:** Enzyme Corridor (the fluid in a line 4 Drifts long becomes Acidic for its allies and costs the Cohort 3 Lytic/Cycle) · Graft-Eater (a hit removes 1 point of a target's graft soak permanently until repaired)

**Special: Digest Apparatus (3 PP):** one target's Apparatus in Contact is dissolved (durability roll 2d6, ≤7 destroys). It gains +1 BD for the rest of the fight.

**Leaves behind:** C10 / G4 / P3 / T1 / T1 · Codon 30 · BSP 4 (the Terminal Codon is why a Hegemony foundry will pay 600 S for this kill)

**Stat block:** Elite · T3 · Macroscopic · MT 16 · MI 76 · soak 5 · BD 5 Lytic · pool 7d · PP 4 · Flow 4

### Recession Feeder

**In a fight:** walks into a bloom and ends it, then turns on whoever is standing nearest.

**Weak point:** it is losing. A Cohort that feeds it (a corpse, a sacrificial animal, a colony's store) can lead a Recession Feeder somewhere useful.

**Traits:** Anti-Bloom (all regeneration, Segment regrowth, and healing within 6 Drifts of it stops) · Starving (it is always at 1 ATP; it will abandon any objective to eat)

**Special: Draw In (4 PP):** everything in a 5-Drift zone is pulled 1 band toward it and takes 6 Osmotic; it then heals 1 MI per creature affected.

**Leaves behind:** C7 / G4 / P2 · Codon 30 · BSP 2

**Stat block:** Standard · T4 · Vast · MT 16 · MI 46 · soak 4 · BD 5 Lytic · pool 7d · PP 3 · Flow 3

## Order II: Protein Sentinels

Parts of a building that defend it: a gate, a shrine, a foundry. They hold ground and punish anyone who enters. **What they want:** to keep their post. **Before a fight:** they usually warn or demand first. **Way out:** most will not chase you more than a few Drifts from what they guard. Leave, or satisfy the demand.

### Chaperone Drone

**In a fight:** builds walls, fixes friends, bites occasionally.

**Weak point:** ignore the drones and cut what they are repairing. They will spend the whole fight on maintenance.

**Traits:** Folding (it can fold a corpse, a door, or a piece of terrain into a barrier: integrity 12, blocks a line) · Loyal to Structure (it will repair a damaged Sentinel or Fold before attacking)

**Special: Assist Fold (2 PP):** gives an adjacent Sentinel +2 soak until the end of the Cycle.

**Leaves behind:** C3 / G1 · Codon 1.5 · BSP 1 (folded chaperone protein is the cheapest Fibrous graft material there is: 20 S at market)

**Stat block:** Swarmling · T1 · MT 12 · MI 10 · soak 0 · BD 1 Kinetic · pool 3d · PP 2 · Segments 4 · Flow 3

### Fold Sentinel

**In a fight:** plants itself at the chokepoint and dares you. It is not stupid; it is doing its job.

**Weak point:** get behind it (Flank Breach: −2 MT) — a braced Sentinel cannot turn, and a Navigator's shear or a Rider's Slip is the answer.

**Traits:** Brace (Core IV: it may not Drift; all attacks against it use MT +2) · Load-Bearing (if it takes 10+ damage from one hit it does not stagger: it anchors, and cannot be pushed for 2 Cycles)

**Special: Present (3 PP):** it declares a zone 3 Drifts wide Held for 3 Cycles: anything crossing it is Marked and costs +1 PP per Drift.

**Leaves behind:** C4 / G1 / P1 · Codon 3 · BSP 2 (the Greater Organ is a Brace Node: +2 soak, −1 Flow, Fibrous)

**Stat block:** Standard · T1 · Macroscopic · MT 13 · MI 22 · soak 1 (+2 while braced) · BD 2 Kinetic · pool 4d · PP 3 · Flow 3

### Bridge Builder

**In a fight:** builds. Continuously. Even during a fight, unless provoked.

**Weak point:** irrelevant; do not fight it. You cannot afford the political cost, and it has allies.

**Traits:** Not hostile unless its work is interfered with. It prints ribbon: 10 Drifts of new bridge per Fugue Interval, and its ribbon is load-bearing law (Bridge jurisdiction applies wherever it builds)

**Special: Offer (free):** if the Cohort helps it (2 Synthesis actions, or defending it for 3 Cycles), it will extend a ribbon to somewhere the Cohort names — an offer that can solve a campaign problem and start three others.

**Leaves behind:** C5 / G2 / P1 — and the Ash will refuse to sell it, and the Sentinels will hunt whoever takes it.

**Stat block:** Standard · T2 · Vast · MT 14 · MI 30 · soak 2 · BD 3 (never used on organisms) · pool 5d · PP 3 · Flow 4

### Lattice Guardian

**In a fight:** arrives, builds, waits. The encounter begins when the Cohort decides whether to fight it on its terms.

**Weak point:** drag the fight out of the lattice (push, shear, a Rider carrying the Cohort out); Desiccation cracks it (soak −2, and the lattice collapses at integrity 20).

**Traits:** Zone Claim (it declares a 4-Drift lattice on entering: it gains +2 soak, +2 MT, and any Cohesion spending inside the lattice costs double) · Unmoving Order (it never Drifts; it extends)

**Special: Cage Fold (4 PP):** one creature in Near is Sequestered in a protein cage (Core IV Sequester; escape AT 16 or the cage's integrity 40 destroyed).

**Special: Chain of Command (1 PP):** all Sentinels in its lattice gain +1 Overflow on their next hit.

**Leaves behind:** C8 / G3 / P2 / T1 · Codon 14 · BSP 4 (Pristine Lattice Rule: needed for Protein Weaver Cathedral Folds and for a Smith's Masterwork bracing)

**Stat block:** Elite · T2 · Macroscopic · MT 15 · MI 60 · soak 4 (+2 in its own zone) · BD 4 Kinetic · pool 6d · PP 4 · Flow 3

### Ribbon Warden

**In a fight:** herds you along the bridge into a knot where its allies are.

**Weak point:** fight it off-ribbon, cut the ribbon under it (integrity 30), or hand it a legal reason to leave (Bridge law is real: an Oath invoked on a bridge must be answered).

**Traits:** Bridge-Bound (on a ribbon it has Flow 6 and cannot be pushed; off one, Flow 2 and −2 to all pools) · Tether (a hit tethers the target to the bridge: DFF −2 until it breaks the tether, AT 15)

**Special: Shear Call (3 PP):** the ribbon vibrates: everything on it makes a DFF contest at AT 14 or is Jostled and pushed off into the drop.

**Leaves behind:** C5 / G2 / P1 · Codon 7 · BSP 2

**Stat block:** Standard · T2 · Macroscopic · MT 14 · MI 30 · soak 2 · BD 3 Kinetic · pool 5d · PP 3 · Flow 5 (on a ribbon only)

### Cathedral Warden

**In a fight:** fortifies, and expects you to come to it. Give it four Cycles and the encounter becomes a siege.

**Weak point:** Phase 3 is the answer: the Cathedral was built around someone. Stop, talk, or extract them, and the whole structure stops functioning — its walls go slack and its allies leave.

**Traits:** Living Structure (its Folds are walls with integrity 60 each; it can raise two per Cycle) · Sacred Ground (inside its shrine, all hostiles' Bursts cost +1 ATP and Oath boxes cannot be spent)

**Special: Consecrate (5 PP):** a 8-Drift zone becomes Held: viscosity 3, all Sentinel allies gain +2 soak, and the Cohort pays +1 PP per Drift.

**Leaves behind:** C15 / G9 / P4 / T2 / T2 · Codon 45 · BSP 10 — the Cathedral Fold specimen is the goal of every Protein Weaver at Stage IV, and the Ash will object to harvesting the core.

**Stat block:** Catastrophe · T3 · Vast · PHASE 1 (the shell) MT 13 · MI 80 · soak 6 · BD 7 Kinetic · pool 7d · PP 5 · Flow 2

### Knot Marshal

**In a fight:** it does not need to win. It needs to observe and report, and then the state arrives.

**Weak point:** kill it before it Files (Silent strikes, Concealment first, or simply winning in three Cycles); its junction can be destroyed (structure integrity 60).

**Traits:** Traffic Law (inside a junction it controls, it decides band order: one ally per Cycle may be moved 1 band by the Marshal — including forcibly away) · Register Eye (it sees Marks, forgeries, and Unwalled disguises: +2 to any contest to identify a fake)

**Special: Queue (5 PP, once):** all hostiles in a 5-Drift zone are Held (AT 16 to break) and the Marshal's side acts one extra full Cycle.

**Special: File (2 PP):** it records the Cohort chemically: a Warden or Hegemony response Clock advances by 1 even if the fight ends in the Cohort's favour.

**Leaves behind:** C10 / G4 / P3 / T1 / T1 · Codon 30 · BSP 4

**Stat block:** Elite · T3 · Macroscopic · MT 16 · MI 76 · soak 5 · BD 5 Kinetic · pool 7d · PP 4 · Flow 4

### Tensile Jailer

**In a fight:** takes out the Cohort's formation, not its MI: separates, walls, delivers one person to the authority it serves.

**Weak point:** split it from its walls (area effects, a Brute's push); any Lytic damage to its sheets drops their integrity to 0.

**Traits:** Restraint (its hits apply Anchor: target cannot Drift; escape VSC contest AT 15) · Wall-Build (can raise a sheet wall, integrity 30, in one action — this is how it splits a Cohort apart)

**Special: Deliver (3 PP):** a held creature is thrown: 2 bands, 8 Kinetic, and it lands where the Jailer chooses (often into a hazard, a vat, or the outside of a membrane).

**Leaves behind:** C6 / G3 / P2 · Codon 15 · BSP 2

**Stat block:** Standard · T3 · Macroscopic · MT 15 · MI 38 · soak 3 · BD 4 Kinetic · pool 6d · PP 3 · Flow 4

### Amyloid Bulwark

**In a fight:** it wins by being there. The fight is a route-planning problem with a body.

**Weak point:** it is a wall, not a combatant: go around, or Desiccate (Sediment stops; soak −4), or a Breacher's charge (Core Atlas: halve soak for 2 Cycles) is the intended answer.

**Traits:** Wall (it occupies 3 bands of a line: nothing crosses without a contest; attacks against it from Far are at −2 dice) · Sediment (each Cycle it grows: +6 MI, and its zone shrinks by 1 Drift)

**Special: Set (5 PP, once per Pulse War):** it hardens: immunity to push and to Kinetic damage for 3 Cycles, and everything attempting to move through its band takes 10 Osmotic.

**Leaves behind:** C12 / G5 / P4 / T2 · Codon 60 · BSP 4

**Stat block:** Elite · T4 · Vast · MT 17 · MI 92 · soak 8 (but −2 to its own pools) · BD 6 Osmotic · pool 6d · PP 4 · Flow 1

### Prion Husk

**In a fight:** walks into a site and unmaking it. It is not a predator; it is an infection of form.

**Weak point:** it cannot regenerate and cannot be reasoned with; Desiccation and Thermal (denaturing) are the only defences that scale. Fire works.

**Traits:** Misfold (a hit that deals 10+ damage spreads: one nearby ally of the Cohort — chosen by the Director, not the player — must make an OSM contest at AT 18 or gain Brittle) · Wrongness Immunity (Genetic effects, Mark, and Hijack fail: there is nothing there to rewrite)

**Special: Template (4 PP):** it touches a structure or corpse and it refolds: integrity halved, and a Swarmling of the Order's choice emerges.

**Leaves behind:** C12 / G5 / P4 / T2 · Codon 60 · BSP 4 (harvesting a Husk is how a Hegemony foundry gets destroyed: the specimen refolds the print line)

**Stat block:** Elite · T4 · Macroscopic · MT 17 · MI 92 · soak 6 · BD 6 Kinetic · pool 8d · PP 4 · Flow 4

## Order III: Nutrient Drifters

The grazing herds of this world: huge, slow and edible. Almost none of them want to fight. **What they want:** to eat and be left alone. **Before a fight:** they bunch, flare or turn when frightened. **Way out:** back off, do not startle them, do not take what they are guarding.

### Drifter Shoal

**In a fight:** arrives with the flow, eats the place, leaves before the fight ends.

**Weak point:** cut off the current, or harvest the Barren ground afterwards (Barren zones yield salt, worth more than the Shoal).

**Traits:** Current-Riders (in any Flow they gain +2 dice and cannot be pushed) · Shoal Sense (if one is attacked, all Drifters in the region flee one band — this can ruin a harvest)

**Special: Graze (2 PP):** strips a zone's nutrients: the zone becomes Barren (no regeneration, no Spore beds, no Bloom) and the Shoal heals 4 MI.

**Leaves behind:** C3 / G1 · Codon 1.5 · BSP 1

**Stat block:** Swarmling · T1 · Microscopic each, Mesoscopic cluster · MT 12 · MI 10 · soak 0 · BD 1 Kinetic · pool 3d · PP 2 · Segments 8 · Flow 6

### Glaze Medusa

**In a fight:** drifts through, stings whoever is in the way, marks them for later.

**Weak point:** viscosity — a Thick or Gel zone collapses its field; Turbid water makes it blind and it flees.

**Traits:** Stinging Line (its band is a 3-Drift trailing field: entering it costs 4 Osmotic and a Jostle) · Transparent (Concealment +2 in clear water; Chemoreception is the only reliable way to find one)

**Special: Glaze (3 PP):** a struck target is coated: −2 to all pools, and it leaves a scent trail readable by every Drifter in the region for a Tide. (This is how you get hunted three Beats later.)

**Leaves behind:** C4 / G1 / P1 · Codon 3 · BSP 2 (the sting-line specimen is the basis of Needle Dagger barbs and of the Glaze Vial)

**Stat block:** Standard · T1 · Macroscopic · MT 13 · MI 22 · soak 1 · BD 2 Osmotic · pool 4d · PP 3 · Flow 5

### Grazing Leech

**In a fight:** attaches to the biggest thing in the band and rides the fight out.

**Weak point:** Thermal, Lytic, or Salt (Desiccation kills an engorged Leech outright). Do not pull them off with Strikers; pull them off with fire.

**Traits:** Slow Thirst (each Cycle it is attached to something, it drains 2 ATP and 2 MI; it does not need to Strike to feed) · Slippery (MT +2 while attached)

**Special: Engorge (3 PP):** having fed, it grows: +10 MI and +1 BD, and it detaches with a wound: the host loses 1 point of graft soak (Corrodes).

**Leaves behind:** C4 / G1 / P1 · Codon 3 · BSP 2 (engorged Leeches carry the host's chemistry: an Ash reader will pay 40 S for a Leech that fed on a specific organism)

**Stat block:** Standard · T1 · Mesoscopic · MT 13 · MI 22 · soak 1 · BD 2 Kinetic · pool 4d · PP 3 · Flow 4

### Plankton Cloud

**In a fight:** grows. That is the whole encounter: you have four Cycles before this becomes six tokens.

**Weak point:** one Thermal or Lytic Burst ends a Cloud; Desiccation ends a Cloud; standing perfectly still often works.

**Traits:** Bloom-Threshold (in nutrient-rich or Hypotonic water it doubles at the end of each Cycle unless the zone is Dry, hot, or contested) · Blind Filter-Feed (ignores organisms unless they are in its band and moving)

**Leaves behind:** C3 / G1 / — · Codon 1.5 · BSP 1 (20 S of raw nutrient paste, and the base material of half the Vial catalogue)

**Stat block:** Swarmling · T1 · Mesoscopic · MT 12 · MI 10 · soak 0 · BD 1 (Lytic or Osmotic, choose on hit) · pool 3d · PP 2 · Segments 8 · Flow 4

### Vacuum Ray

**In a fight:** rearranges the Cohort, then eats the one who was out of position.

**Weak point:** Anchor and Immovable features (it cannot move what won't move); a Brute's counter-push; anything braced.

**Traits:** Intake (as a 2-PP action it creates a suction cone 4 Drifts long: everything in it is pulled 1 band and takes 4 Osmotic; small creatures are pulled two) · Fast Angler (it can bait: a false corpse appears in Far band, and the Cohort's Chemoreception reads it as fresh)

**Special: Drain Field (4 PP):** a zone's fluid is drawn out for 2 Cycles: Dry Zone rules apply (Core Atlas, Desiccation), and the Ray heals 2 MI per creature affected per Cycle.

**Leaves behind:** C5 / G2 / P1 · Codon 7 · BSP 2 (the intake organ is the raw material of Vacuole grafts and of every Desiccation Specialist's dreams)

**Stat block:** Standard · T2 · Macroscopic · MT 14 · MI 30 · soak 2 · BD 3 Osmotic · pool 5d · PP 3 · Flow 6

### Yolk Bloom

**In a fight:** explodes at the end. Standing near one when it dies is a mistake.

**Weak point:** area effects at range; anything that dries it; Engulfment (a Phagocyte Guard loves these).

**Traits:** Brood-Sac (at 0 Segments it does not die: it bursts into 1d4 new Swarmlings at 2 Segments each — kill the sac at range) · Rich (every point of damage it takes spills 1 Substrate onto the ground; a Cohort that kills it fast gets paid twice)

**Leaves behind:** C4 / G1 · Codon 3.5 · BSP 1 (the richest ATP source in the game: 3 S each and one free ATP for whoever consumes it raw — the survival food of the Rim)

**Stat block:** Swarmling · T2 · Mesoscopic · MT 13 · MI 14 · soak 0 · BD 2 Osmotic · pool 4d · PP 2 · Segments 8 · Flow 3

### Filter Back

**In a fight:** walks into a battlefield and eats the terrain. Then uses it.

**Weak point:** feed it deliberately (two hazards, then it is clogged and slow), then break its integrity (structure-equivalent: 50).

**Traits:** Living Sieve (it processes the region: any hazard in a 6-Drift zone is absorbed into it at the start of its turn — spores, acid, spore beds, even Lytic zones; the Filter Back then has that hazard and can vent it) · Slow (Frequency −2)

**Special: Vent (3 PP):** it releases one absorbed hazard at full strength in a band of its choice.

**Special: Clog (free):** while it holds two or more hazards, it loses its Vent and gains −2 MT: this is the intended tactic.

**Leaves behind:** C6 / G3 / P2 · Codon 15 · BSP 2 (a cleaned Filter Back is worth 300 S to any colony with a waste problem; harvesting one while clogged yields one Pristine Sequence per absorbed hazard)

**Stat block:** Standard · T3 · Vast · MT 15 · MI 38 · soak 3 · BD 4 Osmotic · pool 6d · PP 3 · Flow 2

### Tidal Strainer

**In a fight:** picks where the fight happens, then drowns the Cohort there.

**Weak point:** a Navigator's Redirect (contested RTC, AT 16) beats it, and the Cohort should have one; Desiccation to stop the current; attack its integrity (90) rather than its MI if you have a Breacher.

**Traits:** Current-Binder (it makes the current: it can set a 6-Drift channel's flow to any one direction at the start of its turn, and everyone else's Fluid Navigation is now contested against it) · Deep-Lung (immune to pressure, cold, and Hypertonic layers)

**Special: Lock (5 PP, once per Pulse War):** for 3 Cycles the flow in its zone is fixed: any attempt to move against it costs double PP and anyone failing is swept 2 bands into whatever the channel ends in.

**Special: Filter Feed (2 PP):** heals 8 MI and removes one condition; it can do this while doing anything else (it is a filter-feeder: eating is not an action).

**Leaves behind:** C10 / G4 / P3 / T1 / T1 · Codon 30 · BSP 4 (a Strainer's Pristine Sequence is Current Rule: a Smith or Engineer can write a region's flow into a Relic)

**Stat block:** Elite · T3 · Vast · MT 16 · MI 76 · soak 5 · BD 5 Osmotic · pool 7d · PP 4 · Flow 3

### Drift Whale

**In a fight:** migrates. The fight is usually against its migration, not against it.

### Complement Herald

**In a fight:** builds a chain reaction and waits for the Cohort to stand in a row.

**Weak point:** the lattice is broken by the Sequestered themselves: an ally inside can act at −2 dice and cut outward. Do not let it Mark three people at once — spread Marks by taking the cheap ones (Gnats, Clonals) early.

**Traits:** Cascade Reserves (it holds 4 Reserve PP and spends them on Cascades — a Herald's presence roughly doubles a Sentinel squad's action economy) · Membrane Lattice (its Marked targets are outlined: allies may attack them through walls and Gel at full bonus)

**Special: Membrane Attack (5 PP, once per Pulse War):** up to three Marked creatures within Mid are Sequestered in a protein lattice (escape AT 18, or destroy integrity 50). Allied attacks against Sequestered targets gain +2 dice and cannot be defended by Reactive Osmosis.

### Cytokine Screamer

**In a fight:** it is a force multiplier with legs. Fights escalate around it.

**Weak point:** kill it early (its specials are expensive and it has mediocre soak for its tier); Desiccation and Cold end its field; silence gear (Signal Dampener) denies its Broadcast.

**Traits:** Broadcast (its Signal actions cannot be countered while it is at 50% MI or higher: a Messenger's Counter-Signal must win the contest outright) · Inflammation Field (its band burns: 4 Thermal to everything in Contact at the end of its turn, allies included — it does not care)

**Leaves behind:** C6 / G3 / P2 · Codon 15 · BSP 2 (the scream-organ: a Resonator component; a Phase Bell built around one is worth 700 S)

### Graft Inquisitor

**In a fight:** reads you, names what is illegal about you, and then fights with the law already on its side.

**Weak point:** be lawful (the Cohort's best defence is paperwork), or win in three Cycles (it has only 3 PP and one big special).

**Traits:** Audit (free action: it reads every graft and Apparatus on an organism — family, grade, licence status, Gene-Debt balance) · Contraband (it may seize an unlicensed item from a held or Dormant creature; a Warden lawful fight ends with the Cohort poorer)

**Special: Revoke (4 PP, once):** one grafted Harvest trait is suppressed for the rest of the scene (the target's graft loses its benefit and its Minor Cost still applies — this is the worst action in the Bestiary, and it is aimed at Chimeric and harvested builds).

**Special: Parley (free):** it will accept a legal argument. An Antigen Detector, a Forged token, an Argument (Precept 6), or a valid licence ends the encounter without combat.

**Leaves behind:** C7 / G4 / P2 · Codon 30 · BSP 2 — and note: harvesting an Inquisitor is a crime against the Code in every registered region

**Stat block:** Standard · T4 · Mesoscopic · MT 16 · MI 46 · soak 4 · BD 5 Kinetic · pool 7d · PP 3 · Flow 4

### Inflammatory Tempest

**In a fight:** arrives, makes the region unlivable, and drowns the Cohort in bodies.

**Weak point:** it cannot stop recruiting, which means it cannot be flanked usefully — but its own reinforcements block its lines of effect. Cold, Desiccation, and any Dry zone end Fevered Ground; a Navigator's shear can turn its push against it. It burns its own allies: it is on bad terms with every other Order.

**Traits:** Recruitment (each Cycle it gains 1d3 Clonal Sentinels as it calls the response; the reinforcements arrive at Flow 6 and act immediately) · Fevered Ground (its zone: viscosity +1, Thermal 6/Cycle to non-immunes, all healing halved, and its allies gain +1 PP per Cycle there) · Swelling (each Cycle: everything within 4 Drifts is pushed 1 band and the zone shrinks — it physically expands the battlefield)

**Special: Storm (5 PP):** 14 Thermal to a 8-Drift zone, all creatures in it are pushed 2 bands, and the zone becomes a Thermal hazard for 3 Cycles (8 damage/Cycle).

**Leaves behind:** C12 / G5 / P4 / T2 · Codon 60 · BSP 4 (fever organs: the Thermic domain's finest material, and a Barophil Thermal Diver's entire ambition)

**Stat block:** Elite · T4 · Vast · MT 17 · MI 92 · soak 6 · BD 6 Thermal · pool 8d · PP 4 · Flow 5

### Tolerance Priest

**In a fight:** does not fight. Reclassifies.

**Weak point:** it must speak: Silence gear, a Counter-Signal, or killing it in two Cycles (it has no armour, no brace, and its Flow is only 4). Its power depends on there being Sentinels nearby: fight it where the state is absent, and it is a soft old thing with very high MI.

**Traits:** Self/Kin, Absolute (it cannot be attacked first: any hostile action against it marks 2 Stress on the attacker and immediately Mark-flags them for every Sentinel in the region) · Tolerance (it can declare any organism self: for the rest of the scene all Sentinels and Anomalies that recognise treat that organism as own tissue — including, horrifyingly, the Cohort's own allies if the Priest declares them)

**Special: Forgiveness (4 PP):** one creature of the Cohort is declared self: they cannot be attacked by Order VI entities, and they cannot attack them either. It does this to your best damage-dealer. It does this smiling.

**Leaves behind:** C14 / G6 / P5 / T2 / T1 · Codon 120 · BSP 4 (its T1 is the Tolerance Codon: the only known material that can restore a revoked graft. Three factions will buy it sight unseen, and one will kill for it.)

## Order VII: Anomalies

Places where the rules of the world have broken, wearing a body. **What they want:** nobody knows. **Before a fight:** something is wrong that should not be possible. **Way out:** the same trick never works twice. Watch what it does this time.

### Rulebreak Instance

**Special: Absorb (2 PP):** each Inhibitor rolled by any creature in its zone heals it 4 MI.

**Leaves behind:** C6 / G3 / P2 · Codon 15 · BSP 2 — the specimen keeps the Broken Rule for one Craft project, which is the most desirable and most illegal object in the Atlas's grafting tables

**Stat block:** Standard · T3 · Mesoscopic · MT 15 · MI 38 · soak 3 · BD 4 (type: the Cohort's most-used type) · pool 6d · PP 3 · Flow 4

### The Unnumbered
