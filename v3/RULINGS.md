# v3 rulings (November 2026)

Every ruling made while the v3 AI Director was built and played, and where it now lives. Each is written into the original book (.md and .docx) as a note at the end of the section named, listed after that book's contents, printed in the v3 table edition, and baked into the Director's and Players Assistants. Source of truth: `v3/director/data/kit/rulings.js`.

## Core Rulebook

- **Growing, and moving on** (2.3 Codon; 17 Nov 2026). A Growth happens at the next Reconstitution after your Codon total crosses the threshold (Atlas §4.1). An organism carried into a Vector printed at a higher Level is owed the Codon and the Growths to reach it, and chooses them at once, without waiting for a rest (Codex §5.7).
- **Sharing out a harvest** (2.4 Specimens; 12 Nov 2026). Every Specimen a harvest yields is offered round the Cohort in a picking order kept for the whole game. The organism at the front takes it or passes, and goes to the back either way. Nobody chooses for another player.
- **Protean Folding** (3.2 Enzymatic Pathways; 17 Nov 2026). Protean Folding, granted by the Protean tradition (Protein Weaver, Turbulence Navigator), is rolled with **RTC**. This Core list printed no Baseline for it; both Phenotypes that hold it key on RTC.
- **Someone with no stat block** (4.1 Begin the Pulse War; 16 Nov 2026). A person or thing with no printed block (a clerk, a pilgrim, a bystander) never starts a Pulse War. Say the cost first, then make one roll; the consequences come from the world (a witness, a Clock, a faction), and nothing is invented for harvest.
- **An Oath has words and a witness** (11.4 Oaths; 13 and 17 Nov 2026). An Oath is sworn in the player's own words and is owed to somebody the table can see (a fold, a clerk, a crew): name the witness when it is sworn. Breaking an Oath also advances the campaign's **COLONY** Clock by one (Codex §5.2: a broken Oath disturbs a settlement).

## Player's Atlas

- **Starting higher, in full** (Step 8 · Starting higher; 17 Nov 2026). Spend every Synthesis Choice your Level gives (one per Level above 1). Adaptations taken at creation pay their Substrate from your starting Substrate; an Adaptation that also costs a Specimen cannot be taken at creation. Bursts are limited to the Tier your Level reaches.
- **Growth at the table** (4.1 What a Growth gives you; 17 Nov 2026). At a Stage transition (Levels 6, 11, 16) you choose two Synthesis Choices, but still at most one Baseline grade per Growth. An organism carried into a higher-Level Vector takes its catch-up Growths at once (Codex §5.7). Adaptation Substrate comes from your own purse.
- **Chemical Literacy and Pressure Nerves** (4.2 Adaptations; 17 Nov 2026). Chemical Literacy's Burst is not free: learning it still costs a Synthesis Choice (the +1 Sequence Reading rank is free). Pressure Nerves' "+2 Pressure Tolerance" is **+2 dice on Pressure Tolerance rolls**, not Pathway ranks.

## Director's Codex

- **Creature numbers are never said** (3.2 The Cycle, run fast; 13 Nov 2026). Never say how many dice a creature rolls, nor its Membrane Threshold, its Integrity, its soak or how many Segments it has left: no count, no range, no "a handful". Describe what it wants, what it does and what it fears. Nobody at the table holds those numbers, so never send a player to look them up.
- **Someone with no stat block** (4.5 When the encounter is not a fight; 16 Nov 2026). An attack on someone with no printed block is not a Pulse War: say the cost first, take one roll, and let the world answer (witnesses, Clocks, factions). Nothing is invented for harvest.
- **Closing a day** (5.1 Calendars; 17 Nov 2026). A Vector that counts Beats pays its Clocks when a day ends. A route the Vector prices at a Beat costs that day the moment the Cohort takes it. If the table has played a long stretch (about ten exchanges at the table) and nobody has closed the day, close it: the day ran out while the Cohort worked.
- **The campaign's Clocks** (5.2 Clocks; 17 Nov 2026). **COLONY** (6) and **CORE** (4) stand from the first scene of a campaign; **ORDER** (5) opens when a Vector opens it (The First Carve: the Render Cascade opens it at 2). All three travel with the Cohort from Vector to Vector; a Vector's own Clocks stay behind. A broken Oath advances COLONY by itself. A Clock the fiction stops (a jammed pump) does not move until the fiction starts it again. What a Clock's stage prints is in force while the Clock is there and lifts when it is rewound below it. A Vector may print triggers that move one of its Clocks more than once a session (The Ninth Shift's BADGE and PAPER, The Long Swallow's IMMUNE); the one-advance rule is for your own discretionary moves.
- **Carrying organisms into the next Vector** (5.7 Downtime between Vectors; 17 Nov 2026). The Cohort brings its organisms: each takes the same seat in the next Vector with its name, Level, Codon, Substrate, kit, Bursts, Adaptations, Growths and carried Specimens, healed and rested, and that seat's new story hook. An organism below the next Vector's printed Level is owed the Codon to reach it and takes those Growths at once. The table may choose new organisms instead.
- **Exits and lapsed questions** (9.1 The five-room site; 17 Nov 2026). When a player takes an exit the site lists, the Cohort goes. Anything printed in the way (a challenge, a hazard, a toll) is played as a cost on the way: a roll, damage or a Clock, never a wall. Never invent a barrier the page does not print. A question put three times that nobody takes up lapses: do not ask it again, let what waited on it settle the plain way, and move the scene on.
- **A player who cannot come** (1.1 Four jobs, in order; 18 Nov 2026). When a player is away, the Director plays their organism in the open until they are back, as plainly as a creature and no further than the scene needs. Or a stand-in may play it: the organism stays its owner's, borrowed for the night. A stand-in fights, talks, rolls and uses its kit; its name, its Growth, a rebuild and which Apparatus it carries stay the owner's to change. When the owner returns, give it back and tell them what it did and what changed.
- **Going beyond the page** (4.3 Build an encounter in five moves; 18 and 19 Nov 2026). A Director may invent people, places and threads; once named they are canon, so write them down and keep them true. Invent at most one encounter a site: from the Bestiary, at most three bodies, no tougher than the toughest creature the site prints, with a reason the players can hear. Never invent a price, a reward or damage: those come from the page. A **story seed** written ahead (People, Places, Threads, one Encounter, Secrets, How it can end) is the easiest way to bring your own material to the table.

## Lytic Bestiary

- **The block stays behind the screen** (HOW TO READ A BLOCK; 13 Nov 2026). Read a block; never read it out. The players never hear a creature's dice, Membrane Threshold, Integrity, soak or Segments left. They hear what it wants, does and fears (Codex §3.2). A person with no block is never fought as a Pulse War (Codex §4.5).

## The First Carve

- **TOLL and a loud fight** (3.2 TOLL; 13 and 16 Nov 2026). A loud fight reaches the law only half the time: roll a d6, and TOLL advances on 4–6 (HUNGER is paid every time). Once TOLL stands at 3 or more, the Director no longer spends their own discretionary advance on it; only the printed triggers move it from there.
- **Opening a panel** (3.1 CARCASS; 13 Nov 2026). Opening a panel is one event the site pays: the first panel opened in a scene advances CARCASS, and further panels in the same scene do not (the host may set every panel to pay its own segment).
- **A body left uncut** (3.3 HUNGER; 17 Nov 2026). A body left uncut in the water when the Cohort leaves the site advances HUNGER and, as this section prints, the campaign's COLONY Clock.
- **A Beat in the carcass** (3.1 CARCASS; 18 Nov 2026). A cut stamped at a Tally Post is a cut signed. TOLL advances every Beat; CARCASS advances only on a Beat when no cut was stamped and no haul shipped. If about ten exchanges at the table pass with no Beat, the day has run out: close it (Codex §5.1).
- **Salt, priced** (3.1 CARCASS; 18 Nov 2026). Salt costs 10 S per 2 Drifts. A salted panel rewinds CARCASS and a salted wound rewinds HUNGER, each **once a scene**, so a sack of salt is a choice, not a way to empty the Clocks. Salt the Cohort finds (the first crew's cache) costs nothing.
- **A stamped cut is safe** (3.1 CARCASS; 18 Nov 2026). When the whale goes over (CARCASS 6) and in the Render Cascade (HUNGER 4), a cut stamped at a Tally Post counts as shipped: only unstamped cuts are lost or drop a grade. When the whale goes over, HUNGER jumps to 3 and the ORDER Clock opens at 2, as printed.
- **Paper pays once** (3.2 TOLL; 18 Nov 2026). Each kind of paper (a valid claim, a licence, a toll chit, an Ash witness, a Guild tally receipt) rewinds TOLL once in a Vector, and the Gut Stone's serial once more. Shipping a stamped haul earns its own tally receipt: TOLL −1, once a scene.

## The Ninth Shift

- **BADGE in dice** (3.2 BADGE; 17 Nov 2026). BADGE's dice apply to any contest with a Hegemony or Court NPC: −1 at 0, +1 at 2, +2 at 3 and above (Badges 4 and 5 keep the +2 and add what their row gives).
- **Routes and days** (7 · THE FIVE SITES; 17 Nov 2026). A route priced at a Beat costs that day the moment the Cohort takes it (the brine-walk is free to a Cohort at BADGE 0, walking as Kin). If about ten exchanges at the table pass with no Beat, the day has run out: close it, and SHIFT moves.
- **The arch is never a locked door** (7 · 1 · THE FILING GATE; 17 Nov 2026). A Cohort at BADGE 0–1 is challenged at the Warden arch and walked through under escort, which is a scene, never a locked door. A listed exit is always open at a price.

## The Long Swallow

- **Closing a day** (3 · THE UNAVOIDABLE PROBLEM; 17 Nov 2026). If about ten exchanges at the table pass inside the Host with no Beat, the day has run out: close it, and LICENCE moves. Say it, as this section says, every time.
- **Where Goodwill starts** (6.1 GOODWILL; 17 Nov 2026). Goodwill starts at **Curious** unless the Intake sets it (Intake 1, the Honest Road: Client; Intake 11, the Breacher's Hatch: Wary). IMMUNE 3 drops it a rung once; at IMMUNE 5 it can rise no higher than Curious.
- **The Churn, priced** (6.3 THE CHURN CLOCK; 17 Nov 2026). The enzyme sheets are a hazard, never a wall: anyone may swim the deep channel and pays 4 Lytic per Cycle while in it. A fill pushes everyone inside the body 1 band (2 at LICENCE 5 or at Hostile) and pays 6 Osmotic; Braced holds. CHURN then starts again. A jammed pump stops the Clock, and the Cohort swims on through the dead water.
- **A challenge is a cost** (6.4 TOLERANCE; 17 Nov 2026). A Host that is Wary or worse sets every Tolerance contest at AT 18, sponsored or not. A Sentinel's challenge is a cost on the way (a contest, a Mark, a Clock), never a stop: an honest answer of what you are passes it.
