# Running the night — one page for the host

Keep this beside you. Nothing here needs the code open.

## Before anyone arrives (10 minutes)

1. Double-click **`Start Director.bat`**. If anyone is playing from outside your Wi-Fi, use
   **`Start Director (online).bat`** instead and send them the `trycloudflare.com` link it
   prints.
2. Open the **host console** link in the window. Keep that window open: closing it stops the
   table.
3. Put the **table screen** on the shared display.
4. **Director tab:** pick your brain and model, press **Test**. It must say the model called a
   tool. If it does not, pick another model — the Director cannot run without tools.
   * On a local model, set **Thinking** to **Low**. Fast and clean.
   * Your local model (iq3_s) is what the benches test on and costs nothing to run. A cloud model
     such as Gemini bills you, and stops mid-game when its spending cap runs out.
   * Leave the mode on **Co-pilot** for the first session.
5. **Run tab, Session zero card:** set the pause word (default *SLACK WATER*) and a one-line
   tone note, for example *body horror is in, cruelty to animals is out*.
6. **Saves tab:** set up the backup once (paste your Supabase secret key, **Save and test**).
   From then on every save, and the autosave every five minutes, is copied there too.
7. Starting over: **New game…** on the clocks card. Choose the Vector, and whether the players
   stay seated (same people, same organisms, fresh sheets, same session-zero terms). Undo brings
   the old game back if you press it by mistake.

## As they sit down

Players open the **player** link on their phones and type a name. The links (player, table and
host, and the outside link with its table code when you started online) are in the **Join links**
fold-out on the clocks card, on the table screen, and on every player's Colours tab, so anyone
already in can pass them on. Before they can take an
organism they read the session-zero card and press the button. The host console shows
**nobody yet** → **2 not ready** → **all ready**.

Read the pause word out loud anyway. The card is not a substitute for you saying it.

Anyone can add a line to the "off the page" list, then and later. **The list is shared, the
name is not** — do not ask who added one.

When everyone is ready, press **Take a turn** to open the scene.

**Somebody can't come?** Two choices, both under **Players and stand-ins** on the Run tab (bottom
left):

* **Player away** — the Director plays their organism in the open until they are back.
  **Take back** returns it.
* **Hand to a stand-in** — someone new plays it tonight. They join on the player link, read
  session zero, and pick it from **Characters needing a player**. They get a catch-up card (with a
  "Previously…" from the Director when the AI is on). It stays the owner's: no renaming, Growing,
  rebuilding or changing which Apparatus it carries. When the owner is back, **Give back to …**: the
  owner sees what it did and what changed. If a stand-in's phone drops out, the Director covers
  after about 90 seconds and hands it back when they reconnect.

## During play

The Director takes a turn whenever a player types what they do, rolls, or ends a turn. You do
not need to press anything.

On the phones: the **Books** tab reads any public book aloud; the flashing **Show pictures** button
(with a little radar on it) opens the creatures and a map of where everyone is and the ways out;
the living water behind the screen answers the game (players can switch it off on Colours). In a
Pulse War, everyone's actions are told on the Story card, so nobody misses a turn.

**Plain words.** This world has a lot of its own words, and new players get lost in them. Three
things help, and none of them needs you:

* **Tap a word.** The first time a setting word (Tonic, the Churn, Substrate…) or a creature's name
  appears in the story, it is underlined. A tap shows what it means. Every word is on the
  **Words** tab too, with a search box. Tell the table this at the start.
* **The Director explains new words.** It knows which words this table has already met, and says
  what a new one means the first time. If it forgets, the Engine adds the meaning in brackets:
  *the Churn (the Host's digestion)*. You may hear these brackets read aloud by the voice.
* **Ask.** "What does that mean?" typed to the Director always gets a plain answer.

**Rolls.** The Director waits for a player to say what they try ("I look where the light was")
before asking for a look-or-sense roll. Every roll asked for is said in the story with what it is
for, so the whole table hears it. On each phone the health vial, energy gems and specimen satchel
show the organism's state at a glance, and a glow on the sheet says when it can grow.

**In Co-pilot, every turn waits for you.** You see what it did with the Engine and what it
will say.

* **Approve** — it goes to the table.
* Edit the text first if a line is wrong. Your words go out, not its.
* **Try again** — same situation, fresh attempt. Use this when the tone is off.
* **Discard** — nothing happened. The Ledger is untouched.

Once it feels steady, switch to **Auto**. Anything that fails the leak check still stops for
you.

## When it goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| :-- | :-- |
| It stalls on "thinking" | Press **Stop thinking**, or **Restart** to stop and start a fresh turn (Director tab). A local model on a long briefing is slow, so give it a minute first. |
| It says a number for a creature | Discard and **Try again**. It is not meant to, and the check usually catches it. |
| It invents a price, item or seller | Discard. Tell them in your own words what the page actually sells. |
| It forgets someone | Type a note in the box: *Vash has not acted — ask Vash*. |
| It will not start a fight a player declared | **Run tab → Start a Pulse War**. Do it yourself. |
| It keeps a dead scene going | **Run tab → Stand down** on the creature, or end the fight by hand. |
| It stops with an error | Check the key or the address on the Director tab. Set mode to **Off** and run the Run tab yourself while you sort it. |
| Everything is wrong | Mode **Off**. The Run tab runs the whole game without any AI. The night does not depend on it. |

**Undo** on the clocks card takes back the last change to the Ledger.

## Sharing out the harvest

Harvested Specimens don't go to whoever cut them. Each one is offered in turn, by a picking
order kept for the whole game: the player at the front gets **Take it / Pass** on their phone,
and either way they go to the back. To save taps, a player can choose **Take it, and every C that comes to me** (or pass every C): it answers for them when their turn comes, never out of turn, and the x next to it stops it. While it's happening, a card on the Run tab shows who is
choosing, with **Take for them / Pass for them** for anyone away from their phone. The order is
also on the Sheets tab; the ↑ buttons move someone up. The Tally Post won't stamp until
everything is shared.

## Things only you can do

* **Call for a roll** — Run tab, pick who, the Baseline and Pathway, the Threshold, what for.
* **Award** Codon or Substrate. The Director can only pay what the page prints; you are not
  limited.
* **Oaths** — when a player promises something, type it into the Swear box on their sheet. When
  they meet it or walk away from it, press **Kept** or **Broken**. Keeping pays the table a
  Cohesion; breaking marks two Lysis boxes and a scar. Play the consequence, do not scold.
* **Move a clock** by hand with the + and − buttons.
* **End the day** — in a Vector that counts Beats, this closes a day and moves the Clocks a day
  pays. A day also closes itself after about ten of the Director's turns.
* **Salt, shipping and paper** (The First Carve, under the clocks card, outside a Pulse War): salt
  a panel (CARCASS back one) or a wound (HUNGER back one), once a scene each at 10 S per 2 Drifts;
  ship the stamped haul (TOLL back one); and produce paper or the Gut Stone's serial (TOLL back
  one, each kind once a game).

## The Memory tab

Everything said at the table is kept. The **Memory** tab shows:

* **The chronicle:** a paragraph for each site the Cohort left. The Director reads the latest
  three every turn, so if one is wrong, press its **x** and it will not be used.
* **People, places and threads:** who the table has met, and what is known about each. A
  "players not told" chip marks facts the Director knows that the players do not. A chip saying
  **made up by the Director** means it invented that person: it is canon now. If you would
  rather it were not, press **x** to forget it.
* **Search:** ask it anything, like *what did the clerk say about Neth*, before you answer a
  player yourself.

**Page only / Callbacks / Ad-lib:** Page only keeps the Director to what each site prints. With
Callbacks it may bring back people from earlier sites in conversation. With **Ad-lib** it may also
invent people, places and threads (canon once named), and one small encounter a site from the
Bestiary, no tougher than the page's own.

## The Seeds tab: your own stories

Write your own material for ad-lib as a **story seed**. Type an idea or paste a story, press
**Write it up**, read and change it, and **Save this seed**. Switch seeds on and off any time; they
are used while ad-lib is on, and players never see them. The full guide, with a prompt for writing
seeds in any chat app, is `docs/STORY-SEEDS.md`.

## The Voice tab

The table screen reads the Director's narration aloud and shows a small living face that breathes, churns when the Director is thinking and pulses when it speaks. Each screen has its own **Voice on / Voice off** switch on the story card. By default every screen uses its own device voice. If you run a speech server (for example Kokoro on this PC), put its address on the **Voice** tab and everyone hears one steady voice. Phones also get a **Talk** button; each player agrees to it at session zero, and a player who says No simply types.

## At the end

Press **End the night** on the Memory tab first. It writes the night's chapter, so next time the
Director remembers where you left off.

The session saves itself after every change. To keep a copy you can come back to, **Saves tab →
Save this game** with a name (it goes to Supabase too). **Load** brings any save back; Undo undoes
a load. Close the window. Next time, start the same way
and it carries on where you left off — in whichever Vector you were playing.

To play the same Vector again with the same group, **New game…** with *Keep the players seated*
ticked: everyone is straight back in, on fresh sheets, without rejoining.
