Dungeons & Dragons, fifth edition

Tools for running and playing D&D 5e. They work offline in your browser and keep everything on your own device.

Dungeon Master's AssistantTrack the party, roll initiative, run monsters from their stat blocks, build balanced encounters, look up any rule, and run The Lantern of Thornwick Ford. Players' AssistantBuild a character step by step, keep the sheet, roll attacks, checks and saves, cast spells and track slots, and level up. The AI DirectorFor a table where an AI runs the game: start it on your own computer from dnd/director (Start Director.bat). Players join from their phones.
