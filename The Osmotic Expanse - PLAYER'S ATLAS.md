# 1 · MAKING AN ORGANISM

Eight steps, twenty minutes, one body.

## Step 1 · Choose a Lineage

Your Lineage is your body plan and your people. It sets one Baseline up and one down, gives you a **Gift** (once per scene), a **Frailty** (always), a **Bonded Domain**, and a starting social position that other organisms can smell.

Six lineages: **Amoeban · Epithalian · Flagellan · Lysosan · Immunan · Barophil.**

## Step 2 · Assign your Metabolic Baselines

Take the array **4, 4, 3, 3, 2** and place the five numbers on **VSC, DFF, RTC, OSM, AFF**. Then apply your Lineage's ±1. No Baseline may start above 5 or below 1.

| If your idea is… | Raise | Lower |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| to be unbreakable, to break others | VSC | DFF or RTC |
| to be fast, precise, untouchable | DFF | VSC |
| to catalyse, craft, burst | RTC | VSC or AFF |
| to notice, to know, to be unfooled | OSM | AFF or VSC |
| to lead, bond, command, negotiate | AFF | OSM or DFF |

**Grade ladder:** 1 starved · 2 workable · 3 competent · 4 specialist · 5 colony-famous · 6 Nucleoid-grade (not available at creation).

## Step 3 · Choose a Phenotype (your class)

Twenty-four Phenotypes, grouped by lineage tradition in §3. Each gives:

* **Hull** (3, 4, or 6 → part of your MI),
* your **class Pathways** at fixed ranks,
* a **Signature Mechanic** (a resource only you have),
* one **Stage feature per Stage** (I at Level 1, II at 6, III at 11, IV at 16),
* a **Bonded Domain** (which may override your Lineage's),
* a starting kit.

You may take a Phenotype outside your Lineage — it costs you: your Lineage Gift works only once per **Tide** instead of per scene, and one faction two rungs away notices. Multi-lineage organisms are common in the Hegemony and suspicious everywhere else.

## Step 4 · Take your Pathway ranks

Your Phenotype grants ranks. Then choose **two additional Pathways at rank 1 (Expressed)**. Max rank 4 (Sovereign), never above 2 at creation.

## Step 5 · Buy kit

**40 Substrate** at Level 1. You may buy one Apparatus, one graft, and Vials. If you cannot afford your idea, write the debt: a **Hegemony licence loan** is 100 S at 5 S/Beat and cannot be escaped by dying (Ch. XII).

## Step 6 · Write three things down

1. **A chemical name** — a scent-phrase. "Siss-of-the-Nine-Smells" is a name; "Bob" is a rumour.
2. **One Oath** — a promise you have already folded into your chromatin. You have **Oath boxes = AFF Grade**; this Oath uses one. Say what it forbids.
3. **One Vector** — the task that transmits you. Who hired you, what you were paid, and why you have not finished it.

## Step 7 · Compute your derived numbers

```
MI      = 12 + (VSC x 4) + Hull + 0          (Level 1)
ATP max = 8 + (RTC x 2)
PP      = 2 + DFF                            (typical 4)
MT      = 10 + DFF + graft mods
Flow    = 3 + DFF Drifts
Countdown boxes = VSC + ceil(Hull/2) + 2
Graft Slots = 1
Homeostatic Baseline (per pool) = 3 x dice
Stress track = 0/8
```

## Step 8 · Starting higher

Starting at Level 6 (Stage II)? Take the Phenotype's Stage I **and** II features, **150 S**, one Uncommon graft, and set Codon to 400. Level 11 (Stage III): Stage I–III features, **500 S**, one Rare graft, Codon 1300. Level 16 (Stage IV): everything, **1,600 S**, one Very Rare item, Codon 2700. Every Stage transition also grants **+2 to one Baseline or +1 to a Pathway rank** and **+6 max MI** (Stage feature).

## WORKED EXAMPLE — *Siss-of-the-Nine-Smells*

**Concept:** a courier who delivers messages into places messages are not wanted, and who is very good at arriving fast and loudly.

1. **Lineage: Flagellan** (the Rowed). +1 DFF, −1 VSC. Gift: *Current Sense*. Frailty: *Obligate Drifter*. Bonded Domain: **Osmotic**.
2. **Baselines:** array 4,4,3,3,2 → DFF 4(+1)=**5**, RTC 4, OSM 3, AFF 3, VSC 2(−1)=**1**.
   VSC 1 is a real cost: MI will be thin, and Siss cannot be pushed around without consequence. *Good. That is the character.*
3. **Phenotype: Current Rider** (Flagellan tradition). Hull 4, Bonded Osmotic, Pathways: Fluid Navigation 2, Pulse Discipline 1, Concealment 1. Signature mechanic: **Momentum Reserve**.
4. **Extra Pathways:** Signal Discourse 1, Chemoreception 1.
5. **Kit (40 S):** Peptide Blade (20 S), Lipid Weave Wrap (20 S, free slot, soak 1, +5 MI).
6. **Name / Oath / Vector.** Oath: *"I will deliver what I am given, even to a hostile."* Vector: a packet sealed in a capsule Siss cannot open, addressed to a colony that officially does not exist.
7. **Derived:** MI = 12 + 4 + 4 + 5(wrap) = **25**. ATP 16. PP = 2 + 5 = **7**. MT = 10 + 5 + 0 = **15**. Flow 8 Drifts. Countdown = 1 + 2 + 2 = 5. Slots 1.
   Pools to remember: Fluid Navigation **5+2 = 7d** (homeostatic 21), Concealment 5+1 = **6d** (18), Membrane Penetration 0 → Lash attack **5d vs MT 15**, Pulse Discipline 5+1 = **6d**.
   Translation: Siss is *extremely* hard to catch, hits for 3 BD with a blade, survives about six solid hits, and cannot win a shoving contest with a door.

**How this looks in play:** Siss never enters Contact willingly. Three Drifts of Momentum, a Strike at +2 dice, Reactive Osmosis on the counterattack, and a burst of Osmotic push that puts a band of water between everyone. The VSC 1 is the story: the first time something corners Siss, the table should hold its breath.

# 2 · THE SIX LINEAGES

Each lineage is a body plan **and** a politics. In the Expanse, you cannot hide what you are made of — everything you say is partly a confession.

| Lineage | Baselines | Body | Bonded Domain | Gift | Frailty |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Amoeban** | +1 RTC, −1 OSM | no fixed shape | Lytic | *Reshape* | *Unwalled* |
| **Epithalian** | +1 VSC, −1 DFF | sheets, walls, layers | Protean | *Draw Tight* | *Set* |
| **Flagellan** | +1 DFF, −1 VSC | lashes, fins, wake | Osmotic | *Current Sense* | *Obligate Drifter* |
| **Lysosan** | +1 OSM, −1 AFF | latent, doubled, quiet | Somatic | *Latency* | *Registered* |
| **Immunan** | +1 AFF, −1 RTC | recognising, marking, ranks | Galvanic | *Self/Kin* | *Literal* |
| **Barophil** | +1 VSC, −1 DFF | dense, slow, patient | Thermic | *Hardened* | *Slow Reaction* |

## AMOEBAN — *the Unfinished*

No wall you did not choose. Amoebans flow: a body is a temporary decision, and they are the only lineage that finds that normal. They are the Expanse's chemists, digesters, and thieves, and every other lineage has a word for an Amoeban that means "honest in the worst way".

* **Baselines:** RTC +1, OSM −1.
* **Body:** **Reshape** — once per scene, as a 1-PP action, alter your shape: pass a gap of one Drift, hide one Apparatus inside yourself, change your apparent size class by one, or mimic a phenotype you have touched (not a person — a *type*). Lasts until you Reshape again.
* **Frailty — Unwalled:** your grafts are 50% less reliable. Any graft you install takes **one extra Slot**, and Testate grafts cannot be worn at all.
* **Natural apparatus:** pseudopod lash (Lash, Contact, BD 2/4/6/8/10, Lytic).
* **Viewed as:** useful, untrustworthy. The Warden Circles require Amoebans to carry a shape-registry token; the Hegemony licenses their labs eagerly.
* **Common Phenotypes:** Acidic Corrosor, Enzymatic Splicer, Detritus Reclaimer, Membrane Breacher.

## EPITHALIAN — *the Walled*

Epithalians are sheet-beings: they grow in layers, they build in sheets, and they understand instinctively that a wall is a moral position. Colony architects, foundry masters, and the people who sign the toll contracts. They are the lineage most other organisms look at and call *civilisation*.

* **Baselines:** VSC +1, DFF −1.
* **Body:** **Draw Tight** — once per scene, as a Reactive (spend your Reserve), reduce incoming damage by **6 + your Stage** and end one Leaking effect on yourself or an adjacent ally.
* **Frailty — Set:** your shape is decided early. You cannot benefit from Metamorphosis resets, and any condition that changes shape (Turgid, Flaccid) lasts one extra Cycle.
* **Natural apparatus:** none — Epithalians build tools. But your first **Apparatus** crafted or bought costs half.
* **Viewed as:** authority. Immunans trust them, Flagellans resent them, Barophils outlive them.
* **Common Phenotypes:** Protein Weaver, Peptide Constructor, Amino Acid Smith, Ribozyme Engineer.

## FLAGELLAN — *the Rowed*

Born in the Great Flows. Flagellans move or they sulk; a stationary Flagellan is a sick one. They are couriers, herders of drift-herds, navigators, and the only lineage that reads a current the way an Epithalian reads a wall.

* **Baselines:** DFF +1, VSC −1.
* **Body:** **Current Sense** — once per scene, declare a line of fluid anywhere you can smell or see: for the next 2 Cycles, you and any ally touching that line gain **+1 PP** and **+2 Drifts**, and one Osmotic effect you create along it costs 0 ATP.
* **Frailty — Obligate Drifter:** if you begin a Pulse Cycle having not moved since your last turn, you gain no PP that Cycle (only Reactive Osmosis is available). In a Reconstitution you must spend at least one Fugue Interval moving.
* **Natural apparatus:** flagellar strike (Lash, Contact, BD 2/4/6/8/10, Kinetic) and a push-off: once per scene, moving 3 Drifts away costs 0 PP and does not provoke a Flank Breach.
* **Viewed as:** freelancers and smugglers. The Flow Guilds are almost entirely Flagellan.
* **Common Phenotypes:** Current Rider, Turbulence Navigator, Pheromological Messenger, Kinetic Striker.

## LYSOSAN — *the Latent*

The viral lineage — not *infected*, **bilateral**. A Lysosan carries sequences that are not theirs and has negotiated with them. They are the Expanse's information brokers, spies, geneticists, and the only beings who can lie to an antigen scan. Other lineages cannot decide whether they are people or a very political library.

* **Baselines:** OSM +1, AFF −1.
* **Body:** **Latency** — once per scene, when an effect would impose a condition on you, choose instead to **carry** it: nothing happens now, but at any later point you may give that condition to another organism (Contact or via a projectile action, 1 PP) or burn it for +2 dice on your next roll. You cannot carry more than two at once.
* **Frailty — Registered:** every Immune Sentinel, Warden ward, and antigen gate in the Expanse can see that you are *not quite standard*. Social contests with Immunans start one rung worse on the Stakes Ladder, and you cannot hold the **Antigen Hood** graft.
* **Natural apparatus:** none. Lysosans fight with borrowed machinery. Your first **Burst** learned each Tide costs half.
* **Viewed as:** a legal minority with a very good lawyer. The Sequence Underground is Lysosan-founded.
* **Common Phenotypes:** Genetic Hijacker, Pathogen Scout, Viral Mimic, Replicator Agent.

## IMMUNAN — *the Registered*

Immunans are the lineage of *recognising*: they mark, they rank, they remember what belonged and what did not. They staff the Warden Circles, keep the antigen registers, and are the only beings who can look at a Lysosan and name every sequence it carries. They are also, in the estimation of everyone else, incapable of a metaphor.

* **Baselines:** AFF +1, RTC −1.
* **Body:** **Self/Kin** — once per scene, declare one organism within Mid band **self** or **not-self**. While *self*, allies' effects against it count as friendly and its soak increases by 2; while *not-self*, it is **Marked** and all attacks against it by your Cohort gain +1 die.
* **Frailty — Literal:** you cannot be deceived by language alone, but you also cannot *use* it: disadvantage on all deceptive (Affective/Deceptive register) contests, and you always answer a question truthfully unless an Oath forbids it (which physically hurts: 1 Stress per sentence).
* **Natural apparatus:** antigen barb (Projector, Mid, BD 1/3/5/7/9, Voltaic, Lodged).
* **Viewed as:** the law. Feared, hired, and quietly pitied.
* **Common Phenotypes:** Antigen Detector, Phagocyte Guard, Antibody Archer, Complement Cascade Commander.

## BAROPHIL — *the Hardened*

Extremophiles: the lineages that chose the worst places and *did fine*. Barophils live in acid, in pressure, in heat, and in cold so deep that thought slows. They measure time in tides and patience in epochs, and they are the only beings that the Vacuolar Abyss considers weather rather than intruders.

* **Baselines:** VSC +1, DFF −1.
* **Body:** **Hardened** — you ignore one tonic state of your choice at all times (choose at creation; may be changed at each Stage transition). Additionally, hazard and environmental damage against you is reduced by **2 × your Stage**.
* **Frailty — Slow Reaction:** your Frequency Check is always made at −2, and your Reactive Osmosis costs **2 PP**.
* **Natural apparatus:** dense blow (Weight, Contact, BD 3/5/7/9/11, Kinetic, Heavy, Braced).
* **Viewed as:** geology with opinions. The Deep Choir recruits almost exclusively from them.
* **Common Phenotypes:** Acid-Resistant Tank, Thermal Diver, Osmotic Brute, Desiccation Specialist.

# 3 · THE TWENTY-FOUR PHENOTYPES

A **Phenotype** is not a job. It is a way your body is prepared to be wrong about the world — and the specific way it profits from being right.

Each Phenotype grants **four Stage features** (Levels 1, 6, 11, 16), one **Signature Mechanic** available from Level 1, and a **Hull** for MI. Stage features marked **⟂** replace a Synthesis Choice from §4 if you would rather have the mechanic than the upgrade — you never get both.

Six traditions, four Phenotypes each. The tradition is your lineage's usual path; taking a Phenotype outside it is legal, expensive, and visible (Step 3).

---

## THE AMOEBAN TRADITION — *dissolve, enter, revise, reuse*

Amoeban Phenotypes treat the body as a suggestion and other bodies as inventory. They are the only tradition where every feature can be used on **yourself** as well as on someone else, which is why Amoebans live short, interesting lives.

### ACIDIC CORROSOR
*Striker / attrition · Hull 3 · Key: RTC, VSC · Bonded: Lytic*

**Granted Pathways:** Catalysis 2 · Membrane Penetration 1 · Sequence Reading 1.

**Signature Mechanic — ETCH.** Every Lytic hit you land marks **1 Etch** on the target (max 5, one creature at a time visible to you; you can *move* your Etch focus by spending 1 PP). Etch is spent, on your turn, for any combination:

| Spend | Effect |
| --: | :-- |
| 1 | +2 Lytic damage on your next Strike against it |
| 2 | your next Strike against it ignores its soak entirely |
| 3 | inflict **Brittle**; its graft loses 1 soak permanently (max −3) |
| 5 | **Deliquescence:** it takes `2 × Stage` Lytic damage, and its grafts are *loose*: it drops one worn graft (harvestable by anyone, including you) |

**Stage I — Digest Armour.** Your own grafts are acid-hardened: +1 soak vs Lytic; when an attacker uses Lytic damage on you, they take `1 + Stage` Lytic back.
**Stage II — Spreading Taint ⟂.** A Runaway Catalysis on a Lytic hit marks Etch on **one other** creature in Near band. Etch max rises to 6.
**Stage III — Unbound Appetite.** You may spend Etch on **structures and terrain**: eat a wall, a gate, a Gel zone, a hazard. 3 Etch = a 4-Drift hole in anything of tier below yours.
**Stage IV — Total Dissolution ⟂ (capstone).** Once per Pulse War, spend all Etch on a target: it takes `4 × Stage` Lytic damage ignoring all soak. If this takes it below 0 MI, its body is **erased**: no Harvest, no Re-Synthesis, no Legacy for anyone. This is the most feared sentence in the Expanse.

**Kit:** Acid Duct (120 S if affordable, else Spore Pistol), Lytic-friendly graft (Lipid Wrap), 2 Vials.
**How to play:** you are the reason armour stops mattering. Start the fight by *investing* (cheap Strikes to build Etch) instead of bursting; the payoff is a target that cannot be tanked. Never be the one standing in front.
**Links:** Testate-armoured enemies are your purpose; the **Digest Armour** Burst catalogue (Ch. VI) and the **Corrosive Catalyst** stack with Etch. A Detritus Reclaimer in your Cohort turns every dissolved corpse into salvage instead of mush — negotiate before you capstone.

### MEMBRANE BREACHER
*Breaker / opener · Hull 6 · Key: VSC, DFF · Bonded: Osmotic*

**Granted Pathways:** Membrane Penetration 2 · Pressure Tolerance 1 · Fluid Navigation 1.

**Signature Mechanic — BREACH CHARGE.** Whenever your attack beats a target's MT by **6 or more** (i.e. Overflow ≥ 6 against its Membrane Threshold), you **Breach** it:

* its **soak is halved** (round down, min 0) for 2 Cycles;
* your Cohort's next attack against it gains **+2 Reagents**;
* you may instead spend the charge on an *object*: a door, gate, wall, or cyst takes `8 + 4 × Stage` damage and its break AT drops by 4 for your allies.

Only one Breach per creature at a time. Breaches are **visible** — describe them: a split seam, a peeled plate, a rent that sprays.

**Stage I — Forced Entry.** You ignore the first 3 points of soak from any Testate or Fibrous graft when you Strike. Doors never cost you a Synthesise action: 1 PP.
**Stage II — Rupture Push.** A Breach also throws the target **1 band** away from you (its choice of axis). Enemies that hit a wall take 4 Kinetic and become Jostled.
**Stage III — Spatial Breach ⟂.** Spend a Breach to open a **hole in a membrane or a zone**: any ally may move as a free Drift through it, and the passage lasts until the end of the next Cycle. Walls become suggestions; this is how you rescue someone from Sequester.
**Stage IV — Rupture Protocol ⟂ (capstone).** Once per Pulse War, before you roll a Strike, declare it: if it hits, **treat its Overflow as 8** (Breach triggers, and damage is computed with 8). Against structures, this destroys anything below tier 4 outright.

**Kit:** Pressure Ram or Chitin Flail, Pore Locks graft if you can, Harvest Claw optional.
**How to play:** you exist to make the fight's defensive math collapse. Pair your big turns with the Corrosor's Etch and the Archer's Locks — a Breached, Etched, Locked target is a *solved* target. Buy dice (tools, Momentum, Cohesion passes) over damage: your whole kit wants a wide margin, not a lucky hit.
**Links:** Kinetic Striker's Momentum stacks with your Breach; the **Peptide Snare** and **Vortex Lure** Bursts pin things so your allies can line up the 6-Overflow hit. Immune Sentinels in the Bestiary carry anti-Breach plates — read the block before you spend your capstone.

### ENZYMATIC SPLICER
*Support / re-wiring · Hull 3 · Key: RTC, OSM · Bonded: Somatic*

**Granted Pathways:** Catalysis 2 · Synthesis 1 · Chemoreception 1.

**Signature Mechanic — SPLICE.** As a Synthesise action (2 PP), you may perform one **Splice** on a creature within Mid band. A Splice moves something that already exists:

* **a condition** from it to another creature in range (including onto a hazard's owner, or into a willing host);
* **one point of permanent soak loss** (from Brittle or Corrodes) from it onto yourself, willingly, as a Synthesise action — you can carry up to `Stage` lost points, and shed them later as +1 soak for 1 Cycle each;
* **a Burst's Rider** onto an ally's next Strike (costs 1 Common Tissue Specimen);
* **one ATP or 3 MI** between two creatures in range (the donor chooses nothing; the recipient chooses nothing; you are the problem-solver).

**Stage I — Field Revision.** Your Substrate Patch and Mend-style heals also end one condition of *your* choice on the target, not the target's choice.
**Stage II — Rank Splice ⟂.** Once per Pulse War, for 3 Cycles, move **1 Pathway rank** from one creature in Contact to another. A hostile's catalysis just dropped a die; your Kinetic Striker suddenly knows Fluid Navigation.
**Stage III — Type Splice.** As part of a Splice, change the **damage type** of an ally's next Strike or Burst to any type you choose (they must still be able to cause it — you are rewriting what the wound *does*). Synergy: an Antibody Archer's Kinetic bolt becomes Genetic and stops caring about Testate plate.
**Stage IV — Chimera Sequence ⟂ (capstone).** Once per scene, graft one **Harvest trait** from a Specimen you carry onto any creature in range for `Stage` Cycles. Enemies can receive them too. The Director should let this be creative: a Phagocyte Guard wearing a *Wraith's Phase-Lattice* is a legal, terrible idea.

**Kit:** Suture Cannon if affordable, Needle Dagger, Mucoid Mantle later (you will be near the damage).
**How to play:** you are the Cohort's thermostat. Learn which conditions your enemies inflict (read the Bestiary's Orders) and move them back. Your value is highest when the fight is going badly, which is when you will be targeted — buy MT, not soak.
**Links:** the strongest partner for a Detritus Reclaimer (you can move its Salvage costs around) and for a Complement Cascade Commander (its Marks are conditions you can relocate). Parasitic grafts (9.8) are your best friend: you can *share* them.

### DETRITUS RECLAIMER
*Bruiser / scavenger-engineer · Hull 6 · Key: VSC, RTC · Bonded: Lytic*

**Granted Pathways:** Vigour 2 · Synthesis 1 · Membrane Penetration 1.

**Signature Mechanic — SALVAGE.** You convert dead matter into a pool. **Harvesting is free for you** (no action, no contest, tier +8 AT waived) and yields **Salvage points** equal to the creature's tier (Catastrophes: 4). Salvage max = `RTC + Stage`. You spend Salvage:

| Cost | Effect |
| --: | :-- |
| 1 | rebuild a broken graft: restore its lost soak for the scene, or patch Leaking on any creature in Contact |
| 2 | an improvised Apparatus: BD `2 + 2 × Stage`, any type you choose, falls apart next Cycle |
| 2 | **Reclaim a body:** gain one Harvest trait of a creature that died this scene, for `Stage` Cycles (contest waived; the trait is weaker by 1 tier) |
| 3 | raise a **Reclaimed Husk**: an ally-construct with MI `8 × Stage`, MT 8, one Strike at BD `Stage`, Segments if you spent on a swarm |

**Stage I — Efficient Gleaner.** Your Harvest checks take no time outside combat, and your Cohort's Harvest rolls gain +2 dice. You always know, by smell, what died within Far band.
**Stage II — Cycle Doctrine.** When any creature dies within Mid band, gain 1 Salvage. You may spend Salvage at range on allies' grafts (they must be in range of your signal).
**Stage III — Reclaimer's Field.** Once per Pulse War, spend 4 Salvage: everything in a 4-Drift zone is *digested into you*: you gain 3 MI per creature, allies gain 1 ATP per creature, and hostile Harvest traits are suppressed for the scene.
**Stage IV — Return to the Cycle ⟂ (capstone).** Once per Tide, target a creature that Total-Lysed within the last Beat. It rises as a **Reclaimed** ally at half MI, with one Pathway rank less, and owes you a debt the table should absolutely make painful. It is not your character. It is a person made of the person who died.

**Kit:** Harvest Claw (90 S — buy it first), heavy Apparatus, Vacuole Sac.
**How to play:** you are the party's economy. Every fight you win is *matter*, and matter is how the Cohort survives the next three. Say out loud what you are harvesting; the Director is entitled to give you a problem made of what you carried.
**Links:** the Corrosor's Total Dissolution destroys your raw material — this pairing needs an agreement. The **Reclaim a body** trick is the intended use of every Bestiary Harvest trait, so the Bestiary's yields column is, for you, a menu.

## THE EPITHALIAN TRADITION — *wall, machine, tool, pattern*

Epithalian Phenotypes believe that a problem is a gap in a structure, and that structures are cheaper than deaths. Everything they build is real, has MI, and can be destroyed — which is the whole point, and the whole risk.

### PROTEIN WEAVER
*Controller / fortifier · Hull 4 · Key: RTC, VSC · Bonded: Protean*

**Granted Pathways:** Synthesis 2 · Protean Folding 1 · Signal Discourse 1.

**Signature Mechanic — FOLD TOKENS.** You have **Fold Tokens = RTC** (max 8), refreshed at each Reconstitution and by spending 1 PP to re-knot (1 token per Cycle, max 2/Cycle). Spend tokens to fold protein into the world:

| Tokens | Fold |
| --: | :-- |
| 1 | **Snare:** a hidden peptide trap in one band. Target's next entry: VSC contest AT 12 + your Overflow − 4 or become Anchored and −2 DFF. Detectable at OSM 14. |
| 1 | **Handhold:** an ally in range gains +3 Flow and immunity to one push this Cycle |
| 2 | **Sheet wall:** a protein barrier, 4 Drifts long, MI `10 × Stage`, MT 6, soak 2. Blocks line of sight and Osmotic effects; Lytic damage against it is doubled |
| 2 | **Graft-tighten:** an ally's worn graft gains +2 soak until your next Reconstitution. Cannot stack on the same graft twice |
| 3 | **Net:** a 3-Drift zone: anyone exiting must win DFF (AT 14) or be stopped at the band edge and Anchored |
| 4 | **Bridge:** a crossing over a gap of up to `4 × Stage` Drifts, permanent until cut |

**Stage I — Read the Grain.** You can tell, by touch, what a structure is made of, its weak type, and roughly how much MI it has. Structures you craft gain +5 MI.
**Stage II — Living Sheet ⟂.** Your walls **regenerate** 4 MI per Cycle if you spend 1 ATP, and you may set one wall to be *selective*: you choose a chemistry (an Order, a lineage, a pheromone) that passes through it freely. Colonies pay fortunes for this.
**Stage III — Tensile Works.** Your Folds can be **loaded**: as a Synthesise action, store an ally's Strike inside a wall or net (up to `Stage` charges). Anyone can then release it: the stored damage fires at MT 12 from the structure. Ambush engineering.
**Stage IV — Cathedral Fold ⟂ (capstone).** Once per Tide, spend 8 tokens: a **permanent structure** (a gatehouse, a span, a fortress-shell) appears in one region, sized to district scale, and it changes that region's travel routes. It has MI 400, can house a colony, and every faction in the Codex will have an opinion about it.

**Kit:** Grapple Tendril, Chitin Spurs, Fibrous Lattice Vest.
**How to play:** you fight the *map*, not the monster. Fold before the fight if you can; in combat your job is to make the enemy's preferred band expensive. Never spend your last token — always leave one for a Handhold on someone else.
**Links:** Turbulence Navigator sets Flow, you build into it: a net at the end of a current is a kill-zone. Ribosome Engineer can copy your Folds. Bestiary **Protein Sentinels** are your rivals and your textbooks.

### PEPTIDE CONSTRUCTOR
*Summoner / engineer · Hull 4 · Key: RTC, AFF · Bonded: Protean*

**Granted Pathways:** Synthesis 2 · Symbiosis 1 · Sequence Reading 1.

**Signature Mechanic — ASSEMBLY.** You build **Constructs**: simple bodies of folded protein and harvested tissue. A Construct's block (also printed in the Bestiary's *Colony Constructs* section):

```
MI 8 + 8 x your Tier    MT 11 + Tier    pool 3 + Tier    BD 1 + Tier    soak Tier-1
PP 2    Flow 4    size as built    Segments if built from swarmlings
Traits: does not breathe, cannot be Marked by scent, obeys one standing order
```

Cost: **20 S per Tier** plus one Common Specimen per build, one Fugue Interval, a Synthesis contest at AT `10 + 2 × Tier`. You control up to **1 + ⌊RTC ÷ 2⌋** Constructs at a time; excess Constructs go inert. A Construct you destroy still **harvests** (2 Specimens, half the Substrate).

**Stage I — Standing Orders.** Each Construct carries one programmed instruction (guard a band, carry Bulk, block a charge, hand you a Vial). It triggers without your PP. A Construct obeying a standing order acts without costing you Command PP.
**Stage II — Inserted Doctrine ⟂.** When you build, install one **Harvest trait** from a Specimen you carry (a Construct with a *Spore Hound's* Lodged barb, or a *Swarm's* Segments). One trait per Construct.
**Stage III — Linked Line.** Two of your Constructs may share a Reserve pool and a sense: if one is hit, the other learns the attacker's position and may react. Also: your Constructs gain **+1 PP** when adjacent to another Construct.
**Stage IV — Foundry ⟂ (capstone).** You may establish a permanent Foundry in a colony: Constructs build there for half cost and one Fugue Interval, and in a Pulse War you may **assemble in combat** (4 PP, 40 S, no contest, at your Tier − 1). You control up to **3 + ⌊RTC ÷ 2⌋**. A Foundry is a political asset: the Hegemony will licence it, tax it, or try to own it.

**Kit:** Suture Cannon or Barbed Launcher, Vacuole Sac, 60 S reserved for builds.
**How to play:** your Constructs are not heroes; they are *terrain with legs*. Spend them to hold bands, absorb one terrible hit each, and force enemies to spend actions. Repair by rebuilding — cheaper than healing.
**Links:** the Detritus Reclaimer is your supplier (Salvage can substitute 10 S of the cost). The Amino Acid Smith can tune a Construct's apparatus. Immunan Commanders can *cascade* through your Constructs' Marks — a Construct cannot be Marked by scent, but it can be Marked by an Antigen Detector, and that is a legal exploit worth discussing at session zero.

### AMINO ACID SMITH
*Support / gear-shaper · Hull 4 · Key: RTC, AFF · Bonded: Protean*

**Granted Pathways:** Synthesis 2 · Chemoreception 1 · Vigour 1.

**Signature Mechanic — TUNE.** As a Synthesise action on an item within Contact, tune it for the scene. Choose **one**:

* **+2 BD** on an Apparatus, or **+2 Reagents** to its attack pool (pick one, not both);
* its **Rider** triggers on **Overflow 4+** as well as on Runaway Catalysis;
* a graft's **soak +2**, or it stops counting against your Slots for the scene;
* a Vial's effect **doubles**, or it gains one extra target in Near band.

An item may be tuned once per scene (twice at Stage III). A tuned item's tuning ends at a Reconstitution. **Tuning a Relic** costs 4 ATP and is always a negotiation with the Director (the Relic has opinions).

**Stage I — Honest Work.** Your crafting costs −20% Substrate, and you can appraise any item by tasting it: you always know its rarity, its Minor Cost if hidden, and whether it is parasitic.
**Stage II — Overcharge.** As above, but the effect doubles (+4 BD, or +4 dice, or +4 soak) and the item must win a **durability check**: roll 2d6, on 6 or less the item is **destroyed** at the end of the scene. You may overcharge your own graft and take the destruction Stress willingly.
**Stage III — Field Forge.** Once per scene, spend 1 PP and 20 S: produce one Common Apparatus or graft from your kit list, already installed. You may also repair **permanent** soak loss (Corrodes, Brittle, Acidic surge) on one item per Reconstitution.
**Stage IV — Masterwork Sequence ⟂ (capstone).** Once per Tide, grant a **permanent Rider** to one item you have owned for at least a Tide: pick from the catalogue or design one with the Director at the value of an Uncommon property. A Masterwork is a named object; it will outlive you and be fought over. Items can hold one Masterwork Rider ever.

**Kit:** Harvest Claw, Chitin Flail, 40 S in spare parts.
**How to play:** you are the reason the Cohort's gear feels *alive*. Tune the striker with the biggest pool, not the biggest BD — dice are worth more than damage past Stage II (see the Core's Overflow tables). Say "I tune" early: a Smith who waits until someone is dying has already lost.
**Links:** every gear-heavy Phenotype (Breacher, Archer, Brute) is your customer. Your Masterwork Rider and the Core's Relic Gene-Debt system are the same language: **make the Director bill you for it.**

### RIBOZYME ENGINEER
*Ritualist / pattern-thief · Hull 3 · Key: RTC, OSM · Bonded: Somatic*

**Granted Pathways:** Catalysis 2 · Sequence Reading 2 · Chemoreception 1.

**Signature Mechanic — TEMPLATE.** When you **observe** a Burst, a Fold, or a creature's printed Special action used within your senses, you may spend 1 PP to attempt a **Template**: Sequence Reading contest vs `8 + (2 × its tier)`, at −2 dice if it was a creature's alien action. On success you hold a Template of it.

* You may **use** a Template once, at your own Tier's Effect Value, paying that Burst's normal PP and **+1 ATP** (borrowed machinery is dearer). After use, the Template is spent.
* You may hold Templates equal to **⌈RTC ÷ 2⌉ + Stage**, and they survive a Reconstitution.
* Templates of creature actions are how a Cohort gains a *legal* answer to a Bestiary special — this is the intended pressure valve when the Director throws something at you that your class list has no answer to.

**Stage I — Annotated Margin.** When you Template from an ally's Burst, they keep it: you both have it. Their pool for that Burst gains +1 die this scene (they are watching you do their work and improving).
**Stage II — Printed Dose ⟂.** Spend 1 Substrate and 2 PP to print a Template into a **Vial**: any creature (including an ally or an enemy, if they can be tricked) can fire it as a Synthesise action at the printed tier. Vial-Templates are the black market's favourite object.
**Stage III — Revision.** A Template you hold may be **revised** once: change its range band, its damage type, or its target from ally to enemy (or back). Revising costs 2 Stress: you are arguing with the sequence.
**Stage IV — Write a New Sequence ⟂ (capstone).** Design your **own Burst** with the Director: choose domain, tier, cost, and effect within the Core's guardrails (no more than one new condition per Burst). It becomes a permanent entry on your sheet, learnable by others (which is a story in itself), and a copy exists somewhere the moment you use it.

**Kit:** AE Crucible if affordable (450 S, save for it), Needle Dagger, Secondary Lens.
**How to play:** you are the Cohort's memory. Ask the Director to describe *how* an enemy ability works — the fiction is the spell component, and the more precise the description, the cheaper your Template contest should be. Keep one Template slot free for emergencies.
**Links:** the Lysosan Genetic Hijacker steals; you **copy**, which is legal in most jurisdictions and infinitely more useful long-term. Pair with a Weaver: their Folds are cheap to Template and you get free terrain.

## THE FLAGELLAN TRADITION — *move, arrange the water, speak, arrive late and hard*

Flagellan Phenotypes are the Expanse's movement. Their features assume one thing: **you are never where the enemy aimed.** The cost is the Frailty *Obligate Drifter* — a Flagellan that stops is a Flagellan that loses its Cycle.

### CURRENT RIDER
*Skirmisher / mounted striker · Hull 4 · Key: DFF, OSM · Bonded: Osmotic*

**Granted Pathways:** Fluid Navigation 2 · Pulse Discipline 1 · Concealment 1.

**Signature Mechanic — SLIPSTREAM.** When you move at least **3 Drifts** in a Cycle (by your own Drift or by a current), gain **1 Slip**, up to **Stage + 2** stored. Spend Slip for any one:

| Cost | Slip buys |
| --: | :-- |
| 1 | +2 Reagents to one attack, or +2 Drifts of free movement now |
| 1 | an ally in Contact gains +2 Flow and +1 Reagent on their next roll (you woke the water for them) |
| 2 | **Ride Along:** an adjacent ally moves with you, at your Flow, for free, this Cycle |
| 2 | you ignore one push, one Sequester, or one current's pull; instead you move 2 Drifts wherever you choose |

**Stage I — Reads Water.** You always know the Flow, viscosity, and tonic state of a scene as a free action, and currents never force saves against you. Flow +2.
**Stage II — Wake Cutter.** When you leave a Contact band, no Flank Breach is provoked (you cut the wake). Momentum now needs only 2 Drifts and grants +1 PP as well as the dice.
**Stage III — Standing Wave.** As a Reactive (your Reserve), when an attack against you **fails**, you may spend 1 Slip to shove the attacker 1 band and make it Jostled. Misses are ammunition.
**Stage IV — Confluence ⟂ (capstone).** Once per Pulse War, declare Confluence: for **3 Cycles** you choose the field's Flow direction and strength (up to 3) at the start of each of your turns, and you may Split Frequency without limit. Flagellans call this *the Long Ride*. Afterwards, mark 3 Stress and you must spend a Reconstitution eating 20 S of Substrate.

**Kit:** Flagellar whip or Grapple Tendril, Flagellar Motor graft as soon as affordable, Lipid Weave.
**How to play:** you are the Cohort's positioning engine. Buy Flow and Pulse Discipline before damage; your damage comes from dice you already have. Always ride something — a current, a corpse, an ally's push.
**Links:** Turbulence Navigator builds the road you drive on. Osmotic Brutes can throw you. Any Bestiary creature with a Current attack is a gift to you: their terrain is your Slip stream.

### TURBULENCE NAVIGATOR
*Controller / terrain · Hull 4 · Key: DFF, RTC · Bonded: Osmotic*

**Granted Pathways:** Fluid Navigation 2 · Protean Folding 1 · Chemoreception 1.

**Signature Mechanic — CHART.** As a 2-PP action, you **chart** a zone (radius `3 + Stage` Drifts) for `1 + ⌈Stage ÷ 2⌉` Cycles, setting one of:

| Chart | Effect while inside |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Channel** | Flow 3 toward a direction you name. Allies moving with it cost 0 PP and gain +1 PP at their turn's start; enemies must win Fluid Navigation (AT 12 + Stage) or be dragged |
| **Dead Water** | no currents, no Momentum, no push, no Osmotic movement effects. Ranged attacks +1 die. Sweeps and vortexes fail |
| **Eddy** | anyone entering must win DFF (AT 12 + Stage) or end their Drift at the zone edge; a Reactive Osmosis here costs 0 PP for your allies |
| **Shear** | Kinetic damage is +2 against anything that moved more than 2 Drifts last Cycle; Momentum impossible |

Only one chart at a time at Stage I–II, two at Stage III+. You may change a chart as a 1-PP action instead of re-charting.

**Stage I — Chart Read.** At the start of a Pulse War you announce the field's terrain numbers and any hazard the Director has hidden (they are not hidden from you; describe what you smell). Hazards you have seen can be *rerouted*: move a hazard 2 Drifts, 1 PP.
**Stage II — Redirect.** As a Reactive, when an ally is pushed, swept, or Sequestered, you may change the destination band (including "back where they came from"). Costs 1 PP.
**Stage III — Manufactured Current.** As a 2-PP action, create a **Current** (strength up to Stage) anywhere you can sense, for 3 Cycles. It can be vertical, diagonal, or *out of* a wall. Bestiary sweeps and Nutrient Currents count as allies now.
**Stage IV — Rewrite the Flow ⟂ (capstone).** **Downtime:** permanently alter a region's current routing — a trade route closes, a valley floods, a colony's water changes. Every faction reacts (Codex Ch. IV). **In combat:** once per Pulse War, for 3 Cycles you set the **entire field's** viscosity, Flow, and tonic band (one choice), and your charts cost 0 PP.

**Kit:** Tuning Fork of the Deep Flow (500 S, save for it), Barbed Launcher, Osmotic Filter.
**How to play:** you win fights before the first Strike by deciding where everyone can stand. Talk to the Director about the terrain numbers *before* initiative — charting an arena you chose is the Flagellan version of a prepared spell.
**Links:** the Protein Weaver's walls + your Channel = an enforced funnelling. Your Dead Water shuts down Osmotic Brutes, Current Riders, and most Bestiary Pathogenic Wraiths. Antigen Detector pairs well: charts give *your* Cohort the dice that its Marks then spend.

### PHEROMOLOGICAL MESSENGER
*Support / controller (social combat) · Hull 3 · Key: AFF, OSM · Bonded: Galvanic*

**Granted Pathways:** Signal Discourse 2 · Chemoreception 2 · Concealment 1.

**Signature Mechanic — BROADCAST CHARGES.** You have **AFF + Stage** Broadcast Charges, and you regain **1 at the start of each of your Cycles**. A charge buys a free signal (no PP, no roll) to any creature you can sense:

| Charge | Signal |
| --: | :-- |
| 1 | **Rally:** one ally's next roll this Cycle gains +2 Reagents |
| 1 | **Halt:** one enemy's next Drift costs double PP unless it wins OSM (AT 10 + Stage) |
| 1 | **Mark:** one enemy becomes Marked (allies +2 Reagents; ends after `Stage` Cycles or a 2-ATP scrub) |
| 2 | **Quiet:** remove a Mark, a False Scent, or a hostile signal from an ally or an area |
| 2 | **Carry:** deliver a message, an Oath prompt, or a Bond benefit instantly across any distance you have a scent-line to |
| 3 | **Counter-Signal:** force a hostile Burst or signal-based action to target you, or to fizzle (their pool −4, then you mark 1 Stress) |

**Stage I — Interpreter.** You can speak any region's dialect by tasting it, and you always know when someone is lying *to someone else*. You may spend a charge to let an ally understand anything said, anywhere in the scene.
**Stage II — False Chorus.** Once per scene, produce a **False Scent** (Core Bursts, Galvanic III) at half cost, at your own Tier's Effect Value. Creatures that attack the phantom waste a Cycle; they know it was a trick and that has consequences.
**Stage III — Chorus Command.** Your Rally, Halt, and Mark can target **every creature in one band** for the same charge cost. This is the moment a Messenger stops being support and becomes a commander.
**Stage IV — The Ninefold Name ⟂ (capstone).** Once per Pulse War, speak a **name** at one creature (any tier; Catastrophes gain +4 to the save): it must win OSM at AT `12 + (Stage × 2)` or suffer −2 to all pools for 2 Cycles **and obey one short sentence you give it** ("leave", "hold", "stop", "turn"). A named creature never forgets who named it. Roll 1d6 after; on 1, the name reached something further away than the target.

**Kit:** Needle Scatter or Spore Pistol, Signal Vane graft (400 S — buy it), Pheromone Mask Vials.
**How to play:** you are the party's dice-accountant. Your charges are the cheapest +2 in the game — spend them on the ally with the biggest pool, not the one in trouble. In social scenes you are the class: a Messenger can run a negotiation as a Pulse War and win on rungs.
**Links:** the Antigen Detector and Complement Cascade Commander both *Mark*; you Mark for free and at range, so coordinate who spends what. Your Counter-Signal is the intended answer to Pathogenic Wraiths' hijacks.

### KINETIC STRIKER
*Striker / finisher · Hull 4 · Key: DFF, VSC · Bonded: Osmotic*

**Granted Pathways:** Membrane Penetration 2 · Fluid Navigation 1 · Vigour 1.

**Signature Mechanic — IMPACT.** A Strike made after moving **3+ Drifts** (Momentum, Core §4.3) marks **Impact** on the target (max `Stage × 2`). Impact is a debt you collect later:

| Spend | Collect |
| --: | :-- |
| 1 | +3 Kinetic damage on your next Strike against that target, or +1 band of push |
| 2 | your next Strike against it ignores 2 soak |
| 4 | the target is **Jostled** and its Reserve is cancelled this Cycle |

**Stage I — Long Run.** Momentum triggers at 2 Drifts, and Momentum's bonus becomes **+3 Reagents** (or +4 in a current). Flow +1.
**Stage II — Riposte of Arrival.** As a Reactive Osmosis, you may make one Strike as part of the same Reserve: if the attacker is moving toward you, your Strike gains Momentum automatically. You want to be attacked.

**Stage IV — Unanswered Motion ⟂ (capstone).** Once per Pulse War: at the start of your turn, declare it. You gain **an extra full set of PP this Cycle** (refresh everything), you cannot hold a Reserve until your next turn, and you mark 3 Stress. Every Strike this Cycle that hits pushes 1 band for free. Veteran Strikers call this *arriving twice*.

**Kit:** Pressure Ram or Chitin Flail, Momentum-property Apparatus (Barbed Launcher), Chitin Spurs.
**How to play:** you are a plan, not a damage dealer. Your turn order with the Navigator and Rider decides whether you exist: you need 3 Drifts of runway. Ask for the terrain. When you cannot run, spend Impact you already banked — you should almost always be carrying 2+ Impact on someone.
**Links:** Navigator's Dead Water kills *your* Momentum too, so say so out loud. Membrane Breacher + Kinetic Striker is the classic execution pair: Breach halves soak, Impact ignores the rest.

## THE LYSOSAN TRADITION — *carry, transmit, become, multiply*

Lysosan Phenotypes are the lineage that treats identity as a **transport medium**. Their features move things between bodies: sequences, functions, names. Every Lysosan feature is, in some jurisdiction, a crime — which is a mechanic, not just flavour, because the Codex's factions keep receipts.

### GENETIC HIJACKER
*Controller / counter-catalyst · Hull 3 · Key: RTC, OSM · Bonded: Somatic*

**Granted Pathways:** Sequence Reading 2 · Catalysis 1 · Concealment 1.

**Signature Mechanic — INSERT.** As a Synthesise action (2 PP, Mid band) or on a **Genetic**-type hit, attempt an Insert: a contested Sequence Reading against the target's OSM + Pressure Tolerance. Success installs **one Insert** on the target (max `1 + Stage` at once). Each Insert does one thing, chosen when installed:

| Insert | Effect |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Silence** | the target cannot Begin Bursts of one domain you name (they may Shed it with an OSM save each Cycle at AT = your Overflow + 10) |
| **Lock** | the target cannot use its **Signature Mechanic** (Phenotype feature) until it wins the same save |
| **Backflow** | the next time the target deals damage, it takes `Stage + 4` Genetic damage and you heal half of that |
| **Toll** | every action the target takes costs it 1 extra ATP; if it cannot pay, its action fails and it marks 1 Stress |
| **Leash** | one of the target's own Harvest traits or Vials now serves *you*: you may spend 1 PP to use it once |

You can hold **one Leash or Lock per target**, and Inserts are *chemical signatures*: another Lysosan can see exactly who did it.

**Stage I — Reading the Host.** Free action: you know a creature's Order, tier, and one Harvest trait by scent. Inserts you install on a creature you have read gain +2 to their save DC.
**Stage II — Recombinant Payload ⟂.** When an Insert is removed or ends, you may capture it as a **Pristine Sequence** (1 Bulk). These are the raw material of high grafting and Re-Synthesis — and the most addictive loot in the game.
**Stage III — Competitive Exclusion.** An Insert of yours on a target **blocks** any hostile Genetic effect against it: you are not only hijacking, you are *preventing*. This is the single best answer to a Pathogenic Wraith, and Wraiths will prioritise killing you accordingly.
**Stage IV — Permanent Insertion ⟂ (capstone).** Once per Tide, at AT 20 (no contest reduction; Catastrophes gain +4): take **one Pathway rank** or **one Harvest trait** from a creature you have an Insert on. It is yours until a Nucleoid-grade Mend restores it. The creature remembers. So does the Warden Circles.

**Kit:** Needle Dagger (Lodged, Silent), Sequence Key Vials, Mucoid Mantle.
**How to play:** you are the reason a Bestiary special doesn't happen. Ask the Director which printed special is the fight's spine and Lock that one. Do not spend your action economy on damage: your whole value is *cancelling*.
**Links:** Pathogen Scout seeds, you cancel — a Scout/Hijacker pair can hold a region hostage, and the Codex has an entire adventure about that pair. The Ribozyme Engineer copies what you steal, which will cause a fight at the table and a brilliant campaign.

### PATHOGEN SCOUT
*Seeder / attrition · Hull 3 · Key: OSM, DFF · Bonded: Somatic*

**Granted Pathways:** Chemoreception 2 · Concealment 2 · Synthesis 1.

**Signature Mechanic — VECTORS.** You have **OSM + Stage** Vector doses, refreshed at a Reconstitution. Seed a Vector (1 PP, Contact, or loaded into a Lodged Apparatus hit, or left on a surface):

| Vector | Trigger (choose at seeding) | Payload |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Lytic bloom** | enters a band / Strike / Cycle N | `4 + 2 × Stage` Lytic damage, graft loses 1 soak until repaired |
| **Signal rot** | attempts a Burst or Signal action | Burst pool −3, target Locked (save each Cycle) |
| **False self** | is attacked by another creature | the target's next attack against a *different* creature is made at −2 and may hit an ally |
| **Drift mark** | moves more than 2 Drifts | Marked, and leaves a scent trail readable by you at any distance for a scene |

A Vector is **latent**: it does not register to Chemoreception unless the examiner is looking for exactly this (OSM contest at AT 14 + Stage).

**Stage I — Field Inoculation.** Your Vectors work on **structures and terrain**: seed a wall, a gate, a colony's water supply. A structure carrying a Vector is a bomb, and you are the only one who knows.
**Stage II — Latent Period.** A Vector may now be set to trigger after **up to one Metabolic Beat** of your choosing, or on a stated condition ("when it enters the Hegemony's registry hall"). Intrigue becomes mechanical. The Codex's *Bloom of Ash* is built around a Stage II Scout's decision.
**Stage III — Bloom Trigger.** As a 3-PP action, detonate **every Vector you have seeded within Far band** simultaneously. Each trigger fires at −1 Effect Value, and you regain 1 dose per creature affected.
**Stage IV — PANDORA ⟂ (capstone).** Once per Tide, seed a **region**: every organism in it acquires a latent Vector for one Tide. You may trigger all of them at once, at full Effect Value, in a scene you choose. After, mark 4 Lysis Countdown boxes (your body pays for the scale) and every faction with any interest in that region will know your chemical name. This is not a combat ability. It is a campaign ending.

**Kit:** Needle Scatter, Barbed Launcher, Pheromone Mask, Scent Dampener graft.
**How to play:** you win fights before they are fights. Track your doses on the sheet — a Scout without doses is just a scout. Say what you are seeding and where; the Director should be able to *find* your Vectors later, because your own memory of them is the plot.
**Links:** Genetic Hijacker cancels your mistakes; Antigen Detector cancels *you*. Bestiary **Pathogenic Wraiths** use the same vocabulary at a larger scale, and they regard Scouts as either kin or competitors — the Director chooses per Vector you seed.

### VIRAL MIMIC
*Infiltrator / identity · Hull 3 · Key: DFF, OSM · Bonded: Somatic*

**Granted Pathways:** Concealment 2 · Sequence Reading 1 · Symbiosis 1.

**Signature Mechanic — CAMOUFLAGE.** As a 2-PP action, contest **DFF + Concealment** against a subject (or the local antigen register, AT 14 + tier). Success: you wear a creature's chemistry.

* You gain **one Gift** of that lineage and **one Harvest trait** of that creature.
* Antigen scans, Immune Sentinels, and Warden wards read you as **self** of whatever you are wearing.
* While camouflaged, **your own lineage Gift is unavailable**, and your VSC counts as 1 lower for MI purposes (you are wearing a smaller body).
* Maintaining costs 1 PP per Cycle (it is not Sustained in the Core sense: no Stress, no save on damage). It breaks if you Strike a creature you are bonded to, or if a Sequence Reading contest beats you.

**Stage I — Worn Face.** You can camouflage an **object** or a corpse instead: a body you wear gives you its Harvest traits (one, at −1 tier) and lets a companion believe your dead friend is still walking. This is legal in exactly one region.
**Stage II — Borrowed Function.** While camouflaged you may use **one printed Special action** of the creature you are wearing, at your own Tier's Effect Value, once per scene. The first time you do this with a creature type, mark 1 Stress: your body protests.
**Stage III — Hive Mask.** Extend camouflage to `Stage` willing creatures in Contact, at 1 charge each per Cycle. A Cohort wearing an Immunan patrol's chemistry can walk a Warden checkpoint. The checkpoint has a register, and registers notice *gaps*.
**Stage IV — Unbound ⟂ (capstone).** Permanently bind one camouflaged identity: you keep its Gift, one Harvest trait, and its size class **without upkeep**, and you may now wear a **specific individual** you have studied for a Fugue Interval — including their voice-chemistry, their Oath standing, and their enemies. When you are Unbound as someone else, a Total Lysis returns you to your own body (one scar, rolled) — which makes you the only organism who can survive a death by not being yourself.

**Kit:** Needle Dagger, Signal Vane, Graft Antidote Vials (you will need them).
**How to play:** you are the Cohort's second front. Never take the camouflage that gets you *in*; take the one that gets you **out**. Note your VSC penalty: you are the glass cannon of the glass-cannon lineage.
**Links:** the Replicator Agent can supply you with bodies to wear (a legal question the Hegemony has banned). Antigen Detector and Warden Circles are your natural enemies; the Sequence Underground is your natural employer.

### REPLICATOR AGENT
*Summoner / continuity · Hull 4 · Key: RTC, VSC · Bonded: Somatic*

**Granted Pathways:** Catalysis 1 · Vigour 2 · Sequence Reading 1.

**Signature Mechanic — BUDDING.** As a 3-PP action, split: a **Bud** appears adjacent.

```
BUD: MI = half your max (rounded down)   MT = your MT - 1   soak = your soak - 1
Baselines = yours - 2 (min 1)   PP = 2 + your DFF - 1   Flow = your Flow - 1
Frequency: acts after everyone, last in Cycle
Carries: ONE of your Stage features (your choice, and it must be one you could pick at Stage I-II unless you are Stage III+)
Cannot: use your Signature Mechanic, hold a Reserve, or Bond
Upkeep: at the start of your turn, 1 ATP per Bud, or the Bud dies
```

Living Buds: max **Stage** (1 / 2 / 3 / 4). A Bud's death marks **2 Stress** on you and you lose 3 ATP. Buds are *you*, minimally: they feel everything, they remember, and at a Reconstitution you may merge (healing yourself for the Bud's remaining MI).

**Stage I — Shared Sense.** Free action, once per Cycle: perceive through any Bud. Buds can see each other and you, at any distance in the same region.
**Stage II — Strand Transfer.** As a 1-PP action, move one **condition**, one **Insert**, one **Mark**, or **2 Stress** from yourself to a Bud, or from an ally in Contact to a Bud. You are a lightning rod for everyone's bad day.
**Stage III — Differentiation.** Each Bud takes a *different* Stage feature and gains **+1 PP**. Additionally, while at least two Buds live, you gain **+1 PP** (you are thinking in more than one place). You may now Budd at 2 PP.
**Stage IV — Colony of One ⟂ (capstone).** When you would suffer **Total Lysis**, instead transfer to a living Bud (free, before anything else happens): you survive at that Bud's MI, but you lose **one Level of Codon**, roll twice on the Legacy Scarring table and keep one, and every Sequence-reading authority in the region learns that you cannot be killed this way. Once per Tide. The Hegemony has a standing bounty on Stage IV Replicators; the Codex explains why.

**Kit:** Peptide Blade, Full Testate Carapace when affordable, ATP Capacitor Cells.
**How to play:** you are tempo. Buds win by *occupying bands* and forcing enemies to spend actions; they are not damage. Feed yourself: ATP is your real resource, and a Replicator at 0 ATP has just murdered several people who were also them.
**Links:** Viral Mimic can wear a Bud (a trick that has started wars). The Genetic Hijacker can Insert on your Bud — which is not as bad as it sounds, because Strand Transfer means a Bud can hold your problem. Both Phagocyte Guard and Replicator benefit from being adjacent, and a Guard that Engulfs *your* Bud is the strongest defence combo in this book.

## THE IMMUNAN TRADITION — *recognise, protect, answer precisely, coordinate*

Immunan Phenotypes are the lineage that turned **selfhood into a bureaucracy**. Their features are strong, legible, and terrible in organised numbers — and their Frailty (*Literal*) means they are the easiest lineage in the Expanse to ambush socially.

### ANTIGEN DETECTOR
*Support / counter-infiltration · Hull 4 · Key: OSM, AFF · Bonded: Galvanic*

**Granted Pathways:** Chemoreception 2 · Signal Discourse 1 · Pulse Discipline 1.

**Signature Mechanic — ASSAY.** As a 1-PP action (or free once per Cycle when you have not acted), assay a creature within Far band: contest **OSM + Chemoreception** against its Concealment or its natural stealth. Success marks it, and you name its **epitope** — one damage type. While marked:

* attacks of that type against it gain **+2 Reagents**;
* its soak against that type is reduced by **2 + ⌊Stage ÷ 2⌋**;
* every ally may spend 1 Cohesion to know what you know (the mark is *shareable*, which is the whole Immunan point);
* you always know its Order, tier, current conditions, and whether it is **hidden, camouflaged, mimicking, or Hijacked**.

Marks: max **OSM + Stage**. A mark lasts until the creature leaves Far band, or `4 + Stage` Cycles.

**Stage I — Antigen Sight.** Camouflage, False Scent, Masked, and inserted sequences are visible to you as *wrongness*: you may attempt the assay contest as a free action, and revealing a disguise is a Mark for free. Immunan patrols hire Detectives for this and never thank them.
**Stage II — Registry of Enemies ⟂.** Once per **creature type** (per Cohort, permanent): you know everything printed on its Bestiary block — traits, tactics, harvest — and the Cohort's Harvest checks against it gain +2 dice. Saying it aloud is a free action; the information is diegetic, not meta.
**Stage III — Preemptive Recognition.** When a creature Begins a Burst, Charges, or prepares a Strike, you may assay it **immediately, before it resolves**. If its action then fails for any reason, your Cohort gains **+1 PP this Cycle**. Detectives are the reason ambushes stop working.
**Stage IV — Pan-Registry ⟂ (capstone).** As a 4-PP action: every hostile in a zone (`4 + Stage` Drifts) is Marked, every hidden thing in the zone is revealed, and for 3 Cycles all allied attacks against Marked targets gain +2 Reagents and ignore 1 soak. The zone stays *readable* for the rest of the scene. After, you must Vent (1 PP) or mark 2 Stress: knowing everything is heavy.

**Kit:** Needle Scatter or Spore Pistol, Secondary Lens graft, Memory Vial.
**How to play:** you are the reason damage-dealers hit. Pick the epitope your Cohort actually deals — ask them. Sit at Mid band, not the front: your value is your eyes, and eyes can be blinded by turbidity and Gel (buy the Lens).
**Links:** every damage-dealing Phenotype in this book wants your Mark; **Antibody Archer** is the intended partner (Locks want known epitopes). Your natural enemy set is the Lysosan tradition — Viral Mimic, Genetic Hijacker, Pathogen Scout — and the Bestiary's **Anomalies** cannot be assayed at all, which should frighten you.

### PHAGOCYTE GUARD
*Defender / absorber · Hull 6 · Key: VSC, AFF · Bonded: Galvanic*

**Granted Pathways:** Pressure Tolerance 2 · Membrane Penetration 1 · Vigour 1.

**Signature Mechanic — ENGULF.** When an attack or area effect would hit an ally within **Contact** (Near at Stage II+), spend your Reserve: **you take it instead**, with **+4 soak** applied. Then, if the attacker is your size class or smaller and you took 6+ damage from it, you may attempt **Engulfment**: contested **VSC + Pressure Tolerance** vs its VSC. Success:

* it is **Sequestered inside you**: it cannot act, cannot be targeted, and takes `4 + (Stage × 2)` Lytic damage at the start of each of your turns;
* you heal **half the damage you absorbed**, and gain +1 soak while anything is inside;
* capacity: up to **⌈Stage ÷ 2⌉ + 1** creatures inside you at once, total size class ≤ yours + 1;
* you may **release** one as a free action (thrown 1 band, Jostled), or it breaks out with a VSC contest at AT 14 + your Overflow.

While holding a creature, you may still act — but describe it as *carrying the problem*.

**Stage I — Bulwark Reflex.** Allies in Contact gain **+2 MT**. As a 1-PP action, give an ally in Contact **+3 soak** until your next turn.
**Stage II — Extended Self.** Engulf reaches Near band. You cannot be Flank-Breached, and you may position yourself **between** two allies: both count as Contact for your features.
**Stage III — Digestion.** Lytic damage from Engulfment doubles, and you gain **+1 temporary MI** per point of damage the engulfed creature takes (max `Stage × 4`, gone at Reconstitution). You may also expel an engulfed creature *through* a Strike: release it at another target as an attack at BD = Stage.
**Stage IV — Antigen Bulwark ⟂ (capstone).** Once per Pulse War, for **3 Cycles**: all damage dealt to any ally within your bands is reduced by your full soak; you gain **+2 PP**; you cannot be pushed or moved; and each time you reduce damage this way, that ally gains +1 ATP. You may not Drift on those Cycles. Guards call this *standing where the wall was*.

**Kit:** Pressure Ram or Bony Flail, Testate or Somatic graft (you want soak), Pore Locks, Coagulant Packs.
**How to play:** your MI is the largest in the book; your MT is deliberately mediocre, because you are *supposed* to be hit. Engulf is not only defence — a Sequestered enemy is removed from the fight's action economy, which is worth more than damage at Stage III+.
**Links:** your Engulfment is the *only* reliable way to remove a **Pathogenic Wraith** or **Viral Mimic** from a fight without killing it (the Bestiary's phase traits respect Sequester only partly — read the block). Replicator Agents feed you Buds to protect. Enzyme-heavy allies (Corrosor, Splicer) speed your digestion; Lytic allies also hurt you, since you are full of someone.

### ANTIBODY ARCHER
*Ranged striker / precision · Hull 4 · Key: DFF, RTC · Bonded: Galvanic*

**Granted Pathways:** Membrane Penetration 2 (Projector) · Signal Discourse 1 · Chemoreception 1.

**Signature Mechanic — LOCK.** A successful Strike against a creature marks **1 Lock** on it (max 3 per creature). Each Lock:

* **+1 Reagent** and **+2 BD** against that creature;
* Lock 3: your next Strike against it is a **Perfect Match** — its **Rider** triggers regardless of Runaway Catalysis, its soak is treated as 0 for that hit, and it gains **Locked** (it may Shed with an OSM save at AT 12 + your Overflow).

Locks decay by 1 at the start of your turn if you have not hit that creature since. Locks are **public information**: an Antigen Detector can see them, and a Cascade Commander can spend them.

**Stage I — Known Epitope.** Against a creature an ally has **Marked**, your attacks gain **+2 Reagents**, and you may spend 1 Cohesion to *reload certainty*: re-roll one Inhibitor on the attack.
**Stage II — Avidity.** When you reach Lock 3 on a target, you may make one extra Strike this Cycle against a **different** creature in range at −1 Reagent. Cross-reactivity is a real immune phenomenon; here it is free damage.
**Stage III — Cross-Reaction.** When a Strike hits with **Overflow 4+**, your next Strike this Cycle against a *different* creature carrying at least one Lock gains **+2 Reagents and +1 Overflow** — the response spreads.
**Stage IV — Classical Response ⟂ (capstone).** Once per Pulse War, as a 4-PP action: choose one creature in Far band. Make **three Strikes** at your full Lock bonus; each hit applies **Lock 3 instantly** and, on the third, the target suffers a **Perfect Match** with the type you choose. Immune lore calls this *the answer that arrives all at once*.

**Kit:** Needle Scatter (200 S) or Barbed Launcher, Secondary Lens, Antibody Barb Vials.
**How to play:** you are the highest single-target damage in the book, and you are useless at Contact. Buy range (Lens, Signal Vane, Deep Ear), buy dice, and *never* chase: bring the enemy to your bands with the Navigator, the Rider, or the Brute's push. If a Detector Marks and you Lock, the fight's math collapses in two Cycles.
**Links:** Antigen Detector is the single best pair in the game. Your Locks are also *fuel* for the Cascade Commander's chain reactions. Your weakness: anything with soak-scaling (Osmotic Brutes, Barophil Tanks) wants you to waste Cycles building Locks — spend your first Cycle on positioning instead.

### COMPLEMENT CASCADE COMMANDER
*Leader / chain reactions · Hull 4 · Key: AFF, DFF · Bonded: Galvanic*

**Granted Pathways:** Symbiosis 2 · Signal Discourse 2 · Pulse Discipline 1.

**Signature Mechanic — CASCADE.** When an ally hits a creature **you have marked or Lock-flagged**, you may spend **1 PP** to trigger a Cascade: **one other ally** within Far band makes one free Strike against any creature that is marked or Lock-flagged, at **+1 Overflow**. Restrictions: one Cascade per ally per Cycle, and at most **AFF + Stage** Cascades per Pulse War (track them on the sheet — they are your ammunition).

Cascades do not themselves trigger Cascades (nothing in the Expanse is that naive), but a Cascade **counts as an ally hit** for the purposes of Antibody Archer Locks, Antigen Detector marks, and your own markers.

**Stage I — Rally Field.** Allies within Mid band gain **+1 Reagent** on their first Strike each Cycle. You know, as a free action, how many PP each ally has left — say so constantly.
**Stage II — Marker Spread.** When you mark a creature, you may (free) pass the mark to **one adjacent creature** as well, at no cost. With enough Cascades you can turn a whole fight into a chain: a single Cycle with 4 Cascades is called a *classical response* and is remembered for Beats.
**Stage III — Classical Pathway.** As a 3-PP action, once per Pulse War: every creature you have marked takes `4 + (Stage × 2)` Voltaic damage, every ally in Mid band heals `2 × number of marks` MI, and all conditions of the **Marked** type are removed from allies. Your OSM hums; the region hears it.
**Stage IV — Membrane Attack Complex ⟂ (capstone).** As a 5-PP action, once per Pulse War: choose up to **three creatures within Mid band of each other**. A protein-complex lattice closes over them: they are **Sequestered** (Core IV) at AT `16 + your Overflow`, and for as long as the lattice holds (your choice, up to 3 Cycles) **allied attacks against them gain +2 Reagents and cannot be defended by Reactive Osmosis**. When any Sequestered creature inside dies, every ally gains **+2 ATP and +2 MI**. MACs are the reason the Hegemony's enemies organise in groups.

**Kit:** Phase Bell (180 S) or Needle Scatter, Signal Vane graft, Chain Cascade Vials.
**How to play:** you are a force multiplier with a PP budget. Your best move is almost never your own Strike: it is buying a *mark* for someone else's big hit and then spending Cascades on whoever still has PP. Keep minions (Symbiosis 2, AFF-led): the Bestiary's colonists and your own Symbiotes count as allies for Cascades.
**Links:** the entire Immunan tradition is a single machine: Detector marks, Archer Locks, Guard protects, Commander chains. A Cohort of all four is legal, boring to enemies, and the reason the Warden Circles are feared. Against you: Pathogenic Wraiths steal your Reserve, and Anomalies ignore marks entirely.

## THE BAROPHIL TRADITION — *endure, convert, outlast, make the place uninhabitable*

Barophil Phenotypes are the only lineage that fights the **environment** as an ally by default. Their features are slow, enormous, and often ruinous to the terrain — including the terrain your Cohort is standing in. Every Barophil feature should be discussed aloud before it is used.

### ACID-RESISTANT TANK
*Defender / reflector · Hull 6 · Key: VSC, RTC · Bonded: Lytic*

**Granted Pathways:** Pressure Tolerance 2 · Vigour 1 · Synthesis 1.

**Signature Mechanic — VAT.** You keep a internal reservoir of **Acid charges**, max **RTC + Stage**. Gain 1 charge when:

* you take **Lytic** or **Thermal** damage (the insult becomes inventory);
* you spend a Synthesise action drinking 2 S of raw acid, bile, or waste (colonies pay you to drink their spoilage);
* you win a Pressure Tolerance save against a hazard.

| Spend | Effect |
| --: | :-- |
| 1 | your next Strike: **+3 Lytic**, and the target's graft loses **1 soak until repaired** (Corrodes) |
| 2 | **Bile Splash:** a 2-Drift zone, `4 + (Stage × 2)` Lytic damage to hostiles (allies halve it); the ground stays hot for 1 Cycle |
| 2 | **Neutralise:** end one Lytic/Thermal effect, dispel one hazard zone, or cure Brittle/Denatured/Cauterised on any creature in Contact |
| 3 | **Bile Flood:** a zone becomes **Acidic** for 3 Cycles (Core VII). You are immune; allies need a Neutraliser or 3 Lytic soak |

**Stage I — Acid-Hardened.** Lytic damage against you is reduced by **2 + Stage**. Anything that deals Lytic damage to you in Contact takes **Stage** Lytic back (it touched the wrong bag of chemistry).
**Stage II — Swallow.** As a Reactive Osmosis, absorb a ranged Lytic or Thermal effect targeting you or an ally in Contact: **0 damage, +1 Acid charge.** Tanks are the only beings who treat an acid attack as a *reloading* action.
**Stage III — Shared Bath.** While you hold **3+ charges**: +2 soak for you, +1 soak vs Lytic for allies in Contact, and as a Synthesise action you may **coat** an ally's Apparatus: it deals Lytic damage and **+2 BD** for the scene (and needs Neutralising before it eats their hands).
**Stage IV — The Long Burn ⟂ (capstone).** Once per Pulse War, for **3 Cycles**: a zone of radius `Stage × 2` Drifts becomes Acidic for hostile organisms only. You gain **+2 PP** and regenerate **3 MI** per Cycle there; every hostile action there costs +1 ATP. Afterwards the ground stays hostile for a Beat, which is a *colony problem* you now own.

**Kit:** Osmotic Maul or Chitin Flail, Mucoid Mantle (your best friend), Neutraliser Vials for the Cohort.
**How to play:** you are a damage *conversion* engine: the enemy's acid becomes your acid. Position at Contact permanently — you should be the reason an enemy's Lytic order stops being used against you (they will, and that is your whole defence). Watch your Cohort's grafts: your Bile Flood is the single most common cause of friendly casualties in this book.
**Links:** Enzymatic Splicer can move your Brittle onto someone else; Phagocyte Guard and you are the two-tile wall the Warden Circles build checkpoints around. Bestiary **Lysosomal Wastes** creatures are your natural quarry and your food source.

### THERMAL DIVER
*Zone controller / bruiser · Hull 4 · Key: RTC, VSC · Bonded: Thermic*

**Granted Pathways:** Catalysis 2 · Vigour 1 · Pressure Tolerance 1.

**Signature Mechanic — GRADIENT.** You carry a **Gradient** from **−5 (deep-cold)** to **+5 (white-hot)**, starting at 0. At the start of your turn you may shift it **1 for free, 2 for 1 PP**; taking Thermal damage shifts it toward hot by the damage ÷ 3 (rounded); entering a cold region shifts it toward cold by 1.

| Gradient | You |
| :-- | :-- |
| **+1 … +2** | your Strikes gain **+1 BD per point**; your Thermal effects gain +1 Effect Value |
| **+3 … +4** | as above, and your Strikes **ignite**: the target's band becomes a Thermal Hazard for 1 Cycle (2 + Stage damage); cold effects against you fail |
| **+5** | **+5 BD**, everything you hit ignites — but you mark **2 Stress** at the start of each Cycle at +5 |
| **−1 … −2** | gain **+1 soak per point**; your Thermal effects become cold: they inflict Jostled instead of igniting |
| **−3 … −4** | as above, and the fluid in Contact with you thickens: viscosity +1 in your band; heat effects against you fail |
| **−5** | **+5 soak**, everything in Contact is Jostled — but mark **2 Stress** per Cycle and your Flow drops to 2 |

**Stage I — Thermal Indifference.** Choose hot or cold regions: they are Isotonic-equivalent for you (this is in addition to your Barophil *Hardened*). Thermal damage against you is reduced by **Stage**.
**Stage II — Flash Point.** As a Reactive Osmosis, convert incoming Thermal damage entirely into a Gradient shift (toward hot, by damage ÷ 2) and push the attacker **1 band**. You may do this against your own ally's Burst if you are willing.
**Stage III — Steam Vent.** As a 3-PP action, once per Pulse War: a zone of radius `3 + Stage` Drifts flashes: `6 + |Gradient| × 2` Thermal damage to hostiles, everything in the zone is pushed **1 band**, and the zone becomes **Turbid** for 2 Cycles. Cold-Gradient version: the zone becomes viscous for 3 Cycles instead.
**Stage IV — Critical Point ⟂ (capstone).** Once per Pulse War: set Gradient to **+5 or −5 immediately**, and for **2 Cycles** you suffer none of its Stress or Flow cost and your Effect Values gain +4. When it ends, Gradient returns to 0, you take **10 damage**, and mark 3 Stress. Divers call it *the dive you only make once*.

**Kit:** Cryo Vent (300 S, save for it), Enzyme Horn, Heat Sink graft.
**How to play:** you are the terrain's thermostat. Set your Gradient *before* the fight (a Diver at +3 walking in is a different encounter). Keep one Reactive for Flash Point — the best Diver play is absorbing the enemy's Thermal attack and spending it back as push.
**Links:** your Turbid/viscous zones help the Antibody Archer and hurt the Current Rider: coordinate. The Bestiary's **Thermal Vent Fields** and Nucleoid Approaches are your home turf, and Barophil *Hardened* + this feature means you can fight catastrophes in their own exhaust.

### OSMOTIC BRUTE
*Bruiser / positioning destroyer · Hull 6 · Key: VSC, DFF · Bonded: Osmotic*

**Granted Pathways:** Membrane Penetration 2 · Pressure Tolerance 2 · Fluid Navigation 1.

**Signature Mechanic — TURGOR.** You build **Turgor** charges, max **Stage + 3**. Gain 1 when: you take Osmotic or Kinetic damage; you successfully push or are pushed; you spend 2 ATP to pump fluid; or you win a Pressure Tolerance save.

| Spend | Effect |
| --: | :-- |
| 1 | **on a Strike:** +2 BD and push the target **1 Drift per point spent** (bands as usual at 4+ Overflow); or spend 1 to cancel any push on yourself |
| 2 | the target becomes **Turgid** (+5 temp MI, −2 DFF) or **Flaccid** (−2 VSC, reach −1) — your choice, both end after 2 Cycles |
| 3 | **Hydrostatic Ram:** damage `6 + (Stage × 2) + Overflow`, ignore 3 soak, throw the target to the **next band** and inflict Jostled. If it hits a wall, +6 Kinetic |

**Stage I — Immovable.** You cannot be pushed by anything with VSC less than yours. +2 soak vs Osmotic, and Momentum attacks against you lose their bonus dice.
**Stage II — Pore Grapple.** When a Strike hits, you may spend 1 Turgor to **Anchor** the target: it cannot Drift until it wins a VSC contest at AT 12 + your Overflow. You can hold one Anchor at a time (two at Stage III).
**Stage III — Pressure Wave.** Your Strike can be declared along a **line** (up to `Stage × 2` Drifts): every creature in the line is attacked at −1 Reagent each, and all are pushed 1 band. Brutes clear corridors.
**Stage IV — Implosion ⟂ (capstone).** Once per Pulse War: a target in Contact. Spend **all** Turgor: it takes **3 damage per point** (Osmotic, ignores all soak) and everything else within `3 + Stage` Drifts is thrown **2 bands** for 4 Osmotic damage each. Then you are **Flaccid** for 2 Cycles (−2 VSC, reach −1). A Brute that uses Implosion on a swarm usually ends the swarm and starts a funeral.

**Kit:** Pressure Ram (60 S — this is your class weapon), Full Testate Carapace later, Pore Locks.
**How to play:** you do not damage enemies, you **move them into other problems**: walls, hazards, Gel, Acidic zones, your Corrosor's Etch range. Push is a resource: say where you want someone to land. Keep Turgor at 3+ before engaging — an empty Brute is a slow target with mediocre MT.
**Links:** Turbulence Navigator and you are a two-person siege train. Your Turgor economy is fed by the enemy's Osmotic attacks, so Pathogenic Wraiths and Osmotic-order Bestiary creatures hand you your capstone. Against Testate-armoured foes your push works even when damage does not — armour does not make you lighter.

### DESICCATION SPECIALIST
*Attrition / anti-environment · Hull 4 · Key: OSM, RTC · Bonded: Thermic*

**Granted Pathways:** Chemoreception 2 · Synthesis 1 · Vigour 2.

**Signature Mechanic — DRY ZONE.** As a 2-PP action, make a zone of radius **2 + Stage** Drifts **Dry** for `1 + ⌈Stage ÷ 2⌉` Cycles. Inside:

* hostiles suffer the **Hypertonic** rules (DFF −2, PP −1, heals halved) and lose **1 ATP** at their turn's start;
* **no healing of any kind** works on hostiles there, and no Burst may be *Started* at tier above I;
* each hostile that starts its turn inside gives you **2 Substrate** (you are collecting the world's water);
* you are unaffected, and your Cohort may pay 1 ATP each to be unaffected.

Store the drawn water and minerals as **Brine**, max **Stage + 2**:

| Spend | Effect |
| --: | :-- |
| 1 | **+2 Reagents** to any one roll, or cancel one of your own Inhibitors |
| 1 | end one condition on any creature in range (water washes chemistry away) |
| 1 | create one Vial from the Core catalogue (once per Cycle max) |
| 2 | **Salt Lock:** a target's graft soak −2 for the scene, and it cannot gain temporary MI |

**Stage I — Dry Skin.** Hypertonic and Acidic regions are neutral for you (on top of *Hardened*). +1 soak vs Lytic, and Leaking effects on you close at your turn's end.
**Stage II — Withering Touch.** In a Dry Zone your Strikes drain: **+2 BD**, and each hit gives you **1 Brine**. Against swarms, each Segment removed also gives 1 Brine.
**Stage III — Dust Field.** Your Dry Zone also **suppresses regeneration and Segment regrowth**, prevents new hazards or spores from forming inside it, and makes any hostile **Sustained Reaction** cost +1 ATP per Cycle. Specialists are hired by colonies to *unmake* things.
**Stage IV — Salt the Earth ⟂ (capstone).** Once per Tide, choose a region (up to 100 Drifts across). It becomes permanently **Dry** until you release it: no regeneration, no growth, no healing for hostile organisms; travel costs +1 PP for everyone else; nothing there will ever bloom again. **This is a world-altering act.** The Deep Choir will consider you kin or an abomination, the Flow Guilds will put a price on you, and any colony downstream will send an army. Use it in play only with the table's explicit consent.

**Kit:** Suture Cannon (as a weird heal-delivery tool), Barbed Launcher, Scent Dampener, Vacuole Sac.
**How to play:** you are the counter to *numbers*. Swarms, Blooms, regenerating horrors, Sustained-Reaction catalysts, and hypertonic-region natives all stop working around you. The cost is that you are weak in a fast, short fight: you win the long one.
**Links:** Phagocyte Guard in your Dry Zone cannot heal from Engulfment — say so before you both sign up for the same fight. Bestiary **Nutrient Drifters** and Bloom events are your natural prey, and **Anomalies** that regenerate are the reason this Phenotype exists at all.

# 4 · ADVANCEMENT

## 4.1 What a Growth gives you

You **Grow** when your Codon total crosses the next threshold (Core II §2.3), and Growth happens at the next **Reconstitution**. At each Growth:

| You always get | Then choose one **Synthesis Choice** |
| :-- | :-- |
| +2 max MI (from the Level term in the MI formula), +1 Bulk capacity, and Phenotype features for your new Level if any | see below |

**Synthesis Choices** (buy with the choice; some also cost Substrate/Specimens per Core §9.9):

1. **+1 Baseline Grade** (max 6; one per Growth)
2. **+1 Pathway rank** (max 4)
3. **Learn a Burst** from the Core catalogue or your Phenotype's list
4. **Take an Adaptation** (§4.2)
5. **Harvest trait** from a Specimen you hold (graft; takes a Slot)
6. **Craft or buy gear** at a discount (your lab, your contacts, your foundry)

## 4.2 Adaptations

**Chimeric Growth is the multiclass rule.** It grants one Stage I feature, not the class's Hull, and it cannot be taken twice for the same Phenotype. At Stage IV you may take a second Chimeric Growth for the same Phenotype to gain its **Stage II** feature. This is deliberately expensive and weird — the Expanse rewards hybrid organisms, and every faction has a law about them.

## 4.3 Stage transitions

At Levels **6, 11, 16** you reach a new Stage, which is a *body event*, not a level-up:

* your **Phenotype's Stage feature** unlocks;
* **+6 max MI**, and **+1 Graft Slot**;
* one **free Synthesis Choice** (not competing with your normal one);
* describe the change and choose one **visible consequence** the Director may use: a new scent, a changed size class, a readable Oath mark, a limp that is actually a weapon. Stage transitions are when other organisms start treating you differently — say what changed, and say who notices.

At Level 20 you are Stage IV, and the Core's **Apotheosis** track (Ch. XII) becomes available.

## 5.5 Ten questions the table asks

1. **"Do 1s still count as Inhibitors on a Burst?"** Yes. Always. Bursts poison you too.
2. **"Can I spend Overflow on movement *and* damage?"** No. Overflow applies to the one attempt you made. Spend it where the attempt was.
3. **"Is my Homeostatic Baseline usable in combat?"** No. It requires unhurried and unopposed. Someone with a maul is neither.
4. **"Do I get PP back if I Split Frequency?"** You keep unspent PP and re-enter later. It is gone at Cycle end either way.
5. **"Can Reactive Osmosis defend an ally?"** Only if a feature says so (Phagocyte Guard, Bonds, Twin Reserve with a Bond relay).
6. **"Does Soak apply to Overflow and BD separately?"** No. Once, to the total, after Overflow is counted. Minimum 1 damage on a hit.
7. **"Can I heal a Dormant ally?"** Only by **stabilisation** (RTC + Synthesis at AT 12 + their Level). Substrate Patch does not work on Dormant targets; Resynthesis Call does.
8. **"Do I keep gear when I Total-Lyse?"** The dead carries it. Carry them, or it stays with the body — and bodies are harvestable.
9. **"Can a Parasitic Graft be removed for free?"** No. Graft Antidote + Synthesis contest AT 16+, or a Nucleoid ritual. Some of them have opinions about being asked.
10. **"What if I have no idea what to do in a Pulse War?"** Do one of these, in order: Vent, Drift to a better band, Sense, spend Cohesion to pass a die to someone else, then Strike. Never "wait". *Waiting in a fluid is how you get dissolved.*
