# Story seeds: your own stories for the AI Director

A **story seed** is a short piece of your own story material (people, places, threads, one encounter, secrets) written in one fixed layout. The AI Director reads that layout without guessing, keeps your people consistent, keeps your secrets until the players earn them, and brings your encounter at the right moment. Seeds are used while **ad-lib** is on.

## The quickest way: let the Director write it

1. On the host console, open the **Seeds** tab.
2. Type your idea in a few sentences, or paste a whole story you have written.
3. Press **Write it up**. The Director turns it into a seed in the layout below, checked against the Lytic Bestiary and the Vector being played. If its first try does not pass the check, it gets the problems back and tries once more.
4. Read it, change anything you like, and press **Save this seed**. The app checks it again as it saves.
5. Make sure **ad-lib** is on (the Seeds tab has a button for it, or use the Memory tab). Switch any seed on or off at any time. Players never see seeds.

With the AI off, press **Start from the template** and write it yourself.

## The layout

```
# Seed: Vey's Crew
Fits: The First Carve, any site
Tone: a rival who is almost friendly

## People
- Captain Ossa Vey (person): runs a rival salvage skiff. She wants the Gut Stone before the Cohort. She fears the Guild's claim-runners.
- Tib (person): Vey's nephew, sick of the job. He will trade a secret for a way out.

## Places
- Vey's skiff (place): moored at the wound lip, salt barrels on deck.

## Threads
- The unpaid rite (thread): Vey owes the Ash a corpse-rite and has not paid it.

## Encounter
- 2 drifter-shoal at Mid: Vey's crew baited them to slow the Cohort.

## Secrets
- Vey already has half the serial.

## How it can end
- Vey takes a deal.
- Vey is arrested at the Warden arch.
- Vey sinks with the whale.
```

| Line | What it means |
| :-- | :-- |
| `# Seed:` | The title, on the first line. |
| `Fits:` | The Vector it is for, then `any site` or one site's name. A seed for another Vector waits until that Vector is played; a seed for one site comes only there. |
| `Tone:` | A few words for the feel. |
| `## People`, `## Places`, `## Threads` | One line each: `Name (kind): facts.`, where the kind is person, place, thread or faction. Two to four short facts. Wants, fears and secrets are what make a person playable. |
| `## Encounter` | Optional, and only one: `<1 to 3> <creature id or name> at <Contact, Near, Mid or Far>: why they are here`. It counts as that site's one invented encounter. |
| `## Secrets` | True things the players find out only by playing. The Director never says them outright. |
| `## How it can end` | Two or three different ways it could go, so the Director can steer without forcing. |

## The rules the check holds you to

- A title, and at least one person, place or thread.
- The encounter uses a real creature from the Lytic Bestiary (by id or name), one to three bodies, and nothing tougher than the toughest creature the Vector prints (the lists below).
- **No prices, rewards or damage.** Substrate, Codon, Specimens and damage come only from the printed books. A line that names one is saved with a note, and the Director treats it as talk, not a payment. A person may still want or offer something in words.

## Writing a seed in another chat app

Paste the prompt below into any chat app (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini), then add the Vector's line and creature list from further down, then your idea or story. Paste what it gives you into the Seeds tab and press **Save this seed**: the check runs there.

```
You turn a game host's story idea into a STORY SEED for The Osmotic Expanse, a tabletop roleplaying game played inside a microscopic world of cells, membranes and currents. An AI Director will use the seed at the table, so it must follow this layout exactly and use plain words.

THE LAYOUT (nothing before or after it):

# Seed: <a short title>
Fits: <the Vector's title>, <"any site" or one site's name>
Tone: <a few words>

## People
- <Name> (person): <who they are. What they want. What they fear or hide.>

## Places
- <Name> (place): <what it is like, and why it matters.>

## Threads
- <Name> (thread): <a debt, a grudge, a promise, a rumour or a claim that ties things together.>

## Encounter
- <1 to 3> <creature id> at <Contact, Near, Mid or Far>: <why they are here, in the fiction.>

## Secrets
- <something true the players can only find out by playing.>

## How it can end
- <two or three different ways it could go.>

THE RULES:
1. One line per person, place or thread: "Name (person|place|thread|faction): facts." Two to four short facts each, in full sentences.
2. Two to five people, up to three places and up to three threads. Keep what the host gave you; add only what makes it playable.
3. The Encounter is optional and there is at most one. It uses a creature id from the list you are given, one to three bodies, and never a creature tougher than the list allows. Leave the section out if the story has no fight.
4. Never write a number of Substrate (S), Codon or Specimens, a reward, a price or an amount of damage. Payment comes only from the printed books. A person may want or offer something in words ("a share of the haul", "a way out").
5. Fit the Vector: its tone, its places and its factions. A seed sharpens the printed situation; it never replaces it.
6. Write it so the AI Director can play it straight away: wants, fears and secrets are what make a person playable.
```

Then add, for example:

```
THE VECTOR: The First Carve.
CREATURES YOU MAY USE: (copy the list for your Vector from below)
THE HOST'S IDEA OR STORY:
(your idea or story)
Write the seed now, in the layout, and nothing else.
```
